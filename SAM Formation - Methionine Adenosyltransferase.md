# SAM Formation - Methionine Adenosyltransferase

**Part of:** [[Folate One-Carbon Metabolism]]

## Transformation

Methionine + ATP + H2O → S-adenosylmethionine + phosphate products

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | MAT1A or MAT2A with relevant regulatory partners |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Forms the activated methyl donor used by many methyltransferases |

## What controls this step

SAM participates in methylation and other reactions; methylation potential depends also on SAH accumulation and clearance, not simply SAM concentration.
