# Hydroxyacyl-CoA Oxidation

**Part of:** [[Lipolysis and Beta Oxidation]]

## Transformation

L-3-Hydroxyacyl-CoA + NAD+ → 3-ketoacyl-CoA + NADH + H+

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | HADH or HADHA dehydrogenase domain |
| Compartment | Mitochondrial matrix / trifunctional protein |
| Physiological direction | Second oxidation of the spiral generates NADH |

## What controls this step

Requires oxidised NAD+. Respiratory-chain limitations can slow fatty-acid oxidation even when fatty acids are available.
