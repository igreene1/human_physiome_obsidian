# ATP Synthase Rotary Catalysis

**Part of:** [[Electron Transport and Oxidative Phosphorylation]]

## Transformation

ADP + Pi → ATP, driven by proton flow intermembrane space → matrix

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | F1Fo ATP synthase; ATP5 family subunits |
| Compartment | Inner mitochondrial membrane, F1 catalytic head in matrix |
| Physiological direction | Couples rotor motion to binding-change catalysis at beta subunits |

## What controls this step

The proton-motive force and ADP/Pi supply determine synthesis. If the gradient collapses, the enzyme can reverse and hydrolyse ATP; inhibitory mechanisms limit this. Whole-system ATP cost includes metabolite transport.
