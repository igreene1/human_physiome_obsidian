# Voltage-Gated Calcium Channels

**Role:** Voltage-gated Ca²⁺ channels that couple depolarisation to transmitter release, contraction and signalling.

## Canonical mechanism

Depolarisation opens channel families such as L-, N-, P/Q-, R- and T-type; Ca²⁺ enters down its electrochemical gradient.

## Physiological consequence

Presynaptic N/PQ channels trigger vesicle fusion; L-type channels are major in cardiac/smooth muscle and endocrine cells.
