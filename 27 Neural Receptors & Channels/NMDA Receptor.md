# NMDA Receptor

**Role:** Ionotropic glutamate receptor permeable to Na⁺, K⁺ and Ca²⁺ and requiring glutamate plus a co-agonist such as glycine/D-serine.

## Canonical mechanism

At resting negative potentials, Mg²⁺ blocks the pore; sufficient depolarisation relieves block, allowing Ca²⁺ entry.

## Physiological consequence

Coincidence-detection properties make NMDA signalling central to many forms of synaptic plasticity.
