# M3 Muscarinic Receptor

**Role:** Gq/11-coupled receptor on many glands and smooth-muscle/endothelial targets.

## Canonical mechanism

ACh → M3 → PLC/IP3/Ca²⁺. In endothelium, Ca²⁺–calmodulin activates eNOS → NO.

## Physiological consequence

Promotes glandular secretion, smooth-muscle contraction in many organs, and endothelial NO-mediated vasodilation.
