# AMPA Receptor

**Role:** Ionotropic glutamate receptor responsible for much fast excitatory transmission in the CNS.

## Canonical mechanism

Glutamate binding opens a cation channel, producing rapid depolarisation.

## Physiological consequence

AMPA receptor number/conductance at synapses can change during plasticity.
