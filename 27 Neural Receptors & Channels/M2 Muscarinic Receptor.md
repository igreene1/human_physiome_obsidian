# M2 Muscarinic Receptor

**Role:** Gi/o-coupled muscarinic receptor especially important in the heart.

## Canonical mechanism

ACh → M2 → ↓ adenylyl cyclase/cAMP; Gβγ opens GIRK K⁺ channels → hyperpolarisation.

## Physiological consequence

Slows sinoatrial firing and AV conduction.
