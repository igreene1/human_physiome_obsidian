# Adenosine A2A Receptor

**Role:** Gs/olf-coupled adenosine receptor important in striatum, vasculature and immune contexts.

## Canonical mechanism

Adenosine → A2A → ↑ cAMP/PKA.

## Physiological consequence

Modulates basal-ganglia signalling and can promote vasodilation/anti-inflammatory effects depending on tissue.
