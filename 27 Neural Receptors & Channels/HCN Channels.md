# HCN Channels

**Role:** Hyperpolarisation-activated cyclic-nucleotide-gated cation channels carrying the funny/current Ih/If.

## Canonical mechanism

Hyperpolarisation opens HCN channels; cAMP shifts activation to more positive voltages.

## Physiological consequence

HCN4 contributes strongly to pacemaker depolarisation in the sinoatrial node; HCN channels also shape neuronal rhythmicity.
