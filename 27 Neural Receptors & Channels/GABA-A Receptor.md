# GABA-A Receptor

**Role:** Ligand-gated chloride channel mediating most fast synaptic inhibition in the brain.

## Canonical mechanism

GABA opens Cl⁻-selective channels; the effect depends on the chloride electrochemical gradient but in mature neurons is usually hyperpolarising/shunting.

## Physiological consequence

Rapidly reduces neuronal excitability and shapes oscillations, anxiety and sleep circuits.
