# M1 Muscarinic Receptor

**Role:** Gq/11-coupled muscarinic acetylcholine receptor prominent in CNS and selected peripheral sites.

## Canonical mechanism

ACh → M1 → PLCβ → IP3/DAG → intracellular Ca²⁺/PKC signalling.

## Physiological consequence

Generally excitatory/modulatory in neurons and glandular contexts.
