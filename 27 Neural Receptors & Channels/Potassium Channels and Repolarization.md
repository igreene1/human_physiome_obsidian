# Potassium Channels and Repolarization

**Role:** Diverse K⁺ channels that stabilise membrane potential and repolarise action potentials.

## Canonical mechanism

Opening increases K⁺ conductance, usually driving membrane voltage toward the K⁺ equilibrium potential.

## Physiological consequence

Voltage-gated, inward-rectifier, Ca²⁺-activated and ATP-sensitive channels support different excitable-cell functions.
