# 5-HT1A Receptor

**Role:** Gi/o-coupled serotonin receptor expressed as somatodendritic autoreceptor in raphe neurons and postsynaptically in limbic/cortical regions.

## Canonical mechanism

Serotonin → 5-HT1A → ↓ cAMP and increased K⁺ conductance/reduced excitability in many cells.

## Physiological consequence

Regulates serotonergic firing and anxiety/mood-related circuits.
