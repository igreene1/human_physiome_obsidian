# Orexin OX1 Receptor

**Role:** Orexin GPCR with preference for orexin-A and prominent expression in arousal/reward systems.

## Canonical mechanism

Couples mainly to Gq-family signalling in many contexts, increasing neuronal excitability.

## Physiological consequence

Helps orexin neurons activate wake-promoting and motivational circuits.
