# GABA-B Receptor

**Role:** Metabotropic Gi/o-coupled GABA receptor.

## Canonical mechanism

GABA-B activation reduces presynaptic Ca²⁺ entry, opens postsynaptic K⁺ channels and decreases cAMP.

## Physiological consequence

Produces slower inhibitory effects and limits transmitter release.
