# 5-HT3 Receptor

**Role:** Ionotropic serotonin receptor; a ligand-gated cation channel.

## Canonical mechanism

Serotonin binding rapidly depolarises neurons by opening the channel.

## Physiological consequence

Important in visceral afferents and emetic pathways as well as CNS circuits.
