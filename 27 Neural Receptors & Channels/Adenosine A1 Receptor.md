# Adenosine A1 Receptor

**Role:** Gi/o-coupled adenosine receptor widely expressed in brain and peripheral tissues.

## Canonical mechanism

Adenosine → A1 → ↓ cAMP, reduced Ca²⁺ entry and increased K⁺ conductance in many neurons.

## Physiological consequence

Suppresses neuronal activity and transmitter release; contributes to sleep pressure and cardiac effects.
