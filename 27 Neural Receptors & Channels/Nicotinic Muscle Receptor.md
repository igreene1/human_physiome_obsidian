# Nicotinic Muscle Receptor

**Role:** Ligand-gated cation channel concentrated at the skeletal neuromuscular junction.

## Canonical mechanism

Motor-neuron ACh → receptor opening → end-plate depolarisation → muscle action potential.

## Physiological consequence

Initiates skeletal-muscle excitation–contraction coupling.
