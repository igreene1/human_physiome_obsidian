# H3 Histamine Receptor

**Role:** Gi/o-coupled presynaptic histamine receptor acting as an autoreceptor and heteroreceptor.

## Canonical mechanism

Activation decreases cAMP and transmitter release.

## Physiological consequence

Provides feedback control over histamine and other neurotransmitter systems.
