# Glycine Receptor

**Role:** Ligand-gated chloride channel prominent in spinal cord and brainstem inhibitory synapses.

## Canonical mechanism

Glycine binding opens Cl⁻ channels and usually suppresses neuronal firing.

## Physiological consequence

Important for motor, sensory and reflex circuitry.
