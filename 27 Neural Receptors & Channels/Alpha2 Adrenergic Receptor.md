# Alpha2 Adrenergic Receptor

**Role:** Gi/o-coupled adrenergic receptor with important presynaptic and central inhibitory functions.

## Canonical mechanism

Noradrenaline/adrenaline → α2 → ↓ cAMP; can reduce Ca²⁺ entry and transmitter release.

## Physiological consequence

Acts as an autoreceptor on sympathetic terminals and contributes to central sympathetic regulation.
