# D2-like Dopamine Receptors

**Role:** D2, D3 and D4 dopamine receptors, generally Gi/o-coupled.

## Canonical mechanism

Dopamine → D2-like receptor → ↓ cAMP; G-protein subunits also modulate ion channels.

## Physiological consequence

D2 receptors serve as postsynaptic modulators and presynaptic autoreceptors; pituitary D2 signalling inhibits prolactin release.
