# H1 Histamine Receptor

**Role:** Gq/11-coupled histamine receptor involved in wakefulness and many peripheral inflammatory/smooth-muscle responses.

## Canonical mechanism

Histamine → H1 → PLC/IP3/DAG/Ca²⁺.

## Physiological consequence

Central H1 signalling supports arousal; peripheral H1 contributes to vascular permeability and sensory responses.
