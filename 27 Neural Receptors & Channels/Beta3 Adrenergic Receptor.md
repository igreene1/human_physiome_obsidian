# Beta3 Adrenergic Receptor

**Role:** Predominantly Gs-coupled adrenergic receptor expressed in adipose tissue and urinary bladder detrusor among other sites.

## Canonical mechanism

Catecholamine → β3 → adenylyl cyclase → cAMP/PKA.

## Physiological consequence

Supports lipolytic/thermogenic signalling in adipocytes and detrusor relaxation.
