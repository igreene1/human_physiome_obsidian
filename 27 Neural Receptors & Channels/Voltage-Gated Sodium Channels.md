# Voltage-Gated Sodium Channels

**Role:** Family of voltage-gated channels responsible for the rapid upstroke of action potentials in many excitable cells.

## Canonical mechanism

Membrane depolarisation opens channels rapidly; fast inactivation follows; recovery requires repolarisation.

## Physiological consequence

Different Nav isoforms specialise in neurons, skeletal muscle and heart.
