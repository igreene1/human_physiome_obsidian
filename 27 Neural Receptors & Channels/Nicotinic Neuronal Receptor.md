# Nicotinic Neuronal Receptor

**Role:** Ligand-gated cation channel on autonomic ganglia, adrenal chromaffin cells and throughout the CNS.

## Canonical mechanism

Acetylcholine → receptor opens → Na⁺/Ca²⁺ influx with K⁺ efflux → rapid depolarisation.

## Physiological consequence

Converts all autonomic preganglionic cholinergic signals into postganglionic excitation; also drives adrenal medullary secretion.
