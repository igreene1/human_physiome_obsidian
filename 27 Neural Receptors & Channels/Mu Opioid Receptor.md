# Mu Opioid Receptor

**Role:** Gi/o-coupled opioid receptor activated by endogenous opioid peptides and exogenous opioids.

## Canonical mechanism

Activation decreases adenylyl cyclase, reduces presynaptic Ca²⁺ entry and opens postsynaptic K⁺ channels.

## Physiological consequence

Reduces nociceptive transmission but also affects reward, respiration, GI motility and autonomic function.
