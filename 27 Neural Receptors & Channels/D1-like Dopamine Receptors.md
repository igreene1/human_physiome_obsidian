# D1-like Dopamine Receptors

**Role:** D1 and D5 dopamine receptors, generally Gs/olf-coupled.

## Canonical mechanism

Dopamine → D1-like receptor → ↑ adenylyl cyclase/cAMP/PKA.

## Physiological consequence

Facilitate selected neuronal pathways; in striatum they help modulate direct-pathway signalling.
