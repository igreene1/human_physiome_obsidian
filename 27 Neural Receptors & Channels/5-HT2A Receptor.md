# 5-HT2A Receptor

**Role:** Gq/11-coupled serotonin receptor widely expressed in cortex and peripheral tissues.

## Canonical mechanism

Serotonin → 5-HT2A → PLC → IP3/DAG → Ca²⁺/PKC.

## Physiological consequence

Modulates cortical excitation, smooth-muscle and platelet-related responses depending on tissue.
