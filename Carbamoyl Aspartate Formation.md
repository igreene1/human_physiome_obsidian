# Carbamoyl Aspartate Formation

**Part of:** [[Pyrimidine and Thymidylate Synthesis]]

## Transformation

Carbamoyl phosphate + aspartate → carbamoyl aspartate + Pi

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | Aspartate-transcarbamoylase domain of CAD |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Combines nitrogen/carbon precursors before ring closure |

## What controls this step

In human cells this activity is part of the multifunctional CAD protein rather than a separate bacterial-style regulatory enzyme. Substrate channeling and CAD regulation influence flux.
