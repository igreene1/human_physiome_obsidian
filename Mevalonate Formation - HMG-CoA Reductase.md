# Mevalonate Formation - HMG-CoA Reductase

**Part of:** [[Cholesterol and Lipoprotein Metabolism]]

## Transformation

HMG-CoA + 2 NADPH → mevalonate + CoA-SH + 2 NADP+

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | HMGCR |
| Compartment | Endoplasmic reticulum membrane; catalytic domain faces cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Major regulated early reaction in cholesterol synthesis |

## What controls this step

Low sterols promote SREBP2-dependent expression; sterols promote feedback and degradation. AMPK phosphorylation inhibits activity. This is separate from mitochondrial ketogenesis.
