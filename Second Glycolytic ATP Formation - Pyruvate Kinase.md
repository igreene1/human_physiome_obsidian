# Second Glycolytic ATP Formation - Pyruvate Kinase

**Part of:** [[Glycolysis]]

## Transformation

Phosphoenolpyruvate + ADP → pyruvate + ATP

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | PKLR or PKM isoforms; Mg2+ and K+ |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Substrate-level phosphorylation; two ATP per glucose, with strong forward drive |

## What controls this step

Fructose 1,6-bisphosphate activates several isoforms. ATP and alanine restrain selected isoforms; glucagon-PKA inhibits liver pyruvate kinase by phosphorylation. Do not generalise hepatic regulation to all muscle isoforms.
