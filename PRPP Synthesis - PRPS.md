# PRPP Synthesis - PRPS

**Part of:** [[Purine Synthesis and Salvage]]

## Transformation

Ribose 5-phosphate + ATP → PRPP + AMP

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | PRPS1/PRPS2; Mg2+ |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Activates ribose for nucleotide synthesis and salvage |

## What controls this step

Consumes ATP to AMP and is regulated by nucleotide/energy state. PRPP participates in purine salvage and pyrimidine synthesis, so formation is not exclusive commitment to purines.
