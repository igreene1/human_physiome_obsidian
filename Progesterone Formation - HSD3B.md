# Progesterone Formation - HSD3B

**Part of:** [[Steroid Hormone Synthesis]]

## Transformation

Pregnenolone + NAD+ → progesterone + NADH, with double-bond isomerisation

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | HSD3B1 or HSD3B2 |
| Compartment | Steroidogenic membrane-associated systems, principally ER and mitochondrial-associated locations |
| Physiological direction | Oxidises the 3beta hydroxyl and rearranges the double bond |

## What controls this step

Related HSD3B chemistry acts on other delta-5 steroid precursors. Isoenzyme and tissue expression determine physiological contribution.
