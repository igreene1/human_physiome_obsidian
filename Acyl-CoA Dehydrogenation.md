# Acyl-CoA Dehydrogenation

**Part of:** [[Lipolysis and Beta Oxidation]]

## Transformation

Fatty acyl-CoA → trans-2-enoyl-CoA; electrons transferred to ETF and ubiquinone

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | ACADVL, ACADM, ACADS and related enzymes; bound FAD; ETFA/ETFB and ETFDH |
| Compartment | Mitochondrial matrix or matrix-facing inner membrane |
| Physiological direction | First oxidation of the beta-oxidation spiral |

## What controls this step

Different chain lengths use different enzymes. FAD-bound reducing equivalents reach ubiquinone through ETF, not through respiratory complex II.
