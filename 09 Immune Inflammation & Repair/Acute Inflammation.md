# Acute Inflammation

**Function:** Recruits cells and plasma proteins to contain injury or infection.

**Where:** Local microvasculature, interstitium and leukocytes.

## Mechanism

1. Resident cells detect injury and release cytokines, lipid mediators and chemokines.
2. Vasodilation and altered endothelial permeability change local fluid/protein distribution.
3. Leukocytes roll, adhere and cross the endothelium toward chemotactic signals.
4. Phagocytes remove microbes/debris while feedback limits collateral damage.

## Control and constraints

Redness, warmth, swelling and pain arise from different mechanisms. Resolution is an active programme and need not wait for every inflammatory mediator to passively disappear.
