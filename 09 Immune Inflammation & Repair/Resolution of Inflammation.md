# Resolution of Inflammation

**Function:** Ends recruitment and restores tissue function after a threat is controlled.

**Where:** Inflamed tissues, phagocytes, endothelium and lymphatics.

## Mechanism

1. Pro-inflammatory inputs fall and mediator production changes.
2. Neutrophil apoptosis limits continued tissue exposure to destructive contents.
3. Macrophages clear apoptotic cells and shift local repair signals.
4. Lymphatic drainage, matrix remodelling and epithelial restoration rebuild function.

## Control and constraints

Resolution is not identical to blanket suppression; defence and repair must remain coordinated. Persistent antigen, failed clearance or ongoing injury can prevent resolution and favour fibrosis.
