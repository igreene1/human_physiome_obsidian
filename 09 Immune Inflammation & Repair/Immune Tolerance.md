# Immune Tolerance

**Function:** Restrains harmful responses to self and appropriately encountered harmless antigens.

**Where:** Thymus, marrow, lymphoid tissues and peripheral organs.

## Mechanism

1. Central selection removes or edits many strongly self-reactive lymphocyte receptors.
2. Peripheral recognition without suitable context can produce anergy or deletion.
3. Regulatory T cells and inhibitory receptors limit activation.
4. Tissue signals, antigen handling and clearance of dying cells support non-inflammatory outcomes.

## Control and constraints

Tolerance is layered rather than absolute. AIRE/FEZF2 help broaden thymic self-antigen representation. Immune suppression, ignorance and antigen-specific tolerance are related but distinct states.
