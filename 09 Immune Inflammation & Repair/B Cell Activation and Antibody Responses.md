# B Cell Activation and Antibody Responses

**Function:** Produces antibodies with appropriate specificity and effector functions.

**Where:** Follicles, germinal centres, plasma-cell niches and circulation.

## Mechanism

1. BCR binding internalises antigen and triggers signalling.
2. For many protein antigens, cognate T-cell help through CD40 and cytokines supports expansion.
3. Germinal-centre mutation and selection improve affinity, while class switching changes constant-region function.
4. Plasma cells secrete antibody and memory B cells support later responses.

## Control and constraints

Class switching changes isotype without changing the underlying antigen-binding variable region by itself. T-independent responses exist and differ from germinal-centre responses. Selection must limit autoreactivity.
