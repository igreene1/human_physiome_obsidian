# Antigen Processing and Presentation

**Function:** Displays peptide information to T cells in MHC complexes.

**Where:** Antigen-presenting cells and most nucleated cells for MHC I.

## Mechanism

1. Cytosolic proteins are degraded and peptides transported through TAP for loading onto MHC I.
2. Endosomal/lysosomal proteins supply peptides for MHC II loading after invariant-chain processing.
3. Loaded complexes reach the surface and interact with appropriate T-cell receptors.
4. Costimulation and cytokines determine the outcome of recognition.

## Control and constraints

MHC I typically engages CD8 cells and MHC II CD4 cells. Cross-presentation allows selected dendritic cells to place external antigen on MHC I. Peptide recognition alone is insufficient to define activation versus tolerance.
