# Innate Immune Recognition

**Function:** Detects microbial structures and tissue disturbance before antigen-specific clonal expansion.

**Where:** Epithelial cells, phagocytes and other innate-sensing cells.

## Mechanism

1. Surface/endosomal TLRs detect selected extracellular or internalised patterns.
2. Cytosolic sensors detect microbial nucleic acids or danger-associated changes.
3. Adaptor networks activate NF-kB, IRFs and other transcriptional programmes.
4. Cytokines, interferons and antimicrobial functions recruit coordinated responses.

## Control and constraints

A pattern-recognition receptor does not identify every pathogen species. Sensor localisation and regulatory thresholds limit inappropriate responses to self. Innate memory-like reprogramming exists but differs from antigen-receptor-based adaptive memory.
