# Physical and Chemical Barriers

**Function:** Limits microbial entry while maintaining exchange with the environment.

**Where:** Skin and respiratory, gut and genitourinary mucosa.

## Mechanism

1. Tight junctions and stratified or specialised epithelia restrict access.
2. Mucus, cilia, flow and shedding remove or displace material.
3. Antimicrobial peptides, acidic environments and secretory factors limit growth.
4. Resident immune cells sample breaches and recruit additional defences.

## Control and constraints

Barrier function is active and tissue-specific. Harmless antigens must often be tolerated, so impermeability alone is not the goal. Injury can convert a physical barrier problem into inflammatory signalling.
