# Phagocytosis and Microbial Killing

**Function:** Internalises and degrades particles, microbes and damaged cells.

**Where:** Neutrophils, macrophages and other phagocytes.

## Mechanism

1. Pattern, Fc or complement receptors bind a target and organise actin-dependent engulfment.
2. The phagosome matures and fuses with lysosomal compartments.
3. Acidification, proteases and oxidant-generating systems attack the contents.
4. Degraded material is cleared; antigen presentation or resolution programmes may follow.

## Control and constraints

NOX2 generates superoxide in phagosomal or extracellular contexts. Neutrophil MPO can use peroxide and chloride to generate hypochlorous acid. Killing and clearance must be restricted to avoid host damage.
