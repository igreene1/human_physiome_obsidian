# Complement System

**Function:** Tags targets, amplifies inflammation and can form membrane pores.

**Where:** Plasma and tissue-fluid protein cascades.

## Mechanism

1. Classical, lectin and alternative initiation routes generate C3 convertase activity.
2. C3 cleavage deposits C3b on suitable surfaces and releases C3a.
3. C5 convertase activity generates C5a and C5b.
4. C5b recruits terminal components to assemble a membrane attack complex.

## Control and constraints

Host regulators including factor H, factor I, CD55 and CD59 constrain damage. C3b opsonisation and C5a signalling are distinct from direct pore formation; the terminal complex is especially effective against selected susceptible microbes.
