# T Cell Activation and Effector Function

**Function:** Expands and differentiates T cells that recognise relevant antigen.

**Where:** Lymphoid tissues and sites of immune activity.

## Mechanism

1. TCR recognition of peptide-MHC activates CD3-associated Lck/ZAP70 signalling.
2. Costimulation and cytokines shape PI3K, NFAT, NF-kB and AP-1 responses.
3. IL-2-linked proliferation and differentiation create helper, cytotoxic or regulatory populations.
4. Effectors act in tissues; contraction leaves selected memory cells.

## Control and constraints

Naive T-cell activation usually needs professional antigen-presenting cells. Effector function is subset- and context-dependent; CD8 cytotoxicity can use granule pathways or death receptors.
