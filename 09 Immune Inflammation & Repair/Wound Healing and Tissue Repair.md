# Wound Healing and Tissue Repair

**Function:** Restores barrier and mechanical integrity after injury.

**Where:** Platelets, leukocytes, fibroblasts, endothelial cells and tissue progenitors.

## Mechanism

1. Hemostasis limits blood loss and creates a provisional matrix.
2. Inflammatory cells clear debris and coordinate repair signals.
3. Keratinocytes/progenitors restore surfaces while fibroblasts deposit matrix and angiogenesis supports the wound.
4. Matrix turnover and myofibroblast activity remodel and contract the repair.

## Control and constraints

Phases overlap. Regeneration and scar formation vary by tissue and injury extent. Continued TGF-beta/mechanical signalling can favour fibrosis; restored structure does not always reproduce the original tissue architecture.
