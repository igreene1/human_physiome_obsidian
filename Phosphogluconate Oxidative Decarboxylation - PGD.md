# Phosphogluconate Oxidative Decarboxylation - PGD

**Part of:** [[Pentose Phosphate Pathway]]

## Transformation

6-Phosphogluconate + NADP+ → ribulose 5-phosphate + CO2 + NADPH

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | PGD |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Produces the second NADPH and removes one carbon from the original hexose |

## What controls this step

Oxidative pathway flux depends on NADP+ demand and substrate delivery. One glucose 6-phosphate yields two NADPH through the oxidative branch.
