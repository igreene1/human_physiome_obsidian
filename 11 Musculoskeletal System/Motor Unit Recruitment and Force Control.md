# Motor Unit Recruitment and Force Control

**Function:** Adjusts muscle force with recruited fibres and firing frequency.

**Where:** Alpha motor neurons and the fibres each innervates.

## Mechanism

1. Synaptic drive recruits motor neurons according to excitability and task demands.
2. The size principle commonly brings lower-threshold units into action before higher-threshold units.
3. Higher firing rates allow temporal summation of calcium transients and twitches.
4. Sensory feedback and central commands tune recruitment and coordination.

## Control and constraints

Motor units differ in fatigue resistance and contractile properties. Force is not proportional only to action-potential amplitude, which is relatively stereotyped within a fibre.
