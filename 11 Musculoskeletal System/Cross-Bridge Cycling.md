# Cross-Bridge Cycling

**Function:** Converts ATP chemical energy into actin-myosin force and movement.

**Where:** Sarcomeres of striated muscle; related chemistry operates in other myosins.

## Mechanism

1. ATP binding detaches myosin from actin.
2. ATP hydrolysis primes the myosin head while ADP and phosphate remain bound.
3. Permissive thin-filament activation allows actin binding; phosphate release is associated with force generation.
4. ADP release leaves a strongly bound state until new ATP permits detachment.

## Control and constraints

Calcium regulates access via troponin/tropomyosin in striated muscle, not by being consumed each cycle. Force depends on overlap, load, recruitment and calcium activation; without ATP, detachment fails.
