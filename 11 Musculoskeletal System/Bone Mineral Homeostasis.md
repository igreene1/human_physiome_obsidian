# Bone Mineral Homeostasis

**Function:** Integrates a structural mineral reservoir with circulating calcium/phosphate needs.

**Where:** Bone matrix, intestine, kidneys and endocrine regulators.

## Mechanism

1. Dietary absorption supplies calcium and phosphate.
2. Bone-forming cells generate a matrix environment permissive to mineral deposition.
3. Resorptive activity releases mineral when remodelling signals recruit osteoclasts.
4. PTH, calcitriol and FGF23 coordinate renal and intestinal contributions.

## Control and constraints

Serum mineral levels can be maintained at the expense of bone stores. Mineralisation and bone turnover are different processes; normal serum calcium does not establish normal bone strength.
