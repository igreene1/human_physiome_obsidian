# Bone Remodelling

**Function:** Renews bone through coupled resorption and formation.

**Where:** Osteoclasts, osteoblast-lineage cells and osteocytes.

## Mechanism

1. Osteocytes detect loading/damage and influence local remodelling signals.
2. RANKL with supporting factors promotes osteoclast differentiation; OPG acts as a decoy receptor.
3. Osteoclasts acidify a sealed resorption compartment and degrade mineral/organic matrix.
4. Osteoblasts lay down osteoid and support mineralisation, with some becoming osteocytes.

## Control and constraints

Resorption and formation are coupled but can become imbalanced. PTH, sex steroids, mechanical load and local cytokines alter the balance; osteoclasts arise from hematopoietic lineage, osteoblasts from mesenchymal lineage.
