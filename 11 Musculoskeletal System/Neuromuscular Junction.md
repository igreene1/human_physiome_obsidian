# Neuromuscular Junction

**Function:** Transmits motor-neuron excitation to a skeletal muscle fibre.

**Where:** Motor terminal, synaptic cleft and muscle endplate.

## Mechanism

1. Motor-terminal Ca2+ entry triggers acetylcholine release.
2. Muscle nicotinic receptors conduct cations, generating an endplate potential.
3. If threshold is reached, nearby voltage-gated sodium channels initiate a muscle action potential.
4. Acetylcholinesterase hydrolyses acetylcholine; choline is recovered for synthesis.

## Control and constraints

The endplate potential is graded; the propagated muscle action potential is regenerative. The normal safety margin supports reliable transmission, but release, receptor function and membrane excitability are distinct possible failure points.
