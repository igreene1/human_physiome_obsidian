# Exercise Adaptation

**Function:** Changes muscle, cardiovascular and metabolic capacity after repeated loading.

**Where:** Muscle fibres, satellite cells, vessels and whole-body control systems.

## Mechanism

1. Mechanical tension and metabolic stress activate signals such as mTORC1, AMPK and calcium-dependent pathways.
2. Resistance-related signals favour protein synthesis and structural remodelling.
3. Endurance-related signals recruit mitochondrial biogenesis, oxidative enzymes and capillary adaptation.
4. Recovery and repeated exposure consolidate changes in capacity.

## Control and constraints

Responses depend on training stimulus, recovery, nutrition and baseline state. An acute signalling increase does not guarantee a specific long-term adaptation; neural learning also improves performance.
