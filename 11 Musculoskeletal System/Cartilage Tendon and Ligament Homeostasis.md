# Cartilage Tendon and Ligament Homeostasis

**Function:** Maintains connective tissues that distribute or transmit load.

**Where:** Chondrocytes, tenocytes, extracellular matrix and tissue-specific vascular niches.

## Mechanism

1. Cells sense mechanical load through integrins, ion channels and cytoskeletal connections.
2. Collagen and proteoglycan synthesis supports tensile or compressive function.
3. Matrix proteases and their inhibitors regulate turnover.
4. Loading, injury signals and nutrient supply influence repair and adaptation.

## Control and constraints

Articular cartilage is largely avascular and has limited repair capacity; tendon/ligament organisation differs from cartilage. A chain such as collagen to proteoglycan does not imply these are sequential substrates.
