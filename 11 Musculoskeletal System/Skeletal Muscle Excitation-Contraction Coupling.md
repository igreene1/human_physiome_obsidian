# Skeletal Muscle Excitation-Contraction Coupling

**Function:** Links muscle-membrane excitation to calcium-dependent force.

**Where:** Sarcolemma, T tubules, sarcoplasmic reticulum and sarcomere.

## Mechanism

1. An action potential spreads over the sarcolemma and into T tubules.
2. CaV1.1 voltage sensing mechanically couples to RyR1-mediated SR calcium release.
3. Calcium binds troponin C and shifts thin-filament regulation.
4. SERCA returns calcium to SR, reducing activation and enabling relaxation.

## Control and constraints

Skeletal triggering differs from the calcium-induced calcium-release mechanism of cardiac muscle. Calsequestrin and other proteins shape SR storage/release; ATP is required for both cross-bridge cycling and calcium reuptake.
