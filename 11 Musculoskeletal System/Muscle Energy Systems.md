# Muscle Energy Systems

**Function:** Maintains ATP supply across rapid and sustained muscle work.

**Where:** Myocyte cytosol, mitochondria and substrate stores.

## Mechanism

1. Existing ATP supports only a brief period of high demand.
2. Phosphocreatine rapidly buffers ADP through creatine kinase.
3. Glycolysis increases ATP production and exchanges lactate/redox equivalents.
4. Oxidative phosphorylation increasingly supports sustained work using carbohydrates and fatty acids.

## Control and constraints

All systems overlap from exercise onset; they do not switch on one at a time at rigid time cut-offs. Recovery includes phosphocreatine restoration, ion-gradient restoration and substrate replenishment.
