# Pigmentation and Photoprotection

**Function:** Produces and distributes pigment that influences light absorption and UV protection.

**Where:** Melanocytes and neighbouring epidermal keratinocytes.

## Mechanism

1. Melanocyte signalling regulates tyrosinase and melanogenic machinery within melanosomes.
2. Tyrosine-derived intermediates form eumelanin and/or pheomelanin through branching chemistry.
3. Melanosomes are transferred to keratinocytes and positioned around nuclei.
4. UV exposure can alter melanogenesis and stress responses.

## Control and constraints

Melanin reduces some UV damage but does not eliminate it. Pigment output depends on synthesis, type, packaging and distribution, not simply the number of melanocytes.
