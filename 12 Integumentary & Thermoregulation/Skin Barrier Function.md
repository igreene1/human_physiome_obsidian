# Skin Barrier Function

**Function:** Restricts water loss and entry of harmful substances while supporting sensation and immunity.

**Where:** Epidermis, particularly stratum corneum and granular-layer junctions.

## Mechanism

1. Basal keratinocytes proliferate and move outward during differentiation.
2. Keratin and cross-linked envelope proteins strengthen corneocytes.
3. Lamellar-body secretion supplies extracellular lipids that organise the water barrier.
4. Controlled proteolysis and desquamation renew the surface.

## Control and constraints

The barrier depends on both cells and lipid architecture. Filaggrin processing contributes natural moisturising factors; junctions and antimicrobial defence add distinct layers. Epidermal turnover changes with injury and inflammation.
