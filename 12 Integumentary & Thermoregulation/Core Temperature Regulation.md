# Core Temperature Regulation

**Function:** Balances heat production and loss across environments and activity.

**Where:** Peripheral/central thermosensors and hypothalamic effector networks.

## Mechanism

1. Temperature-sensitive afferents and central neurons report thermal conditions.
2. Preoptic and associated circuits integrate temperature with circadian, immune and behavioural signals.
3. Sweating and cutaneous vasodilation increase heat loss; vasoconstriction/shivering conserve or generate heat.
4. Behaviour changes clothing, exposure and activity.

## Control and constraints

Fever involves immune-driven changes in thermoregulatory control; hyperthermia can occur without such a regulated shift. Heat transfer depends on humidity, air movement, surface area and clothing, not body temperature alone.
