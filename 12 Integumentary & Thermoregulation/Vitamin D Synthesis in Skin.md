# Vitamin D Synthesis in Skin

**Function:** Initiates vitamin D production from a cholesterol-derived precursor.

**Where:** UVB-exposed epidermis; later activation occurs elsewhere.

## Mechanism

1. UVB converts 7-dehydrocholesterol to previtamin D3.
2. Thermal rearrangement generates vitamin D3.
3. Transport to liver permits 25-hydroxylation, producing the main circulating status marker.
4. Kidney and selected other tissues perform regulated 1alpha-hydroxylation to calcitriol.

## Control and constraints

Skin production varies with exposure, season, pigmentation and other factors. Cutaneous vitamin D is not already active calcitriol. PTH, phosphate/FGF23 and feedback influence renal activation.
