# Hair and Nail Growth

**Function:** Produces keratinised appendages with tissue-specific growth programmes.

**Where:** Hair follicles and nail matrix.

## Mechanism

1. Hair matrix keratinocytes proliferate and differentiate under follicular signalling control.
2. Hair cycles alternate growth, regression and relative rest rather than proceeding continuously in each follicle.
3. Nail-matrix cells form a progressively keratinised plate.
4. Local niche, endocrine and nutritional signals influence growth.

## Control and constraints

Hair-cycle stages vary across body regions; shafts are nonliving keratinised structures. Follicular stem cells and dermal papilla signalling determine regeneration. Nail and hair growth are not identical programmes.
