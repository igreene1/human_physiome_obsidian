# Cutaneous Blood Flow

**Function:** Adjusts skin heat exchange while participating in systemic circulatory demands.

**Where:** Cutaneous arterioles, venous plexuses and arteriovenous connections.

## Mechanism

1. Cold increases sympathetic vasoconstrictor activity, reducing warm blood delivery to the skin.
2. Heat reduces constrictor influence and recruits active vasodilator mechanisms in relevant skin regions.
3. Local temperature and endothelial signals further modify vessel tone.
4. Blood transfers heat between core and skin for external loss.

## Control and constraints

Skin circulation is not regulated solely by local oxygen demand. Thermoregulation competes with pressure maintenance, especially during heat and dehydration. Glabrous and non-glabrous skin have different control features.
