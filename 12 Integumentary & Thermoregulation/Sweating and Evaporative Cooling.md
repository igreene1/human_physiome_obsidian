# Sweating and Evaporative Cooling

**Function:** Loses heat through evaporation of sweat from the skin.

**Where:** Eccrine glands and skin surface.

## Mechanism

1. Sympathetic cholinergic fibres release acetylcholine onto glandular muscarinic receptors.
2. Secretory coils produce primary fluid through coupled ion and water movement.
3. Ducts reabsorb some salt while remaining relatively water-impermeable.
4. Evaporation consumes heat from the skin.

## Control and constraints

Sweat that drips off without evaporating provides much less cooling. High humidity limits evaporation. Acclimatisation and aldosterone alter salt conservation; high flow leaves less time for ductal reabsorption.
