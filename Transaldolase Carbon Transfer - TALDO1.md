# Transaldolase Carbon Transfer - TALDO1

**Part of:** [[Pentose Phosphate Pathway]]

## Transformation

Sedoheptulose 7-phosphate + glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate ⇌ fructose 6-phosphate + erythrose 4-phosphate

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | TALDO1; catalytic lysine Schiff base |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Transfers a three-carbon unit; no NADPH is produced in this non-oxidative step |

## What controls this step

Direction follows substrate requirements. The full non-oxidative network interconverts three pentoses with two fructose 6-phosphates and one glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate.
