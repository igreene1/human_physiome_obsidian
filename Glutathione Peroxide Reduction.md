# Glutathione Peroxide Reduction

**Part of:** [[Reactive Oxygen Species and Antioxidant Defence]]

## Transformation

2 GSH + H2O2 → GSSG + 2 H2O

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | Glutathione peroxidases; several are selenoproteins |
| Compartment | Cytosol and other compartments, depending on isoenzyme |
| Physiological direction | Uses reduced glutathione to remove peroxide |

## What controls this step

Glutathione reductase uses NADPH to restore GSH. GPX4 acts on phospholipid hydroperoxides and protects against ferroptosis; it is not simply interchangeable with GPX1.
