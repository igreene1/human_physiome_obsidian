# Glutamate Decarboxylation - GAD

**Part of:** [[GABA]]

## Transformation

Glutamate → GABA + CO2

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | GAD1/GAD67 or GAD2/GAD65; pyridoxal phosphate |
| Compartment | GABAergic neuronal cytosol and terminal-associated pools |
| Physiological direction | Converts an excitatory-transmitter precursor into an inhibitory-transmitter molecule in appropriate cells |

## What controls this step

Receptor/ion-gradient context determines the eventual electrical effect of GABA. GAD isoforms have overlapping but different localisation/regulation.
