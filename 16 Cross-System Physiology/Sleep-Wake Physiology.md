# Sleep-Wake Physiology

**Function:** Coordinates arousal, endocrine timing, autonomic state and behaviour across the day.

**Where:** Circadian pacemaker, sleep homeostat and distributed sleep/wake networks.

## Mechanism

1. Circadian signals bias the probability of sleep or wakefulness at different biological times.
2. Prior waking increases sleep pressure through multiple mechanisms.
3. Sleep-state transitions reorganise cortical rhythms, autonomic activity and hormone secretion.
4. Light, meals, activity and social timing influence synchronisation.

## Control and constraints

Sleep duration does not fully describe sleep quality or circadian alignment. NREM and REM have different physiological patterns; melatonin and cortisol rhythms are related outputs rather than the entire control system.
