# Carbon Dioxide and pH Homeostasis

**Regulated quantity:** PaCO2 and associated acid-base effects.

| Control-loop element | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Sensors / integration | Central chemosensitive networks and peripheral chemoreceptors |
| Effectors | Respiratory muscles and ventilation, with renal bicarbonate adjustment over time |
| Example response | Higher CO2 production requires greater alveolar ventilation to hold PaCO2 constant |
| Time scales | Seconds-minutes for breathing; hours-days for renal compensation |
| Feedback / constraint | CO2 removal reduces the chemosensory stimulus; metabolic acid changes can independently affect ventilation |
