# Acute Stress Response

**Function:** Prioritises immediate coping and resource availability.

**Where:** Autonomic and HPA networks with multiple tissue targets.

## Mechanism

1. Appraisal and interoceptive signals recruit hypothalamic/brainstem outputs.
2. Sympathetic nerves and adrenal catecholamines rapidly alter cardiovascular and metabolic activity.
3. The HPA axis adds slower cortisol effects.
4. Negative feedback and resolution of the demand support recovery.

## Control and constraints

Responses depend on the type, duration and controllability of the stressor. Similar outward symptoms may result from unrelated endocrine, cardiac or other processes.
