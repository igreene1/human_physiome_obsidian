# Heat Response

**Function:** Disposes of excess metabolic or environmental heat.

**Where:** Skin circulation, eccrine glands, cardiovascular and fluid-balance systems.

## Mechanism

1. Thermal signals increase skin heat transfer through vasodilation.
2. Sweat secretion increases, and evaporation removes heat where environmental conditions permit.
3. Fluid losses recruit thirst and renal water-conserving signals.
4. Behaviour reduces heat gain and workload.

## Control and constraints

Sweating without effective evaporation is insufficient cooling. Maintaining skin flow and arterial pressure competes for circulating volume, especially with dehydration.
