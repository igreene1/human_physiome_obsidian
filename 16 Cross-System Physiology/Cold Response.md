# Cold Response

**Function:** Defends core temperature when heat loss threatens balance.

**Where:** Skin vessels, skeletal muscle, thermogenic adipose and behaviour.

## Mechanism

1. Thermal afferents recruit heat-conserving vasoconstriction.
2. Shivering increases muscle ATP hydrolysis and heat generation.
3. Sympathetic signalling can recruit UCP1-mediated thermogenesis in relevant adipocytes.
4. Behaviour reduces exposure and increases insulation.

## Control and constraints

Vasoconstriction preserves core heat at the expense of peripheral warmth. Brown-fat contribution varies; acclimatisation is different from the immediate cold response.
