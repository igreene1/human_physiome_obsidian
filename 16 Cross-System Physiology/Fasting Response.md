# Fasting Response

**Function:** Maintains essential fuel supply while mobilising stored energy.

**Where:** Liver, adipose, muscle, brain and kidney.

## Mechanism

1. Falling insulin permits increased hepatic glucose output and adipose lipolysis.
2. Liver glycogen contributes early, while gluconeogenesis uses lactate, glycerol and amino-acid carbon.
3. Fatty-acid oxidation supplies energy and hepatic ketone production increases with prolonged low-insulin conditions.
4. Brain ketone use rises over time, changing glucose and amino-acid requirements.

## Control and constraints

The time course depends on stores, activity and prior intake. Red cells remain dependent on glycolysis; kidney glucose production and nitrogen handling become more important in relevant conditions.
