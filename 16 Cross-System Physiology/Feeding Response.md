# Feeding Response

**Function:** Coordinates ingestion, absorption and storage of incoming nutrients.

**Where:** Gut, pancreas, liver, muscle, adipose tissue and brain.

## Mechanism

1. Cephalic/vagal signals prepare secretion before nutrients fully reach blood.
2. Absorption and incretins increase glucose-dependent insulin secretion.
3. Liver and muscle replenish glycogen while adipose/liver pathways favour storage when substrate supply permits.
4. Gut and central signals contribute satiation and later satiety.

## Control and constraints

Insulin does not force every tissue to use glucose exclusively. Protein-rich meals can also increase glucagon, and nutrient composition changes the response.
