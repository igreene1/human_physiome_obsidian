# Arterial Blood Pressure Homeostasis

**Regulated quantity:** Arterial pressure adequate for organ perfusion.

| Control-loop element | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Sensors / integration | Carotid/aortic stretch receptors, renal perfusion and macula-densa signals |
| Effectors | Heart, resistance vessels, capacitance veins and renal salt/water handling |
| Example response | A sudden pressure fall usually reduces baroreceptor firing and recruits cardiac/vascular sympathetic responses |
| Time scales | Seconds for baroreflex; hours-days for fluid balance and renal adaptation |
| Feedback / constraint | Restored stretch suppresses reflex drive; pressure-natriuresis and hormonal feedback alter longer-term volume |
