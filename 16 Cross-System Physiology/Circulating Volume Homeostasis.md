# Circulating Volume Homeostasis

**Regulated quantity:** Effective arterial filling and venous return, distinct from total extracellular fluid volume.

| Control-loop element | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Sensors / integration | Arterial and cardiopulmonary mechanosensors; renal perfusion and NaCl delivery |
| Effectors | Venous tone, heart, renal sodium/water retention and transvascular/lymphatic exchange |
| Example response | Acute loss recruits sympathetic responses; hormonal and renal responses conserve salt/water |
| Time scales | Seconds for redistribution; hours-days for replenishment or excretion |
| Feedback / constraint | Improved filling restrains retention; persistent low cardiac effectiveness can maintain retention despite fluid excess |
