# Blood Glucose Homeostasis

**Regulated quantity:** Circulating glucose availability relative to tissue demand.

| Control-loop element | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Sensors / integration | Pancreatic alpha/beta cells; neural and portal nutrient-sensing networks |
| Effectors | Insulin promotes storage/uptake in responsive tissues and restrains hepatic production; glucagon supports hepatic production |
| Example response | Meal absorption increases insulin drive; fasting reduces insulin and favours glycogenolysis/gluconeogenesis |
| Time scales | Seconds-minutes for secretion and uptake changes; hours-days for transcription and substrate adaptation |
| Feedback / constraint | Glucose and intra-islet signals modify further hormone secretion; fuel stores and energy expenditure constrain the response |
