# Tissue Injury Response

**Function:** Coordinates bleeding control, defence, clearance and reconstruction.

**Where:** Damaged tissue, blood, immune system and repair-cell niches.

## Mechanism

1. Damage exposes matrix and releases signals that recruit hemostasis and innate sensing.
2. Platelets/coagulation limit blood loss while inflammatory cells address contamination and debris.
3. Efferocytosis and changing mediators favour repair.
4. New vessels, matrix deposition and tissue-specific regeneration restore function where possible.

## Control and constraints

Healing can trade perfect architecture for rapid closure. Persistent injury or dysregulated repair can produce fibrosis. Stages overlap in time and vary substantially by organ.
