# Water and Osmolality Homeostasis

**Regulated quantity:** Effective extracellular osmolality and sufficient water availability.

| Control-loop element | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Sensors / integration | Hypothalamic osmosensitive networks plus arterial/cardiopulmonary volume-related inputs |
| Effectors | Thirst, vasopressin release and collecting-duct water permeability |
| Example response | Water deficit raises osmotic drive, increasing thirst and ADH-dependent water conservation |
| Time scales | Minutes for secretion/AQP2 trafficking; hours for net intake and excretion effects |
| Feedback / constraint | Water intake/retention lowers osmotic drive; severe circulatory depletion can prioritise volume defence over osmotic balance |
