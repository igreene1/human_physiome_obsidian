# Exercise Response

**Function:** Matches oxygen, substrate and heat handling to muscular work.

**Where:** Working muscle, heart, lungs, vessels, liver, adipose and skin.

## Mechanism

1. Motor commands and muscle contraction rapidly increase ATP demand and local metabolic signalling.
2. Heart rate, contractility and regional flow adjust delivery; ventilation responds through feedforward and feedback pathways.
3. Glycogen, circulating glucose and fatty acids contribute according to intensity/duration.
4. Heat production recruits thermoregulation while recovery replenishes stores and gradients.

## Control and constraints

Muscle glucose uptake can increase through contraction-related GLUT4 trafficking even without an insulin increase. Whole-body pressure and local vasodilation must be coordinated.
