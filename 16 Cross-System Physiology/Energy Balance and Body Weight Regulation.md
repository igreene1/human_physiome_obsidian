# Energy Balance and Body Weight Regulation

**Regulated quantity:** Energy stores and fuel availability, with body weight as one consequence.

| Control-loop element | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Sensors / integration | Adiposity/nutrient signals, gut-vagal input and hypothalamic/reward networks |
| Effectors | Food intake, activity, basal expenditure and substrate storage/mobilisation |
| Example response | Energy deficit alters appetite/endocrine signals and can reduce expenditure; feeding replenishes substrates and stores |
| Time scales | Minutes for meal signals; days-months for store changes and adaptation |
| Feedback / constraint | Leptin/insulin and other signals report state, but regulation is distributed and influenced by environment; no single fixed weight thermostat explains all behaviour |
