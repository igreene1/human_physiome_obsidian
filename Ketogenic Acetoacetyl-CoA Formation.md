# Ketogenic Acetoacetyl-CoA Formation

**Part of:** [[Ketone Body Metabolism]]

## Transformation

2 Acetyl-CoA ⇌ acetoacetyl-CoA + CoA-SH

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | ACAT1 and related mitochondrial thiolase activity |
| Compartment | Hepatic mitochondrial matrix |
| Physiological direction | Condenses acetyl units at the entry to ketogenesis |

## What controls this step

High fatty-acid-derived acetyl-CoA during low-insulin states favours substrate delivery. This reaction is reversible and is also used in ketone utilisation in other tissues.
