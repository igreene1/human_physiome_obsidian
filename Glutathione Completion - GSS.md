# Glutathione Completion - GSS

**Part of:** [[Reactive Oxygen Species and Antioxidant Defence]]

## Transformation

Gamma-glutamylcysteine + glycine + ATP → glutathione + ADP + Pi

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | GSS |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Adds glycine to form the tripeptide reducing cofactor |

## What controls this step

Synthesis replenishes the pool, while glutathione reductase recycles oxidised glutathione; these are different maintenance processes.
