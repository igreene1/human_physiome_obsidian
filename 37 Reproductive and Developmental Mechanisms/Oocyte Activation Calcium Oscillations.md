# Oocyte Activation Calcium Oscillations

**Function:** Releases the mature egg from arrest and initiates developmental programmes.

**Where:** Fertilised oocyte cytosol and ER calcium stores.

## Mechanism

1. Sperm delivery of factors including PLC-zeta promotes IP3 production.
2. IP3 receptors release ER Ca2+ in repeated transients.
3. Calcium-dependent signalling promotes cortical-granule exocytosis and cell-cycle resumption.
4. Meiosis II completes, pronuclear programmes develop and early embryonic activation proceeds.

## Control and constraints

The oscillatory pattern matters and species differ. Cortical granule effects help prevent polyspermy, but mammalian protection is not adequately captured by a universal fast membrane-voltage block.
