# Mammary Milk Synthesis

**Function:** Packages water, carbohydrate, lipid, protein and protective factors for infant nutrition.

**Where:** Mammary alveolar epithelial cells.

## Mechanism

1. Prolactin-JAK2-STAT5-associated signalling supports the differentiated secretory programme.
2. Lactose synthase, including alpha-lactalbumin-dependent specificity, generates lactose in Golgi compartments.
3. Lactose contributes osmotic water movement; proteins undergo secretory-pathway processing.
4. Lipids are synthesised/assembled and secreted through specialised milk-fat-globule pathways.

## Control and constraints

Oxytocin mediates ejection through myoepithelial contraction rather than synthesising milk. Local removal and feedback strongly influence continuing supply, and composition changes over lactation.
