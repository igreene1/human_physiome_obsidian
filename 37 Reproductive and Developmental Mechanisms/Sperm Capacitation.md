# Sperm Capacitation

**Function:** Prepares ejaculated sperm for fertilising interactions.

**Where:** Sperm membrane, ion channels and signalling machinery in reproductive-tract conditions.

## Mechanism

1. Membrane lipid remodelling and bicarbonate entry alter the sperm state.
2. Soluble adenylyl cyclase raises cAMP and activates PKA-associated programmes.
3. Calcium signalling, including CatSper-dependent entry, supports hyperactivated motility.
4. The sperm becomes competent for appropriate acrosomal and oocyte interactions.

## Control and constraints

Capacitation is distinct from epididymal maturation and from the acrosome reaction. Human CatSper regulation differs from some animal models; progesterone is an important human activator in the relevant context.
