# Neurulation and Neural Crest Migration

**Function:** Builds the neural tube and migratory neural-crest lineages.

**Where:** Embryonic ectoderm and surrounding tissues.

## Mechanism

1. Inductive signals specify neural plate identity.
2. Cell-shape changes and tissue forces bend/fuse neural folds in the relevant regions.
3. Neural-crest cells undergo regulated delamination and migration.
4. Regional cues guide differentiation into diverse peripheral neural and non-neural derivatives.

## Control and constraints

Neural-crest contribution is broader than peripheral neurons alone. Tube formation and closure vary along the axis, and development cannot be reduced to one universal fold sequence.
