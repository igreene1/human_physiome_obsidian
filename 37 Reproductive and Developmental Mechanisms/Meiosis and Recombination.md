# Meiosis and Recombination

**Function:** Creates haploid germ-cell genomes with recombined parental homologues.

**Where:** Developing germ-cell nuclei.

## Mechanism

1. Premeiotic S phase duplicates DNA.
2. SPO11-associated breaks initiate homology search, synapsis and recombination with meiosis-specific machinery.
3. Meiosis I separates homologous chromosomes while sister cohesion is regulated.
4. Meiosis II separates sister chromatids without another intervening S phase.

## Control and constraints

Crossovers support segregation as well as diversity. Human oocytes remain arrested for long periods, whereas male germ cells follow a different timing programme. Meiotic errors can alter chromosome number.
