# Fetal Circulation and Birth Transition

**Function:** Directs placental oxygen delivery prenatally and redirects flow after lung aeration.

**Where:** Placenta, fetal shunts, pulmonary vessels and heart.

## Mechanism

1. Placental blood enters through the umbilical vein, with ductus-venosus routing of a portion toward systemic venous return.
2. Foramen-ovale and ductus-arteriosus pathways redistribute flow around high-resistance fetal lungs.
3. Lung aeration lowers pulmonary resistance after birth while loss of placental circulation changes systemic loading.
4. Pressure/oxygen/prostaglandin changes promote functional and later structural closure of shunts.

## Control and constraints

Fetal streams mix incompletely and oxygenation differs by vessel. Functional closure timing differs among structures; transition is a coordinated haemodynamic process rather than simultaneous closure of every shunt.
