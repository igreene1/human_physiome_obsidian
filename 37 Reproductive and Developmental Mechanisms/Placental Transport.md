# Placental Transport

**Function:** Transfers gases, nutrients and selected macromolecules between maternal and fetal compartments.

**Where:** Syncytiotrophoblast, villous stroma and fetal capillary endothelium.

## Mechanism

1. Oxygen/CO2 diffuse along pressure gradients across the exchange interface.
2. GLUT-mediated routes transfer glucose, while multiple transport systems actively manage amino-acid supply.
3. FcRn-mediated pathways transfer selected maternal IgG, especially later in gestation.
4. Waste products move toward maternal elimination according to permeability and gradients.

## Control and constraints

Maternal and fetal blood normally remain in distinct circulations. Transport depends on surface area, thickness, blood flow, binding and metabolism; there is no universal placental yes/no barrier for all molecules.
