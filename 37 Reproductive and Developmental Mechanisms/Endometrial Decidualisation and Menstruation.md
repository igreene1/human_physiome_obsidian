# Endometrial Decidualisation and Menstruation

**Function:** Prepares uterine stroma for implantation and renews it after steroid withdrawal.

**Where:** Endometrial stromal, epithelial, vascular and immune cells.

## Mechanism

1. Estrogen supports proliferative growth; progesterone shifts the post-ovulatory tissue toward a secretory receptive state.
2. Stromal cells undergo decidual differentiation through progesterone/cAMP-linked programmes.
3. Without pregnancy maintenance, progesterone withdrawal recruits inflammatory and proteolytic changes.
4. Breakdown, haemostasis and rapid repair shed/rebuild functional tissue.

## Control and constraints

Human decidualisation can begin before implantation. Menstruation involves coordinated vascular and matrix changes; persistent or unusually heavy bleeding requires clinical reasoning beyond this normal-process map.
