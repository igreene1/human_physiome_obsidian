# Trophoblast Differentiation and Spiral Artery Remodelling

**Function:** Builds placental exchange and remodels maternal supply vessels.

**Where:** Villous cytotrophoblast, syncytiotrophoblast, extravillous trophoblast and decidua.

## Mechanism

1. Trophoblast lineages diverge into exchange-associated and invasive programmes.
2. Cell fusion creates syncytiotrophoblast covering villous surfaces.
3. Extravillous trophoblast interacts with decidual/immune cells and maternal vessels.
4. Spiral arteries are remodelled toward a lower-resistance supply to the placental interface.

## Control and constraints

Maternal immune cells participate in normal development rather than simply being globally suppressed. Invasion depth and timing require regulation; the placenta is not an unrestricted extension into maternal tissue.
