# Oocyte Meiotic Arrest and Resumption

**Function:** Times oocyte maturation with follicular signalling and ovulation.

**Where:** Oocyte and surrounding granulosa/cumulus cells.

## Mechanism

1. Cyclic-nucleotide signalling maintains high oocyte cAMP/PKA-associated restraint of meiotic progression.
2. Somatic-cell cGMP delivery restrains oocyte PDE3A and helps preserve cAMP.
3. LH-driven follicular changes reduce the arrest-supporting network.
4. CDK1-cyclin-B activation permits meiosis-I completion before a second arrest at metaphase II.

## Control and constraints

Oocyte and follicular somatic cells communicate through gap junctions and paracrine signals. The LH receptor signal acts substantially through follicular cells rather than a simple direct LH action on the oocyte.
