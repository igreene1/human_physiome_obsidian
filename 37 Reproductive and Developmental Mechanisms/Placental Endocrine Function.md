# Placental Endocrine Function

**Function:** Coordinates pregnancy support and maternal metabolic adaptation.

**Where:** Placental trophoblast and maternal/fetal endocrine partners.

## Mechanism

1. Early hCG stimulates LH/hCG receptors to maintain corpus-luteum steroid support.
2. Placental progesterone production becomes a major source as pregnancy progresses.
3. Estrogen production uses precursor cooperation with maternal/fetal tissues.
4. Placental lactogen and other signals influence maternal metabolism and mammary preparation.

## Control and constraints

Placental enzyme complement differs from adrenal/gonadal cells; it relies on external androgen precursors for much estrogen synthesis. Hormone roles vary by gestational stage.
