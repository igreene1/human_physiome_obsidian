# Sertoli Cell Support and Blood-Testis Barrier

**Function:** Coordinates germ-cell development in a protected seminiferous environment.

**Where:** Sertoli cells and their junctions.

## Mechanism

1. FSH/androgen-responsive programmes regulate support and local secretion.
2. Sertoli tight junctions divide basal and adluminal compartments.
3. Metabolic support, paracrine signals and phagocytosis assist developing germ cells.
4. Junctional remodelling permits germ-cell movement while maintaining barrier function.

## Control and constraints

The blood-testis barrier is mainly a Sertoli junctional structure, not simply an endothelial seal. Inhibin B feeds back on FSH-related regulation. High local androgen levels are important.
