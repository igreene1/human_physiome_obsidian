# Myometrial Contraction and Cervical Remodelling

**Function:** Coordinates force generation with a birth canal capable of dilation.

**Where:** Myometrial smooth muscle and cervical extracellular matrix.

## Mechanism

1. Near-term signalling increases excitability and intercellular coupling, including connexin-associated gap junctions.
2. Oxytocin/prostaglandin-related signals increase calcium and contractile activation.
3. Repetitive coordinated contractions act on a cervix undergoing collagen/proteoglycan and inflammatory remodelling.
4. Stretch and local signals can amplify the process until delivery changes the conditions.

## Control and constraints

Human labour initiation is multifactorial. Functional progesterone-related changes do not require a simple systemic progesterone concentration collapse. Cervical remodelling and uterine force are separate cooperating processes.
