# Ovulation and Corpus Luteum Function

**Function:** Releases an oocyte and establishes a temporary progesterone-producing gland.

**Where:** Dominant follicle and post-ovulatory luteal tissue.

## Mechanism

1. The LH surge recruits meiotic, inflammatory-like, vascular and matrix-remodelling programmes.
2. Follicular-wall changes permit oocyte-cumulus release.
3. Remaining granulosa/theca-derived cells luteinise and support progesterone/estradiol production.
4. Without sustained pregnancy rescue the corpus luteum regresses; hCG can maintain early pregnancy support.

## Control and constraints

Ovulation is not simply the follicle bursting from pressure. LH/hCG receptor signalling and angiogenesis sustain luteal function, with species-specific regulation of regression.
