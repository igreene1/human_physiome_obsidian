# Two-Cell Ovarian Steroidogenesis

**Function:** Cooperatively generates ovarian estradiol using distinct cell enzyme complements.

**Where:** Theca and granulosa cells of growing follicles.

## Mechanism

1. LH stimulates theca-cell steroidogenic activity, producing androgen precursors.
2. Androgens cross to neighbouring granulosa cells.
3. FSH promotes granulosa aromatase and related differentiation.
4. Aromatase converts suitable androgens to estrogens; later luteinisation changes steroid output.

## Control and constraints

Theca and granulosa cells are not biochemically interchangeable. Follicular stage changes receptor expression and enzyme capacity; the model summarises a cooperative network rather than all ovarian steroid chemistry.
