# Gastrulation and Germ Layers

**Function:** Establishes germ layers and major embryonic body axes.

**Where:** Epiblast and primitive-streak-associated structures.

## Mechanism

1. Spatial signalling establishes a primitive-streak region.
2. Cells change adhesion and migrate inward through regulated epithelial-mesenchymal transitions.
3. Definitive endoderm and mesoderm populations form while remaining epiblast contributes ectoderm.
4. Further patterning assigns axial and regional identities.

## Control and constraints

Germ layers are lineage frameworks, not complete organ maps; many organs combine derivatives from multiple layers. Human-specific details continue to be studied through embryos and models with different limitations.
