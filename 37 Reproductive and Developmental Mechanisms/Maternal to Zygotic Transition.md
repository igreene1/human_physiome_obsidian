# Maternal to Zygotic Transition

**Function:** Shifts control from stored oocyte products toward embryonic gene expression.

**Where:** Early cleavage embryos.

## Mechanism

1. Maternal RNAs/proteins initially support many cellular activities.
2. Regulated degradation and translational changes reduce or redistribute maternal stores.
3. Embryonic transcription increases in staged waves.
4. Chromatin and cell-cycle reprogramming establish new developmental control.

## Control and constraints

The major timing and mechanisms differ among species; mouse timing should not be directly substituted for human development. This is a programme of many genes, not one activation switch.
