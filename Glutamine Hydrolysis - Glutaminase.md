# Glutamine Hydrolysis - Glutaminase

**Part of:** [[Glutamate Glutamine Cycle]]

## Transformation

Glutamine + H2O → glutamate + ammonium

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | GLS or GLS2 |
| Compartment | Predominantly mitochondria, with tissue-specific expression |
| Physiological direction | Releases amide nitrogen and supplies glutamate carbon/nitrogen |

## What controls this step

Supports neuronal transmitter precursor recovery, renal ammoniagenesis and other tissue metabolism. Its systemic acid-base effect depends on where nitrogen is routed and excreted.
