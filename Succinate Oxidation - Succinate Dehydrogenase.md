# Succinate Oxidation - Succinate Dehydrogenase

**Part of:** [[TCA Cycle]]

## Transformation

Succinate + ubiquinone ⇌ fumarate + ubiquinol

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | SDHA, SDHB, SDHC and SDHD; enzyme-bound FAD and iron-sulfur centres |
| Compartment | Inner mitochondrial membrane, catalytic domain facing matrix |
| Physiological direction | Transfers electrons from succinate to the ubiquinone pool; also respiratory complex II |

## What controls this step

Complex II does not pump protons. Enzyme-bound FAD is an intermediate, not free FADH2 released into solution. Redox state and substrates determine net direction.
