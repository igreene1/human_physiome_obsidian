# Dihydroorotate Ring Closure

**Part of:** [[Pyrimidine and Thymidylate Synthesis]]

## Transformation

Carbamoyl aspartate ⇌ dihydroorotate + H2O

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | Dihydroorotase domain of CAD |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Forms the initial pyrimidine ring |

## What controls this step

The human de novo pathway next requires mitochondrial-associated oxidation. This ring precedes attachment to ribose phosphate, contrasting with purine ring construction.
