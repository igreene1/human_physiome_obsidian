# DOPA Decarboxylation - DDC

**Part of:** [[Catecholamine Synthesis and Clearance]]

## Transformation

L-DOPA → dopamine + CO2

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | DDC, aromatic L-amino-acid decarboxylase; pyridoxal phosphate |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Removes the amino-acid carboxyl group to produce an amine transmitter |

## What controls this step

The same enzyme also decarboxylates 5-hydroxytryptophan. Tissue product depends on precursor-generating enzymes and transport, not DDC identity alone.
