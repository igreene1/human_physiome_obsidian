# Glycogen Chain Extension - Glycogen Synthase

**Part of:** [[Glycogen Metabolism]]

## Transformation

UDP-glucose + glycogen(n) → UDP + glycogen(n+1), forming an alpha-1,4 bond

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | GYS1 in muscle/other tissues; GYS2 in liver; a pre-existing primer supports extension |
| Compartment | Cytosol at glycogen particles |
| Physiological direction | Adds glucose to non-reducing ends |

## What controls this step

Glucose 6-phosphate activates; phosphorylation generally inhibits. Insulin signalling promotes dephosphorylation and restrains GSK3. Initiation and branching are separate operations.
