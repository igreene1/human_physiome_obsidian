# Glucose Phosphate Interconversion - PGM1

**Part of:** [[Glycogen Metabolism]]

## Transformation

Glucose 6-phosphate ⇌ glucose 1-phosphate

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | PGM1; phosphoserine intermediate and Mg2+ |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Connects glycolytic hexose phosphate with glycogen synthesis and breakdown |

## What controls this step

A reversible reaction; direction follows whether glycogen is being stored or mobilised. A glucose 1,6-bisphosphate intermediate participates in phosphate transfer.
