# Cortisol Final Hydroxylation - CYP11B1

**Part of:** [[Steroid Hormone Synthesis]]

## Transformation

11-Deoxycortisol → cortisol by 11beta hydroxylation

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | CYP11B1 with mitochondrial electron-transfer partners |
| Compartment | Adrenal mitochondrial inner membrane |
| Physiological direction | Completes a major glucocorticoid-synthesis step |

## What controls this step

ACTH regulates substrate delivery and steroidogenic capacity. Distinct zona-glomerulosa CYP11B2 chemistry supports aldosterone formation.
