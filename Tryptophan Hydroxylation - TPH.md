# Tryptophan Hydroxylation - TPH

**Part of:** [[Serotonin]]

## Transformation

Tryptophan + BH4 + O2 → 5-hydroxytryptophan + oxidised pterin products + H2O

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | TPH1 or TPH2; iron and tetrahydrobiopterin |
| Compartment | Cytosol; isoenzyme distribution differs between peripheral and neural tissues |
| Physiological direction | First major regulated step in serotonin synthesis |

## What controls this step

TPH2 is prominent in central serotonergic neurons, while TPH1 has major peripheral roles. Precursor transport and cofactor recycling influence synthesis.
