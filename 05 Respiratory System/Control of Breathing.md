# Control of Breathing

**Function:** Adjusts ventilation to metabolic demand and acid-base conditions.

**Where:** Brainstem respiratory networks, chemoreceptors and respiratory muscles.

## Mechanism

1. Brainstem circuits generate and pattern respiratory motor output.
2. Central chemosensitive networks respond to CO2-related changes in local acid-base state.
3. Carotid bodies detect arterial hypoxaemia and respond to CO2/H+ changes.
4. Higher centres, lung afferents and exercise-related signals modify the pattern.

## Control and constraints

PaCO2 depends on CO2 production relative to alveolar ventilation. Peripheral oxygen drive becomes particularly strong at low PaO2; chemoreceptors do not directly measure total oxygen content in the same way as haemoglobin concentration.
