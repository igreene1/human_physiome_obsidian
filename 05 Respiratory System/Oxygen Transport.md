# Oxygen Transport

**Function:** Delivers oxygen from lungs to tissues for oxidative metabolism.

**Where:** Plasma, erythrocytes, circulation and tissue mitochondria.

## Mechanism

1. Oxygen diffuses into blood and binds reversibly to haemoglobin.
2. Cooperative binding creates a sigmoidal oxygen-dissociation curve.
3. Circulatory flow delivers oxygen content to organs; extraction lowers venous oxygen content.
4. Tissue diffusion supplies mitochondrial oxygen consumption.

## Control and constraints

Oxygen delivery equals cardiac output times arterial oxygen content. Haemoglobin concentration can fall while PaO2 remains normal. Temperature, pH, CO2 and 2,3-BPG shift affinity; oxygen saturation is not the same as total oxygen delivery.
