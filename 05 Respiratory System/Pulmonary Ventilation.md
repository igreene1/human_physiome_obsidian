# Pulmonary Ventilation

**Function:** Moves fresh gas to alveoli and removes exhaled gas.

**Where:** Respiratory muscles, thorax, conducting airways and alveoli.

## Mechanism

1. Inspiratory muscle contraction expands the thorax and lowers alveolar pressure relative to atmosphere.
2. Airflow follows the pressure gradient against airway resistance.
3. Elastic recoil usually supports quiet expiration.
4. Alveolar ventilation equals respiratory frequency times tidal volume minus physiological dead space per breath.

## Control and constraints

Minute ventilation and alveolar ventilation differ. Compliance, resistance, surfactant and chest-wall mechanics affect effort; rapid shallow breathing can waste a large fraction of ventilation in dead space.
