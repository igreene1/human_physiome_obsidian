# Ventilation Perfusion Matching

**Function:** Aligns alveolar gas supply with capillary blood flow.

**Where:** Heterogeneous lung units and pulmonary vessels.

## Mechanism

1. Regional mechanics and gravity distribute ventilation and perfusion unevenly.
2. Low alveolar oxygen triggers local pulmonary vasoconstriction that can divert flow toward better ventilated regions.
3. Blood leaving different units mixes, determining systemic arterial oxygenation.
4. Shunt-like units have perfusion with little ventilation; dead-space-like units have ventilation with little perfusion.

## Control and constraints

Hypoxic pulmonary vasoconstriction is opposite to the common systemic response to tissue hypoxia. Widespread alveolar hypoxia can increase overall pulmonary vascular resistance rather than improve distribution.
