# Alveolar Gas Exchange

**Function:** Transfers oxygen and carbon dioxide between alveolar gas and pulmonary capillary blood.

**Where:** Alveolar epithelium, interstitium and capillary endothelium.

## Mechanism

1. Ventilation replenishes alveolar oxygen and removes carbon dioxide.
2. Partial-pressure gradients drive dissolved gas across the diffusion barrier.
3. Haemoglobin binding maintains a gradient for oxygen uptake as blood passes the alveolus.
4. Perfusion carries loaded oxygen away and supplies carbon dioxide for elimination.

## Control and constraints

Flux depends on surface area, barrier thickness, diffusion properties and transit time. Oxygen content is distinct from oxygen partial pressure. Gas exchange also depends on matching ventilation to perfusion.
