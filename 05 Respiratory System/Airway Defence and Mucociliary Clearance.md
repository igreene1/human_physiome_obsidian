# Airway Defence and Mucociliary Clearance

**Function:** Removes inhaled particles and protects the respiratory surface.

**Where:** Ciliated epithelium, secretory cells, submucosal glands and alveolar immune cells.

## Mechanism

1. Mucus traps particles and microbes above a hydrated periciliary layer.
2. Coordinated ciliary beating transports mucus toward the pharynx.
3. Ion and water transport maintain suitable surface hydration.
4. Cough and innate immune responses supplement clearance; alveolar macrophages handle distal material.

## Control and constraints

CFTR and ENaC influence airway hydration; ciliary dynein requires ATP. Excess viscosity, impaired beating or epithelial damage disrupt clearance. Alveoli do not use the same mucociliary conveyor as conducting airways.
