# Nucleotide Excision Repair

**Function:** Removes bulky helix-distorting DNA lesions.

**Where:** Nuclear chromatin.

## Mechanism

1. Global-genome surveillance or stalled transcription identifies a lesion.
2. TFIIH-associated XPB/XPD activities help open and verify the repair region.
3. ERCC1-XPF and XPG make incisions on opposite sides of the lesion.
4. Polymerase fills the gap and ligase seals it.

## Control and constraints

Transcription-coupled and global-genome initiation converge on shared repair machinery. Humans use excision pathways for UV photoproducts rather than relying on photolyase-mediated direct photoreactivation.
