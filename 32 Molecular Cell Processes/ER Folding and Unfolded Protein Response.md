# ER Folding and Unfolded Protein Response

**Function:** Matches ER folding load to folding and disposal capacity.

**Where:** ER lumen and IRE1, PERK, ATF6-associated signalling.

## Mechanism

1. BiP and other chaperones assist folding while protein disulfide isomerases support oxidative folding.
2. ER stress recruits sensors through mechanisms involving luminal state and membrane conditions.
3. IRE1 processes XBP1 RNA, PERK alters translation through eIF2alpha, and ATF6 is cleaved after Golgi transport.
4. Adaptive gene expression increases folding/disposal capacity; severe persistent stress can recruit death programmes.

## Control and constraints

The three branches overlap but are not identical. UPR activation does not automatically mean apoptosis. ER calcium, lipid composition and secretory demand also influence stress.
