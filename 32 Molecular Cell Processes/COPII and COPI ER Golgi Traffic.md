# COPII and COPI ER Golgi Traffic

**Function:** Exports cargo from ER and retrieves selected components within the early secretory pathway.

**Where:** ER exit sites, ER-Golgi intermediates and Golgi.

## Mechanism

1. SAR1 activation recruits COPII coat components for ER export.
2. Cargo adaptors select proteins and carriers bud.
3. Rab/tether/SNARE systems support targeting and fusion.
4. ARF-associated COPI routes retrieve selected escaped ER proteins and recycle machinery.

## Control and constraints

COPII is associated with forward ER export; COPI has major retrograde/intra-Golgi roles. These labels simplify a network with varying carrier sizes and mechanisms.
