# Ferroptosis Control

**Function:** Prevents or permits iron-dependent accumulation of lethal phospholipid peroxides.

**Where:** Cell membranes and antioxidant/metabolic networks.

## Mechanism

1. Polyunsaturated phospholipids provide oxidisable substrates.
2. Iron-dependent chemistry and enzyme systems can initiate/amplify lipid peroxidation.
3. GPX4 uses glutathione to reduce phospholipid hydroperoxides; parallel protective systems also contribute.
4. Failure of sufficient protection can culminate in membrane damage and death.

## Control and constraints

Ferroptosis is not defined simply by elevated total iron or general ROS. Cystine uptake, glutathione synthesis, lipid composition and iron handling jointly influence susceptibility.
