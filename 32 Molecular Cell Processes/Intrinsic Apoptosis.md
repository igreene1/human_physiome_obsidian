# Intrinsic Apoptosis

**Function:** Executes cell death through mitochondrial permeabilisation.

**Where:** BCL2-family networks, mitochondria and cytosolic caspases.

## Mechanism

1. Cell stress changes BH3-only protein and anti-apoptotic protein balance.
2. BAX/BAK oligomerisation permits mitochondrial outer-membrane permeabilisation.
3. Released cytochrome c supports APAF1 apoptosome assembly and caspase-9 activation.
4. Executioner caspases cleave substrates and produce a cell suitable for clearance.

## Control and constraints

Mitochondrial permeabilisation is a key commitment event in many contexts. Anti-apoptotic BCL2-family proteins restrain it; ATP/dATP-dependent apoptosome assembly is distinct from respiratory ATP production.
