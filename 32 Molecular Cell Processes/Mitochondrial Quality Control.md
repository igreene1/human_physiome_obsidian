# Mitochondrial Quality Control

**Function:** Matches organelle maintenance to protein, genome and membrane damage.

**Where:** Mitochondrial proteases, dynamics machinery and lysosomal pathways.

## Mechanism

1. Chaperones/proteases manage defective mitochondrial proteins.
2. MFN1/2 and OPA1 support fusion, while DRP1-associated machinery supports fission.
3. Dynamics can segregate damaged regions and mix functional components.
4. Selective removal and new biogenesis adjust mitochondrial populations.

## Control and constraints

Fission is not always damage and fusion is not always beneficial; both have physiological roles. Quality control includes local repair before whole-organelle removal.
