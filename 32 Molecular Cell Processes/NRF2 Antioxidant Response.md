# NRF2 Antioxidant Response

**Function:** Induces adaptive gene expression during electrophilic or oxidative challenge.

**Where:** KEAP1-CUL3 system and nuclear NRF2 complexes.

## Mechanism

1. Under basal conditions KEAP1-associated ubiquitination promotes NRF2 turnover.
2. Electrophilic/oxidative changes in the sensor system reduce NRF2 degradation.
3. NRF2 accumulates and binds regulatory DNA with partner proteins.
4. Genes supporting glutathione, detoxification and redox maintenance increase expression.

## Control and constraints

This is a transcriptional adaptation, slower than direct peroxide removal. Persistent NRF2 activity can have different effects in normal tissue and transformed cells; more activity is not universally better.
