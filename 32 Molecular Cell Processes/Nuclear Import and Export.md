# Nuclear Import and Export

**Function:** Selectively exchanges proteins and RNAs across the nuclear envelope.

**Where:** Nuclear pore complexes and soluble transport receptors.

## Mechanism

1. Importins recognise appropriate cargo localisation signals.
2. Cargo-receptor complexes cross the selective pore barrier.
3. Nuclear Ran-GTP promotes release for many import routes; exportins assemble cargo with Ran-GTP for export.
4. Cytosolic GTP hydrolysis and nuclear exchange factors maintain directionality.

## Control and constraints

Small molecules can diffuse more freely, but large cargo commonly requires regulated transport. RNA export includes pathways beyond the canonical importin/exportin model.
