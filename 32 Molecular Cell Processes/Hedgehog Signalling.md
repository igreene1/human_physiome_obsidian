# Hedgehog Signalling

**Function:** Converts Hedgehog ligand distribution into context-dependent gene regulation.

**Where:** Primary-cilium-associated machinery in many vertebrate cells.

## Mechanism

1. Without ligand, PTCH restrains Smoothened-associated signalling and GLI processing favours repression.
2. Ligand binding relieves PTCH-mediated restraint.
3. Smoothened/ciliary trafficking changes GLI regulation.
4. GLI activator/repressor balance changes target-gene expression.

## Control and constraints

Ciliary localisation is central to many vertebrate Hedgehog processes. Pathway effects vary by developmental region and timing; a gradient produces different outcomes through thresholds and cellular competence.
