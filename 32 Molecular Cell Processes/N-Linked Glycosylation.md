# N-Linked Glycosylation

**Function:** Adds and remodels glycans on selected asparagine residues.

**Where:** ER membrane/lumen and Golgi.

## Mechanism

1. A dolichol-linked precursor is assembled across ER membrane compartments.
2. Oligosaccharyltransferase transfers the glycan to suitable Asn-X-Ser/Thr motifs, usually excluding proline at X.
3. Glucose trimming and calnexin/calreticulin cycles support folding surveillance.
4. Golgi enzymes remodel glycans into diverse structures.

## Control and constraints

A sequence motif is necessary for many sites but does not guarantee occupancy. N-linked glycosylation differs from several O-linked routes. Glycans affect folding, trafficking and recognition, not only protein mass.
