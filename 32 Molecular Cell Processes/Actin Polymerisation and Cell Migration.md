# Actin Polymerisation and Cell Migration

**Function:** Creates protrusion and contractile forces for directed cell movement.

**Where:** Cell cortex, leading edge and adhesion sites.

## Mechanism

1. Rac/Cdc42/Rho-family signalling organises polarity and actin regulators.
2. Arp2/3 nucleates branched networks while formins promote selected linear filaments.
3. New adhesions couple actin to extracellular substrate.
4. Myosin II contraction, rear release and filament recycling advance the cell.

## Control and constraints

Actin monomer addition and motor contraction are different sources of force. Chemotaxis requires spatial bias in these processes, not merely faster movement.
