# Nonsense-Mediated mRNA Decay

**Function:** Removes many transcripts whose translation termination context suggests a premature stop.

**Where:** Translation-associated cytosolic RNA surveillance.

## Mechanism

1. A ribosome encounters a stop codon in an unfavourable messenger-ribonucleoprotein context.
2. UPF factors and associated signalling assemble the surveillance response.
3. Endonucleolytic cleavage or decapping/deadenylation pathways initiate degradation.
4. Decay machinery removes the transcript and recycles components.

## Control and constraints

Exon-junction complexes are important in many cases, but NMD is not governed by a single absolute distance rule for all transcripts. It also regulates some normal RNAs.
