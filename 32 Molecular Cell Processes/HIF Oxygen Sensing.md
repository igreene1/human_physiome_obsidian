# HIF Oxygen Sensing

**Function:** Links oxygen availability to transcriptional adaptation.

**Where:** Cytosolic oxygen-dependent enzymes and nuclear HIF complexes.

## Mechanism

1. With adequate oxygen, prolyl hydroxylases modify HIF-alpha using oxygen, iron and 2-oxoglutarate.
2. VHL-associated ubiquitination promotes HIF-alpha degradation.
3. Lower oxygen reduces hydroxylation, permitting HIF-alpha accumulation and dimerisation with ARNT.
4. Target genes alter erythropoiesis, angiogenesis and metabolism according to cell context.

## Control and constraints

Different HIF-alpha isoforms and tissues have different targets; renal EPO regulation prominently involves HIF-2alpha. Oxygen tension is sensed through enzyme chemistry rather than a single oxygen-binding receptor.
