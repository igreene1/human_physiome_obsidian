# Chromatin Accessibility and Histone Modification

**Function:** Controls DNA packaging and access to regulatory or repair machinery.

**Where:** Nucleosomes and nuclear chromatin.

## Mechanism

1. ATP-dependent remodellers reposition, exchange or remove nucleosomes.
2. Histone-modifying enzymes add or remove acetyl, methyl and other groups.
3. Reader proteins recognise combinations of marks and recruit further machinery.
4. DNA methylation and chromatin-loop interactions contribute additional regulation.

## Control and constraints

A histone mark is context-dependent; methylation is not uniformly repressive. Epigenetic states interact with DNA sequence, transcription factors and cell history rather than acting as a separate universal code.
