# Cytokinesis

**Function:** Partitions cytoplasm after nuclear division.

**Where:** Cell cortex and intercellular bridge.

## Mechanism

1. Central-spindle signals spatially activate RhoA at the equatorial cortex.
2. Actin and myosin II assemble a contractile ring.
3. Furrow ingression narrows the intercellular bridge.
4. ESCRT-associated machinery contributes to final membrane abscission.

## Control and constraints

Timing is coordinated with chromosome clearance to avoid cutting trapped chromatin. Some physiological cell types become multinucleated or polyploid through modified division programmes.
