# Molecular Circadian Clock

**Function:** Generates an approximately daily intracellular oscillation.

**Where:** Nuclear-cytosolic transcriptional feedback loops.

## Mechanism

1. CLOCK or NPAS2 with BMAL1 activates genes including PER and CRY.
2. PER/CRY proteins accumulate, are modified and enter repressive complexes.
3. These complexes inhibit transcription driven by the activating complex.
4. Timed degradation and auxiliary loops involving REV-ERB/ROR permit another cycle.

## Control and constraints

Period emerges from synthesis, modification, trafficking and degradation delays. Light entrains specialised neural clocks; peripheral clocks also respond to feeding and systemic cues.
