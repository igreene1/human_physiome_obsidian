# RNA Polymerase II Initiation and Elongation

**Function:** Synthesises protein-coding and many regulatory transcripts.

**Where:** Nuclear promoters and gene bodies.

## Mechanism

1. Sequence-specific factors, Mediator and general factors help recruit polymerase II.
2. TFIIH activities support promoter opening and polymerase CTD phosphorylation.
3. Promoter-proximal pausing and regulated pause release control productive elongation.
4. CTD-associated factors couple elongation to capping, splicing and 3-prime processing.

## Control and constraints

Enhancers can act through three-dimensional contacts. RNA abundance reflects synthesis and turnover; transcription is often burst-like rather than a constant rate per gene.
