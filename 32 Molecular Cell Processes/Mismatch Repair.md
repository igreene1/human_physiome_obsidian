# Mismatch Repair

**Function:** Corrects selected replication mismatches and insertion-deletion loops.

**Where:** Newly replicated nuclear DNA.

## Mechanism

1. MSH2-MSH6 or MSH2-MSH3 complexes recognise mismatch structures.
2. MLH1-PMS2 and associated factors coordinate strand-directed incision and excision.
3. The erroneous newly synthesised segment is removed.
4. DNA synthesis and ligation restore the sequence using the parental template.

## Control and constraints

Strand discrimination in human cells uses replication-associated cues rather than the bacterial methylation scheme often shown in older diagrams. Failure increases mutation accumulation and microsatellite instability.
