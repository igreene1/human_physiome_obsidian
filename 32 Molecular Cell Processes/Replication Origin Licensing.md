# Replication Origin Licensing

**Function:** Marks DNA replication origins so genome duplication can occur once per cycle.

**Where:** Nuclear chromatin in late mitosis/G1.

## Mechanism

1. ORC recruits CDC6 and CDT1 to candidate origin regions.
2. MCM2-7 helicase double hexamers are loaded around DNA.
3. S-phase CDK and DDK activities promote activation with CDC45/GINS to form the CMG helicase.
4. High CDK activity, geminin and protein turnover restrain new licensing after S phase begins.

## Control and constraints

Not every licensed origin fires; dormant origins provide reserve capacity under replication stress. Licensing and firing are separate molecular events.
