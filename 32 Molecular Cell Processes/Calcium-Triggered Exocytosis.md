# Calcium-Triggered Exocytosis

**Function:** Rapidly releases stored cargo in response to local calcium elevation.

**Where:** Neuronal, endocrine and other regulated secretory terminals.

## Mechanism

1. Granules/vesicles are docked and primed with SNARE-associated machinery.
2. Electrical or receptor signals raise local cytosolic Ca2+.
3. Calcium-binding sensors such as synaptotagmins promote fusion of release-ready vesicles.
4. Endocytosis and replenishment rebuild the releasable pool.

## Control and constraints

Calcium nanodomains near channels can trigger release without a uniformly high whole-cell calcium concentration. Sensor isoforms and coupling distances shape speed and release probability.
