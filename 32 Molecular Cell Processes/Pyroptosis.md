# Pyroptosis

**Function:** Links selected innate immune sensing to gasdermin-mediated membrane permeabilisation.

**Where:** Innate-sensing cells and other susceptible cells.

## Mechanism

1. Inflammasome assembly can activate caspase-1, while human caspase-4/5 detect cytosolic LPS in noncanonical pathways.
2. Caspases cleave gasdermin D to release its pore-forming domain.
3. Membrane pores permit ion flux and inflammatory mediator release.
4. Further membrane rupture can release additional contents.

## Control and constraints

Caspase-1 also processes pro-IL-1beta and pro-IL-18. Pore formation and final lysis are related but separable; not every inflammasome response immediately kills the cell.
