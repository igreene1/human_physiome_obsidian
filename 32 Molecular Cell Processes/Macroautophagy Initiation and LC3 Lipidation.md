# Macroautophagy Initiation and LC3 Lipidation

**Function:** Builds double-membrane carriers that deliver cytoplasmic material to lysosomes.

**Where:** ER-associated and other membrane initiation sites.

## Mechanism

1. Energy/nutrient signalling regulates the ULK initiation complex.
2. Class III PI3K complexes generate PI3P and recruit phagophore-associated factors.
3. ATG conjugation systems attach LC3/GABARAP proteins to membrane lipids and support expansion/cargo capture.
4. Closure and fusion deliver the autophagosome to lysosomal degradation.

## Control and constraints

mTORC1 generally restrains initiation when nutrients are abundant; AMPK can promote it under energy stress. LC3 puncta alone cannot distinguish increased formation from blocked clearance.
