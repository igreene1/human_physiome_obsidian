# Epithelial Junction Assembly

**Function:** Establishes cohesive, polarised sheets with selective paracellular permeability.

**Where:** Lateral/apical epithelial membranes.

## Mechanism

1. Cadherin-catenin complexes establish actin-linked adherens contacts.
2. Claudins, occludin-associated proteins and scaffolds organise tight junctions.
3. Desmosomes link intermediate-filament networks between cells.
4. Polarity complexes coordinate apical and basolateral domains.

## Control and constraints

Claudin composition can create selective paracellular ion pathways; a tight junction is not always an absolute seal. Different epithelia vary greatly in leakiness and electrical properties.
