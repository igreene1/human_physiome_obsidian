# SNARE Membrane Fusion

**Function:** Brings selected lipid bilayers into a fusion-competent configuration.

**Where:** Secretory, endosomal and organelle membranes.

## Mechanism

1. Rab/tether systems position a carrier near its target.
2. Complementary SNAREs assemble across opposing membranes.
3. SNARE zippering draws membranes together and supports pore formation.
4. NSF and alpha-SNAP use ATP to disassemble post-fusion SNARE complexes.

## Control and constraints

Fusion specificity also relies on tethers, lipids and regulators. SNARE assembly provides force, while the ATP expenditure for recycling occurs in a distinct step.
