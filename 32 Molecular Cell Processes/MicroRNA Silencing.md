# MicroRNA Silencing

**Function:** Reduces expression of selected target mRNAs through sequence-guided regulation.

**Where:** Nucleus and cytosol.

## Mechanism

1. Drosha-DGCR8 processes many primary microRNAs in the nucleus.
2. Export and Dicer processing produce a short duplex.
3. Argonaute loads one strand to guide target recognition.
4. Recruitment of repression/deadenylation machinery reduces translation and promotes decay; extensive complementarity can permit cleavage in appropriate contexts.

## Control and constraints

Canonical processing has exceptions. Animal microRNAs often recognise short seed matches, so each can influence many transcripts. Association alone does not establish a physiologically meaningful target.
