# Nonhomologous End Joining

**Function:** Reconnects broken DNA ends without requiring long homologous templates.

**Where:** Nuclear double-strand breaks, including programmed lymphocyte breaks.

## Mechanism

1. Ku70/Ku80 bind DNA ends and recruit DNA-PKcs.
2. End bridging and regulated processing prepare compatible ends.
3. XRCC4/XLF-associated ligase IV seals the break.
4. End-joining factors are released after repair.

## Control and constraints

Classical NHEJ can be precise for compatible ends or mutagenic when processing is necessary. It should not be described as invariably error-prone. Alternative end joining uses different machinery and often microhomology.
