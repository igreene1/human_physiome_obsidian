# tRNA Charging and Translational Fidelity

**Function:** Attaches the appropriate amino acid to its cognate tRNA.

**Where:** Cytosol and mitochondria with distinct synthetase sets.

## Mechanism

1. An aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase activates an amino acid using ATP, forming aminoacyl-AMP.
2. The aminoacyl group is transferred to the tRNA 3-prime end.
3. Editing sites in selected synthetases remove incorrectly activated or attached substrates.
4. Charged tRNA enters translation through elongation-factor delivery.

## Control and constraints

Charging spends ATP to AMP, equivalent to two high-energy phosphate bonds. Codon-anticodon pairing alone cannot detect every incorrectly charged tRNA, making synthetase specificity essential.
