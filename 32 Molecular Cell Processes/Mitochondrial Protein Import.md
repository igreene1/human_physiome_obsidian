# Mitochondrial Protein Import

**Function:** Places nuclear-encoded proteins into the correct mitochondrial compartment.

**Where:** TOM/TIM and additional mitochondrial translocases.

## Mechanism

1. Cytosolic chaperones maintain import-competent precursors.
2. Outer-membrane receptors/translocases recognise targeting information.
3. Inner-membrane pathways use membrane potential and ATP-dependent motors where appropriate.
4. Sorting, processing and assembly place proteins in matrix, membranes or intermembrane space.

## Control and constraints

Different precursor classes use different routes; not all have cleavable N-terminal presequences. Most mitochondrial proteins are nuclear encoded, while mitochondria encode selected respiratory components locally.
