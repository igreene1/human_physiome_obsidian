# Telomere Maintenance

**Function:** Protects chromosome ends from being treated as ordinary DNA breaks.

**Where:** Chromosome termini.

## Mechanism

1. Telomeric repeats and shelterin proteins organise a protected end structure.
2. Replication and end processing recreate an appropriate overhang.
3. Telomerase, where active, extends the G-rich strand using its RNA template.
4. Complementary-strand synthesis and capping restore end integrity.

## Control and constraints

Telomerase activity varies greatly by cell type. Short or uncapped telomeres can trigger damage responses, but telomere length is not a complete measure or sole cause of organismal aging.
