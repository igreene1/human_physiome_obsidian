# ER-Associated Degradation

**Function:** Removes selected persistent ER protein-folding defects.

**Where:** ER membrane and cytosolic proteasome system.

## Mechanism

1. Quality-control factors recognise and retain a defective substrate.
2. E3 ligase complexes ubiquitinate appropriate substrates while retrotranslocation machinery exposes them to cytosol.
3. VCP/p97-associated extraction uses ATP to remove membrane/luminal cargo.
4. Proteasomes degrade the unfolded substrate and ubiquitin is recycled.

## Control and constraints

Substrate classes use partly different complexes. ERAD clears selected proteins, whereas large aggregates or organelle regions may require lysosomal routes. Failure can raise ER stress.
