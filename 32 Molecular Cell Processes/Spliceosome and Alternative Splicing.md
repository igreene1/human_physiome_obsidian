# Spliceosome and Alternative Splicing

**Function:** Removes introns and combines exons in regulated transcript isoforms.

**Where:** Nuclear pre-mRNA, often during transcription.

## Mechanism

1. snRNPs and accessory proteins recognise splice sites and a branch-point region.
2. Spliceosome rearrangements position RNA for the first transesterification and lariat formation.
3. The second transesterification joins exons and releases the intron lariat.
4. Regulatory RNA-binding proteins influence alternative site usage.

## Control and constraints

The spliceosome uses ATP for assembly/rearrangement, while bond exchange is transesterification. Alternative splicing can alter coding sequence, localisation or decay susceptibility; not every isoform is functional.
