# Clathrin-Mediated Endocytosis

**Function:** Internalises selected membrane cargo for sorting or signalling.

**Where:** Plasma membrane and endosomes.

## Mechanism

1. Cargo motifs and phosphoinositides recruit AP2 and other adaptors.
2. Clathrin assembly and accessory proteins organise a coated pit.
3. Dynamin-dependent membrane scission releases many canonical vesicles.
4. Uncoating and fusion deliver cargo to endosomes for recycling or degradation.

## Control and constraints

Internalisation need not terminate signalling immediately; endosomes can signal. Cargo selection and recycling determine net surface receptor abundance.
