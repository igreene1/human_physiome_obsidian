# Cell Cycle Checkpoints

**Function:** Delays transitions when DNA or chromosome attachment is not ready.

**Where:** Nucleus, kinetochores and cyclin-CDK control systems.

## Mechanism

1. ATM/ATR-associated signalling detects DNA damage or replication stress.
2. CHK1/CHK2, p53/p21 and other pathways restrain cell-cycle drivers.
3. The spindle-assembly checkpoint inhibits APC/C activation until attachment conditions are satisfied.
4. Repair or corrected attachment permits release; unresolved stress can recruit senescence or death.

## Control and constraints

Checkpoint activation is a signalling response, not repair itself. Different checkpoints operate at different phases. Some damaged cells escape control, so a checkpoint is not an absolute guarantee of genomic integrity.
