# Extracellular Matrix Turnover

**Function:** Remodels matrix to match tissue growth, loading and repair.

**Where:** Extracellular space and cell-surface proteolytic systems.

## Mechanism

1. Cells synthesise collagens, proteoglycans and adhesive glycoproteins.
2. MMPs, ADAMTS enzymes and other proteases cleave selected matrix substrates.
3. TIMPs and spatial activation constrain protease effects.
4. Released fragments and altered mechanics feed back on cells.

## Control and constraints

Turnover is substrate-specific and can release stored growth factors. Net fibrosis reflects production, degradation, cell activation and mechanics rather than collagen synthesis alone.
