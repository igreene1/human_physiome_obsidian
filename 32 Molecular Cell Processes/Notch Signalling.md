# Notch Signalling

**Function:** Uses contact-dependent communication to influence cell fate and tissue organisation.

**Where:** Adjacent ligand-presenting and receptor-bearing cells.

## Mechanism

1. Delta/Jagged-family ligand on one cell engages a Notch receptor on another.
2. Mechanical and proteolytic events expose and cleave the receptor.
3. Gamma-secretase releases the Notch intracellular domain.
4. The domain enters the nucleus and works with RBPJ/coactivators to change gene expression.

## Control and constraints

Ligand/receptor interactions in the same cell can alter signalling. Output depends on context, dose and timing; Notch is reused in development, renewal and immune differentiation.
