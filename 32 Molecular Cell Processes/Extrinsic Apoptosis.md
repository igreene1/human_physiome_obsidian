# Extrinsic Apoptosis

**Function:** Couples selected death-receptor signals to caspase-dependent death.

**Where:** Plasma-membrane receptor complexes and cytosol.

## Mechanism

1. FAS or related receptor activation recruits appropriate adaptor complexes.
2. FADD-associated assembly activates initiator caspase-8 in canonical death-inducing complexes.
3. Caspase-8 directly activates executioner caspases or cleaves BID to amplify the mitochondrial pathway.
4. Regulatory proteins and competing signalling complexes set the outcome.

## Control and constraints

TNF-family signalling can also promote survival and inflammation; receptor binding is not automatically a death instruction. Caspase-8 additionally restrains some necroptotic programmes.
