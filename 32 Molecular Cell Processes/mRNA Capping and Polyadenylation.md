# mRNA Capping and Polyadenylation

**Function:** Protects and prepares most mRNAs for export and translation.

**Where:** Nascent nuclear RNA.

## Mechanism

1. A triphosphatase/guanylyltransferase system forms the 5-prime cap, followed by methylation.
2. Capping factors couple to the polymerase-II transcription machinery.
3. Cleavage/polyadenylation complexes recognise 3-prime processing signals.
4. Poly(A) polymerase adds the tail and binding proteins organise the mature end.

## Control and constraints

Replication-dependent histone mRNAs are a notable non-polyadenylated exception. Tail length is regulated over a transcript's lifetime. A cap/tail improves competence but does not guarantee translation.
