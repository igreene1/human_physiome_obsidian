# Lysosomal Acidification and Digestion

**Function:** Creates an acidic degradative compartment and recycles its products.

**Where:** Lysosomal limiting membrane and lumen.

## Mechanism

1. V-ATPase hydrolyses ATP to pump protons into the lumen.
2. Counterion conductance and ion exchange support electroneutrality and osmotic stability.
3. Acid-active hydrolases digest proteins, lipids, nucleic acids and glycans.
4. Specific transporters return products to the cytosol.

## Control and constraints

Hydrolase delivery includes mannose-6-phosphate-dependent sorting for many enzymes, with exceptions. Membrane integrity is essential; low pH alone does not determine all degradative capacity.
