# Store-Operated Calcium Entry

**Function:** Replenishes ER calcium and sustains selected cellular signals.

**Where:** ER-plasma membrane contact sites.

## Mechanism

1. Receptor signalling or other events lower ER luminal Ca2+.
2. STIM proteins sense reduced luminal Ca2+ and cluster at membrane contacts.
3. STIM activates ORAI calcium channels in the plasma membrane.
4. Incoming Ca2+ supports signalling and SERCA-mediated ER refilling.

## Control and constraints

Store depletion differs from direct voltage gating. Local calcium feedback and store refilling limit the response; persistent calcium elevation can be damaging.
