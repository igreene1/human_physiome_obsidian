# Receptor Desensitisation and Recycling

**Function:** Adjusts responsiveness during sustained or repeated stimulation.

**Where:** Plasma membrane, endosomes and lysosomes.

## Mechanism

1. Activated GPCRs can be phosphorylated by GRKs and recruit beta-arrestins.
2. Arrestin can reduce G-protein coupling and recruit endocytic machinery.
3. Endosomal sorting returns receptors to the surface or directs degradation.
4. Phosphatases and new synthesis restore or reshape sensitivity.

## Control and constraints

Internalisation is not universally equivalent to complete signal termination. Different receptor classes use different downregulation routes; beta-arrestins can also scaffold signalling.
