# Ubiquitin Proteasome System

**Function:** Selectively marks and degrades proteins or regulates their interactions.

**Where:** Cytosol, nucleus and membrane-associated signalling complexes.

## Mechanism

1. E1 activates ubiquitin using ATP and transfers it to an E2 enzyme.
2. E3 ligases confer much substrate specificity and build ubiquitin modifications.
3. Appropriate chains recruit the 26S proteasome, which unfolds and feeds substrates into its proteolytic core.
4. Deubiquitinases remove/recycle ubiquitin and edit signalling chains.

## Control and constraints

Not every ubiquitin mark means destruction: linkage type and architecture can recruit signalling machinery. Proteasomes degrade proteins rather than entire organelles.
