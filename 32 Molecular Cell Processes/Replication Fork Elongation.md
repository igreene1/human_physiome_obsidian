# Replication Fork Elongation

**Function:** Copies leading and lagging strands while preserving fork stability.

**Where:** Nuclear replication forks.

## Mechanism

1. CMG helicase unwinds parental DNA and RPA protects single strands.
2. Polymerase alpha-primase creates RNA-DNA primers; polymerases epsilon/delta perform most nuclear elongation.
3. PCNA and RFC support processivity; lagging-strand fragments undergo primer removal and ligation.
4. Proofreading and fork-protection mechanisms limit errors and collapse.

## Control and constraints

Synthesis is always 5-prime to 3-prime. Leading/lagging assignments are useful canonical descriptions, with flexibility during repair and stress. Topoisomerases relieve torsional stress ahead of forks.
