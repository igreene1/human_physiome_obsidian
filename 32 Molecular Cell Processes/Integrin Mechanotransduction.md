# Integrin Mechanotransduction

**Function:** Converts matrix binding and force into intracellular signalling and structural change.

**Where:** Cell-matrix adhesions.

## Mechanism

1. Integrins bind extracellular ligands and intracellular talin/kindlin-associated machinery.
2. Force exposes or stabilises binding sites and recruits adhesion proteins such as vinculin.
3. FAK/Src and Rho-family pathways alter cytoskeleton and cell behaviour.
4. Mechanically responsive transcriptional regulators can change growth and differentiation.

## Control and constraints

Ligand identity, stiffness and force history affect output. Integrins transmit bidirectional signalling; adhesion strength is not determined by receptor abundance alone.
