# SRP ER Protein Targeting

**Function:** Routes many secreted and membrane proteins into the ER during synthesis.

**Where:** Ribosome, signal-recognition particle and ER translocon.

## Mechanism

1. An emerging hydrophobic signal is recognised by SRP.
2. SRP targets the ribosome-nascent chain to its ER receptor.
3. Transfer to the SEC61 channel permits co-translational translocation or membrane insertion.
4. Signal cleavage and topology determinants establish luminal, cytosolic and membrane domains.

## Control and constraints

Not all ER proteins use one identical signal sequence or route; post-translational pathways also exist. Free and ER-bound ribosomes are not permanently different ribosome species.
