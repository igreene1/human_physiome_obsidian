# Microtubule Dynamics and Motor Transport

**Function:** Organises long-range transport, cell polarity and the spindle.

**Where:** Tubulin polymers, organising centres and motor complexes.

## Mechanism

1. GTP-tubulin adds to microtubule ends.
2. GTP hydrolysis and cap stability create dynamic instability with growth and shrinkage.
3. Kinesins and dynein carry cargo in directionally biased movements along polar tracks.
4. Microtubule-associated proteins tune stability and access.

## Control and constraints

Most cytoplasmic dynein moves toward minus ends; many but not all kinesins move toward plus ends. Microtubules are dynamic structures rather than permanent rigid tracks.
