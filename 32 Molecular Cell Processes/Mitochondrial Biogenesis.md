# Mitochondrial Biogenesis

**Function:** Increases and renews mitochondrial machinery to match demand.

**Where:** Nuclear transcription, mitochondrial genomes and protein-import systems.

## Mechanism

1. Energy, calcium and exercise signals alter transcriptional coactivators including PGC-1alpha.
2. Nuclear programmes increase oxidative proteins and regulatory factors.
3. TFAM-associated processes support mitochondrial genome maintenance/expression.
4. Protein import, membrane synthesis and assembly coordinate new capacity.

## Control and constraints

Biogenesis is coordinated expansion/remodelling of existing organelles, not spontaneous assembly from nothing. Increased marker expression alone does not establish increased functional respiratory capacity.
