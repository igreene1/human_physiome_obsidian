# Translation Initiation and Elongation

**Function:** Positions ribosomes and drives codon-by-codon peptide growth.

**Where:** Cytosolic and ER-associated ribosomes.

## Mechanism

1. eIF complexes recruit the small subunit and initiator tRNA, often through cap-dependent scanning.
2. Start-codon recognition permits large-subunit joining.
3. eEF1A delivers aminoacyl-tRNAs; the ribosomal peptidyl-transferase centre forms peptide bonds.
4. eEF2-driven translocation moves the ribosome; release factors terminate at stop codons.

## Control and constraints

mTORC1 and eIF2 phosphorylation regulate distinct initiation steps. The ribosome's catalytic centre is RNA-based. Some transcripts use alternative initiation mechanisms.
