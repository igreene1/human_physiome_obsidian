# Mitotic Spindle and Chromosome Segregation

**Function:** Distributes duplicated chromosomes to daughter cells.

**Where:** Centrosomes, spindle microtubules, kinetochores and chromosomes.

## Mechanism

1. Microtubules capture kinetochores and establish bipolar chromosome attachment.
2. Aurora and checkpoint pathways correct or restrain improper attachments.
3. APC/C-mediated securin degradation frees separase to cleave cohesin.
4. Anaphase movements separate chromatids and cyclin-B destruction promotes mitotic exit.

## Control and constraints

Attachment geometry and tension contribute to fidelity. Motor proteins and microtubule dynamics work together; chromosomes are not simply pulled by shortening ropes without regulation.
