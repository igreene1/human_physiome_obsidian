# Necroptosis

**Function:** Produces regulated membrane-disruptive death in selected signalling contexts.

**Where:** RIPK-family signalling complexes and plasma membrane.

## Mechanism

1. Appropriate receptor/stress signals occur in a context permissive for necroptosis.
2. RIPK3 activation promotes MLKL phosphorylation.
3. Activated MLKL oligomerises and associates with membranes.
4. Membrane disruption releases intracellular contents and can recruit inflammation.

## Control and constraints

RIPK1 involvement is context-dependent. Caspase-8 often restrains this pathway. Necroptosis is mechanistically distinct from accidental necrosis and from gasdermin-mediated pyroptosis.
