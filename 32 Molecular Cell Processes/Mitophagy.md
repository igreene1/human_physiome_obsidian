# Mitophagy

**Function:** Selectively removes mitochondria or mitochondrial portions through autophagy.

**Where:** Mitochondrial surfaces, autophagy receptors and lysosomes.

## Mechanism

1. Loss of import competence in a canonical damage pathway stabilises PINK1 on the outer membrane.
2. PINK1-dependent phosphorylation can recruit/activate Parkin and ubiquitin-based cargo tagging.
3. Autophagy receptors connect the tagged region to LC3/GABARAP-associated membranes.
4. Lysosomal delivery dismantles the selected material.

## Control and constraints

PINK1-Parkin is an important model, not the only human mitophagy route. Receptor-mediated pathways involving proteins such as BNIP3/NIX operate in specific physiological contexts.
