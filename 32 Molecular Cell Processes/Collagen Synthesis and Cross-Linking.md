# Collagen Synthesis and Cross-Linking

**Function:** Produces mechanically functional extracellular collagen networks.

**Where:** ER/Golgi, extracellular space and connective-tissue cells.

## Mechanism

1. Procollagen chains undergo proline/lysine hydroxylation and selected glycosylation in the ER.
2. Triple-helix assembly precedes secretion.
3. Extracellular propeptide cleavage allows fibril assembly for fibrillar collagens.
4. Lysyl oxidase initiates covalent cross-link formation.

## Control and constraints

Vitamin C supports hydroxylase chemistry; copper supports lysyl oxidase. Not all collagen types form the same fibrils. Matrix organisation and cross-links influence function as well as total collagen amount.
