# Motile Cilia Assembly and Beating

**Function:** Builds and powers coordinated surface fluid movement.

**Where:** Ciliated epithelia and axonemal microtubules.

## Mechanism

1. Basal bodies organise axonemes and intraflagellar transport supplies components.
2. In many motile cilia, nine peripheral microtubule doublets surround a central pair.
3. Axonemal dynein hydrolyses ATP to slide adjacent doublets.
4. Structural links convert sliding into bending and coordinated beats move surface material.

## Control and constraints

Not all cilia are motile or share the same axoneme arrangement. Primary sensory cilia have distinct signalling roles; embryonic nodal cilia are a notable motile structural exception.
