# Noradrenaline Methylation - PNMT

**Part of:** [[Catecholamine Synthesis and Clearance]]

## Transformation

Noradrenaline + S-adenosylmethionine → adrenaline + S-adenosylhomocysteine

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | PNMT |
| Compartment | Cytosol, prominently adrenal chromaffin cells |
| Physiological direction | Transfers a methyl group to form adrenaline |

## What controls this step

Adrenal glucocorticoid exposure supports PNMT expression. Product must be packaged into granules; not every sympathetic neuron performs this conversion.
