# Leukocyte Recruitment Cascade

**Function:** Moves selected circulating leukocytes into an affected tissue.

**Where:** Postcapillary venules and endothelial junctions.

## Mechanism

1. Selectin-ligand interactions support tethering and rolling.
2. Endothelial chemokines activate leukocyte GPCRs and inside-out integrin signalling.
3. High-affinity integrins bind ICAM/VCAM-family ligands for firm adhesion.
4. Crawling and junctional/transcellular migration permit entry followed by chemotaxis.

## Control and constraints

Different leukocyte types use different adhesion combinations. Shear forces, endothelial activation and local chemokine display determine recruitment; random motility alone is not chemotaxis.
