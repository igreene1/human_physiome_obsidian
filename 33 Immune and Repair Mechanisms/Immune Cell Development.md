# Immune Cell Development

**Function:** Generates and maintains diverse innate and adaptive cell populations.

**Where:** Marrow, thymus, peripheral lymphoid tissues and resident tissue niches.

## Mechanism

1. Hematopoietic progenitors integrate growth-factor and transcriptional cues.
2. Myeloid and lymphoid programmes generate specialised precursors with context-dependent trajectories.
3. T-cell maturation requires thymic selection, whereas B-cell maturation begins in marrow.
4. Some tissue-resident immune populations are seeded early and self-maintain locally.

## Control and constraints

Adult blood monocytes are not the sole origin of every resident macrophage. Developmental origin and current activation state are separate features.
