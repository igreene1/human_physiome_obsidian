# BCR Signalling

**Function:** Detects antigen and couples binding to B-cell activation and presentation.

**Where:** Surface immunoglobulin with CD79A/CD79B and intracellular adaptors.

## Mechanism

1. Antigen binding recruits Src-family/SYK signalling through ITAM-containing components.
2. BLNK-associated pathways activate BTK/PLCgamma2, calcium and other responses.
3. Antigen internalisation supports MHC-II presentation to helper T cells.
4. Costimulation and cytokines determine expansion and differentiation.

## Control and constraints

The BCR binds native antigen; TCRs usually recognise processed peptide-MHC. Coreceptors and inhibitory signals alter thresholds, and self-reactive cells are subject to tolerance controls.
