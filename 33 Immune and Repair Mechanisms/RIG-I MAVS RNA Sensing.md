# RIG-I MAVS RNA Sensing

**Function:** Detects selected cytosolic RNA patterns associated with viral replication.

**Where:** RIG-I-like receptors, mitochondrial-associated MAVS and downstream kinases.

## Mechanism

1. RIG-I or MDA5 recognise different RNA structural features.
2. Receptor activation exposes signalling domains that recruit MAVS assembly.
3. TBK1/IKK-related networks activate IRF and NF-kB programmes.
4. Type I/III interferon-related outputs induce antiviral states.

## Control and constraints

Different RNA viruses and replication compartments engage sensors differently. RNA length, ends and structure affect recognition; viral antagonists can block several steps.
