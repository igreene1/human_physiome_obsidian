# Germinal Centre Affinity Maturation

**Function:** Selects B-cell clones with improved antigen binding while preserving tolerance.

**Where:** Germinal-centre dark/light zones and T-follicular-helper interactions.

## Mechanism

1. Activated B cells proliferate and AID-dependent somatic hypermutation diversifies variable regions.
2. Cells compete for antigen displayed by follicular dendritic-cell networks.
3. Antigen uptake and presentation support access to T-cell help.
4. Selected clones re-enter mutation cycles or become memory/plasma cells.

## Control and constraints

Mutation is not directed toward a beneficial sequence; selection enriches favourable variants. Affinity maturation differs from class switching and can also create autoreactivity requiring restraint.
