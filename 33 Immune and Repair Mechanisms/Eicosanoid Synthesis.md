# Eicosanoid Synthesis

**Function:** Produces short-lived lipid mediators from membrane fatty-acid precursors.

**Where:** Membranes and enzyme complexes in many tissues.

## Mechanism

1. Phospholipase A2 releases arachidonic acid from phospholipids.
2. Cyclooxygenase routes generate prostanoid precursors, followed by tissue-specific synthases.
3. Lipoxygenase routes generate leukotrienes and other lipid mediators.
4. Receptor engagement alters vascular, platelet, airway and inflammatory responses.

## Control and constraints

Different products can have opposing effects: platelet thromboxane and endothelial prostacyclin illustrate this. Synthesis is cell-context dependent; eicosanoids are not one uniform inflammatory hormone.
