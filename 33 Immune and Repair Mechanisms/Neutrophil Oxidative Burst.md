# Neutrophil Oxidative Burst

**Function:** Produces antimicrobial oxidants after appropriate activation.

**Where:** Neutrophil phagosomal and plasma membranes.

## Mechanism

1. NOX2 complex components assemble on an activated membrane.
2. NADPH provides electrons transferred across the membrane to oxygen, generating superoxide.
3. Superoxide yields peroxide through spontaneous or enzyme-supported dismutation.
4. Myeloperoxidase can use peroxide and chloride to produce hypochlorous acid.

## Control and constraints

This intentionally generates oxidants rather than removing them. Spatial restriction and antioxidant systems reduce host injury. NADPH supply connects antimicrobial function to the pentose phosphate pathway.
