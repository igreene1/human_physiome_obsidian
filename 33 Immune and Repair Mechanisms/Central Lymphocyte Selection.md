# Central Lymphocyte Selection

**Function:** Reduces dangerous self-reactivity while preserving useful receptor repertoires.

**Where:** Thymus for T cells and bone marrow for B cells.

## Mechanism

1. Developing T cells undergo positive selection for useful self-MHC interaction.
2. Strong self-reactivity can trigger deletion or selected regulatory-cell fates; AIRE/FEZF2 expand self-antigen display.
3. Immature B cells can undergo receptor editing, deletion or anergy after self-recognition.
4. Surviving cells enter peripheral tolerance systems.

## Control and constraints

Selection is incomplete and probabilistic, so peripheral regulation remains necessary. Positive selection of T cells is not selection for binding foreign antigen encountered during development.
