# Angiogenesis

**Function:** Builds new vascular branches from existing vessels.

**Where:** Endothelial tip/stalk cells, supporting cells and matrix.

## Mechanism

1. Hypoxia and growth-factor signals increase endothelial VEGF responses.
2. Tip-cell migration and stalk-cell proliferation extend sprouts with Notch-linked patterning.
3. Lumen formation and anastomosis establish new flow paths.
4. Pericyte recruitment, basement-membrane formation and pruning stabilise useful vessels.

## Control and constraints

More vessels do not necessarily mean effective perfusion. Matrix, flow, oxygen demand and antiangiogenic signals determine mature network structure.
