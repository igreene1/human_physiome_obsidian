# Efferocytosis

**Function:** Clears apoptotic cells while supporting inflammatory resolution.

**Where:** Macrophages and other neighbouring phagocytes.

## Mechanism

1. Dying cells expose recognition signals including phosphatidylserine.
2. Bridging molecules and receptors such as TAM-family systems support recognition.
3. Actin-dependent engulfment internalises the cell without uncontrolled content release.
4. Digestion and metabolic/signalling changes can promote repair and limit inflammation.

## Control and constraints

Recognition combines eat-me and do-not-eat-me signals. Failed clearance can permit secondary membrane breakdown and inflammation; efferocytosis is not the same as killing a live pathogen.
