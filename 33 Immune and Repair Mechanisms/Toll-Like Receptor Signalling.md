# Toll-Like Receptor Signalling

**Function:** Recognises selected microbial or damage-associated patterns and recruits innate programmes.

**Where:** Plasma membrane/endosomal TLRs and adaptor complexes.

## Mechanism

1. Ligand recognition promotes receptor assembly in the appropriate compartment.
2. Most TLRs use MyD88-dependent signalling; TLR3 uses TRIF, and TLR4 can use both routes.
3. Kinase/adaptor complexes activate NF-kB/MAPK and selected IRF pathways.
4. Transcription increases cytokines, costimulatory molecules and antimicrobial capacity.

## Control and constraints

Endosomal localisation helps discriminate nucleic-acid context. TLR signals are regulated by trafficking and inhibitors; a ligand response depends on accessory proteins and cell type.
