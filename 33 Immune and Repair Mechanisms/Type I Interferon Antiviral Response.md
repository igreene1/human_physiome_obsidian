# Type I Interferon Antiviral Response

**Function:** Induces an antiviral programme in producing and neighbouring cells.

**Where:** IFNAR receptors and intracellular JAK-STAT machinery.

## Mechanism

1. Pattern-sensing pathways induce interferon-alpha/beta production.
2. Interferon binds IFNAR and activates associated JAK1/TYK2.
3. STAT1/STAT2 with IRF9 form a transcriptional complex.
4. Interferon-stimulated genes restrict viral entry, replication, translation or spread through multiple mechanisms.

## Control and constraints

Interferon is not a direct antibody-like neutraliser of every virus. Feedback regulators limit tissue damage from persistent signalling; type II and type III interferons have distinct receptors and distributions.
