# Inflammasome Assembly

**Function:** Activates inflammatory caspase processing in response to defined cellular danger signals.

**Where:** Cytosolic sensor-adaptor-caspase complexes.

## Mechanism

1. Priming commonly increases sensor/pro-IL-1beta expression and licensing modifications.
2. A sensor such as NLRP3 or AIM2 responds to its relevant activation context.
3. ASC-associated assembly recruits and activates caspase-1 in many canonical inflammasomes.
4. Caspase-1 processes cytokine precursors and gasdermin D.

## Control and constraints

NLRP3 integrates several disturbances and is not a single direct receptor for all proposed stimuli. Priming/activation requirements vary by cell type and sensor.
