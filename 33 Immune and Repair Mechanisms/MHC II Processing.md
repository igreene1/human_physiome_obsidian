# MHC II Processing

**Function:** Displays endocytosed and selected endogenous antigens to CD4 T cells.

**Where:** Professional antigen-presenting-cell endolysosomal compartments.

## Mechanism

1. MHC II assembles with invariant chain, which blocks premature peptide loading and directs trafficking.
2. Proteolysis leaves CLIP in the groove while antigen is degraded in acidic compartments.
3. HLA-DM promotes peptide exchange and editing.
4. Stable peptide-MHC II complexes reach the cell surface.

## Control and constraints

Presentation depends on peptide generation, affinity and processing context. MHC II expression is regulated and can be induced in some nonprofessional cells, but this does not necessarily provide full naive T-cell priming.
