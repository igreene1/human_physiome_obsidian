# CD8 Cytotoxic Killing

**Function:** Eliminates selected infected or altered target cells.

**Where:** Cytotoxic T-cell-target immune synapse.

## Mechanism

1. TCR recognition with suitable context establishes a focused synapse.
2. Granules polarise and release perforin and granzymes.
3. Perforin supports granzyme access, and granzymes trigger intracellular death mechanisms.
4. Death-ligand routes such as FASL can provide additional killing signals.

## Control and constraints

Target recognition and effector killing are separate steps. Focused release limits bystander exposure; target resistance and inhibitory signals can modify the outcome.
