# TCR Signalling and Costimulation

**Function:** Converts peptide-MHC recognition into a regulated T-cell response.

**Where:** TCR/CD3 microclusters and intracellular signal complexes.

## Mechanism

1. Lck phosphorylates CD3 ITAMs and recruits ZAP70.
2. LAT/SLP76-associated complexes organise PLCgamma1, calcium and Ras pathways.
3. Calcium-calcineurin activates NFAT; DAG-linked routes support PKCtheta/NF-kB and Ras/MAPK.
4. CD28 and cytokine signals support metabolism, IL-2 production and proliferation.

## Control and constraints

Signal strength, duration and inhibitory receptors such as CTLA4/PD1 influence outcome. A TCR binds peptide-MHC, not free soluble antigen in the same manner as a BCR.
