# Antigen Receptor Gene Rearrangement

**Function:** Builds diverse B- and T-cell receptor variable regions.

**Where:** Developing lymphocyte nuclei.

## Mechanism

1. RAG1/2 recognise recombination signal sequences and initiate selected V(D)J rearrangements.
2. DNA-end processing and junctional nucleotide addition diversify the join.
3. Classical end-joining machinery seals coding ends.
4. Productive receptors are tested and subject to developmental selection.

## Control and constraints

Heavy-chain/TCR-beta rearrangement and light-chain/TCR-alpha rearrangement use different segment combinations. This development-stage process differs from AID-driven somatic hypermutation and class switching in activated B cells.
