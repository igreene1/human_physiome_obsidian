# cGAS STING DNA Sensing

**Function:** Connects misplaced DNA to interferon-related innate signalling.

**Where:** Cytosolic DNA sensing and ER-to-Golgi STING trafficking.

## Mechanism

1. cGAS binds suitable double-stranded DNA and synthesises cyclic GMP-AMP.
2. cGAMP binds STING, altering its conformation and trafficking.
3. STING-associated TBK1 activates IRF3 while additional pathways recruit inflammatory signals.
4. Interferon and other outputs alter cell state and neighbouring-cell defences.

## Control and constraints

Self-DNA can activate this system if normal compartmentation/clearance fails. STING is an adaptor responding to cyclic dinucleotides, not a DNA-binding receptor itself.
