# NK Cell Recognition and Killing

**Function:** Responds rapidly to abnormal cells through integrated activating and inhibitory signals.

**Where:** Natural killer cells and target-cell interfaces.

## Mechanism

1. Inhibitory receptors recognise selected self-MHC-I ligands.
2. Activating receptors detect stress-related ligands; CD16 detects antibody-coated targets.
3. The balance of signals determines granule polarisation and release.
4. Perforin/granzyme killing and cytokine production alter target survival and neighbouring immunity.

## Control and constraints

Missing-self is one principle, not the only recognition rule. NK education calibrates responsiveness; reduced MHC I alone does not guarantee killing in every context.
