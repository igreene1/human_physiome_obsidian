# Mucosal IgA Transport

**Function:** Delivers antibody to mucosal surfaces while limiting inflammatory exposure.

**Where:** Plasma cells in lamina propria and epithelial polymeric-Ig receptor.

## Mechanism

1. Plasma cells produce polymeric IgA with J chain.
2. Basolateral pIgR binds polymeric IgA and internalises it.
3. Transcytosis carries the complex to the apical surface.
4. Receptor cleavage releases secretory IgA with a protective secretory component.

## Control and constraints

Secretory IgA helps exclude and neutralise luminal targets with relatively restrained inflammation. This is receptor-mediated epithelial transport, not free leakage of antibody across an intact barrier.
