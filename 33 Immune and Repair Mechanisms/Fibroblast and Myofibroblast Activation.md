# Fibroblast and Myofibroblast Activation

**Function:** Deposits and contracts repair matrix under biochemical/mechanical control.

**Where:** Connective-tissue fibroblasts and repair niches.

## Mechanism

1. Injury-derived factors and matrix changes activate fibroblast programmes.
2. TGF-beta-associated signalling and mechanical tension promote myofibroblast features in suitable contexts.
3. Cells deposit collagen and other matrix and generate contractile force.
4. Resolution removes/deactivates cells and remodels matrix; persistence can favour fibrosis.

## Control and constraints

Myofibroblast states and origins vary by organ. A stiff matrix can reinforce activation, creating feedback between mechanics and gene expression.
