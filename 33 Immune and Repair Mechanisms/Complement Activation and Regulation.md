# Complement Activation and Regulation

**Function:** Amplifies target tagging while protecting host surfaces.

**Where:** Plasma/tissue-fluid complement proteins and membrane regulators.

## Mechanism

1. Classical or lectin initiation uses C4/C2 components, while alternative activation uses factors B and D.
2. C3 convertases generate C3b; alternative-pathway amplification increases deposition.
3. C5 convertases enable C5a release and C5b-9 assembly.
4. Factor H/I, CD46/CD55 and CD59 restrain selected stages on host cells.

## Control and constraints

Convertase nomenclature has historical variants, so component functions are more reliable than memorising one letter convention. Complement supports clearance and inflammation as well as lysis.
