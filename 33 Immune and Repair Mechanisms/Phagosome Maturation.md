# Phagosome Maturation

**Function:** Converts an engulfed-particle compartment into a degradative organelle.

**Where:** Phagosomes and endolysosomal network.

## Mechanism

1. Receptor-driven actin remodelling closes a phagosome around cargo.
2. Rab conversion and membrane fusion alter its molecular identity.
3. V-ATPase, hydrolase delivery and lysosomal fusion establish degradative conditions.
4. Degradation products are recycled or routed to antigen-processing systems.

## Control and constraints

Maturation depends on cell type and cargo; some pathogens actively interrupt it. Acidification, oxidant generation and protease delivery are coordinated but distinguishable processes.
