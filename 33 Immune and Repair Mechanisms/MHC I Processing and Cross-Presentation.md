# MHC I Processing and Cross-Presentation

**Function:** Displays intracellular and selected external antigens to CD8 T cells.

**Where:** Proteasome, ER and dendritic-cell cross-presentation routes.

## Mechanism

1. Proteasomes generate peptide fragments from cellular proteins.
2. TAP transports many peptides into ER for trimming/loading onto MHC I with beta2-microglobulin.
3. Quality-controlled complexes reach the surface.
4. Selected dendritic cells route external antigen into MHC-I presentation through cytosolic or vacuolar variants.

## Control and constraints

Most nucleated cells present MHC I, but naive CD8 activation usually requires professional presentation and costimulation. Cross-presentation route usage varies with antigen and cell subtype.
