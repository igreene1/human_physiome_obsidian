# Antibody Class Switching

**Function:** Changes antibody effector isotype while retaining antigen-binding variable-region identity.

**Where:** Activated B-cell immunoglobulin heavy-chain loci.

## Mechanism

1. CD40 and cytokine signals support germline transcription at selected switch regions.
2. AID initiates cytidine deamination that is processed into DNA lesions.
3. Switch-region breaks are joined to place a different constant region downstream of the rearranged variable region.
4. The B cell expresses an isotype with altered effector and distribution properties.

## Control and constraints

Switching is DNA recombination, whereas naive IgM/IgD coexpression largely uses RNA processing. The cytokine/isotype relationship is not identical in mice and humans.
