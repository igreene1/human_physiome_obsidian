# T Cell Differentiation

**Function:** Adapts T-cell effector programmes to antigenic and cytokine context.

**Where:** Activated T cells in lymphoid and peripheral tissues.

## Mechanism

1. TCR/costimulatory signals initiate activation.
2. Cytokine-receptor signalling recruits transcription-factor programmes such as T-bet, GATA3, RORgamma-t, BCL6 or FOXP3 in relevant subsets.
3. Metabolic and epigenetic changes reinforce selected functions.
4. Expansion, tissue migration, contraction and memory formation shape the population.

## Control and constraints

Helper subsets are useful categories but have plasticity and overlap. Human tissue states cannot always be assigned to rigid in-vitro polarisation labels.
