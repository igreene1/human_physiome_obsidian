# Peripheral Tolerance and Treg Cells

**Function:** Restrains inappropriate lymphocyte activation outside primary lymphoid organs.

**Where:** Lymphoid/peripheral tissues and regulatory T-cell networks.

## Mechanism

1. Antigen recognition without appropriate costimulation can induce anergy or deletion.
2. FOXP3-associated regulatory T cells use mechanisms including CTLA4 and suppressive cytokine context.
3. PD1 and other inhibitory receptors tune responses during activation.
4. Antigen clearance and growth-factor withdrawal contribute to contraction.

## Control and constraints

No one molecule accounts for all suppression. Treg stability and function depend on context; immune tolerance is distinct from an absence of antigen exposure.
