# Carbamoyl Phosphate for Pyrimidines - CPS2

**Part of:** [[Pyrimidine and Thymidylate Synthesis]]

## Transformation

Glutamine + bicarbonate + 2 ATP + H2O → carbamoyl phosphate + glutamate + 2 ADP + Pi

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | Carbamoyl-phosphate-synthetase-II domain of CAD |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Supplies carbamoyl phosphate to de novo pyrimidine synthesis |

## What controls this step

Unlike CPS1 it uses glutamine nitrogen and does not depend on N-acetylglutamate activation. UTP feedback and PRPP/other signals regulate pathway entry.
