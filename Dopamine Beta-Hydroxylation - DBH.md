# Dopamine Beta-Hydroxylation - DBH

**Part of:** [[Catecholamine Synthesis and Clearance]]

## Transformation

Dopamine + O2 + reducing equivalents → noradrenaline + H2O

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | DBH; copper and ascorbate-supported electron supply |
| Compartment | Secretory-vesicle lumen in noradrenergic/chromaffin cells |
| Physiological direction | Adds a beta hydroxyl group to dopamine |

## What controls this step

Requires vesicular dopamine transport and appropriate enzyme expression. Dopaminergic neurons lacking DBH stop the canonical synthetic sequence at dopamine.
