# Natural Killer Cell

**Location / identity:** Innate lymphoid cytotoxic population.

**Function:** Integrates stress, antibody and self-associated signals to regulate killing.

## Cellular machinery

Activating/inhibitory receptor combinations determine granule release and cytokine output. NK education and tissue context tune responsiveness; it is not simply a T cell lacking antigen specificity.
