# Dendritic Cell

**Location / identity:** Multiple conventional and plasmacytoid populations.

**Function:** Connects innate recognition with antigen presentation and T-cell priming.

## Cellular machinery

Antigen uptake, MHC presentation, costimulation and migration change during maturation. Selected conventional subsets specialise in cross-presentation; plasmacytoid cells have prominent type-I-interferon capacity in suitable contexts.
