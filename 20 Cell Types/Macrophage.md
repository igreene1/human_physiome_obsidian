# Macrophage

**Location / identity:** Tissue-resident and monocyte-derived populations.

**Function:** Phagocytoses, processes antigens and coordinates inflammation/repair.

## Cellular machinery

Fc/complement/pattern-recognition receptors, lysosomes and metabolic programmes support context-dependent functions. Tissue identity and activation states form continua rather than a universal M1-versus-M2 switch.
