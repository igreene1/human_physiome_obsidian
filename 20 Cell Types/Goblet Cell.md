# Goblet Cell

**Location / identity:** Mucus-secreting epithelial cell in selected mucosae.

**Function:** Produces mucins that help protect and lubricate epithelial surfaces.

## Cellular machinery

ER/Golgi processing and regulated granule secretion supply heavily glycosylated mucins. Hydration, bicarbonate and local ionic conditions influence expansion and mucus properties after release.
