# Neutrophil

**Location / identity:** Blood and rapidly recruited tissue effector.

**Function:** Provides rapid antimicrobial defence and granule-associated effector activity.

## Cellular machinery

NOX2-dependent oxidant generation, proteases and phagocytosis cooperate. Short-lived inflammatory activity must be constrained and followed by clearance to avoid host injury.
