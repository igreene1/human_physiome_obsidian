# Hepatocyte

**Role:** Performs most liver metabolic, synthetic and detoxification functions.

## Core mechanism

substrates/hormones enter via sinusoidal blood → transporter/receptor signalling → pathway-specific flux → metabolites, glucose, ketones, urea, lipoproteins, proteins or bile components leave cell
