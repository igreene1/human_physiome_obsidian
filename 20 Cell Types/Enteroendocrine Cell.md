# Enteroendocrine Cell

**Location / identity:** Diverse hormone-secreting gut epithelial populations.

**Function:** Transduces luminal/nutrient conditions into endocrine and neural signals.

## Cellular machinery

Receptors, transporters and metabolic sensing couple to granule release. Cells can express combinations of hormones, so a one-cell-one-hormone classification is a useful simplification rather than a complete atlas.
