# Osteoclast

**Location / identity:** Multinucleated hematopoietic-lineage bone-resorbing cell.

**Function:** Removes mineral and organic bone matrix.

## Cellular machinery

A sealing zone and ruffled border localise proton transport and proteolysis. Differentiation depends on RANKL/M-CSF-associated programmes; it is not a mature osteoblast changing jobs.
