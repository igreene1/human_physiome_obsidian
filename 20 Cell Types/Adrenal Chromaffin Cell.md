# Adrenal Chromaffin Cell

**Role:** Neuroendocrine cell that secretes epinephrine/norepinephrine during sympathetic activation.

## Core mechanism

preganglionic sympathetic ACh → nicotinic receptor depolarisation → Ca2+ entry → catecholamine vesicle exocytosis
