# Osteoblast

**Location / identity:** Bone-forming mesenchymal-lineage cell.

**Function:** Synthesises osteoid and supports controlled mineralisation.

## Cellular machinery

Collagen-rich matrix secretion and alkaline-phosphatase-associated phosphate/pyrophosphate balance shape mineral deposition. Osteoblast-lineage cells also regulate osteoclast recruitment through RANKL/OPG.
