# Keratinocyte

**Location / identity:** Major epidermal cell population.

**Function:** Builds a renewing mechanical, water and immune barrier.

## Cellular machinery

Basal proliferation gives way to stage-dependent differentiation, envelope formation, lipid secretion and cornification. Keratinocytes also respond to injury and participate in local immune communication.
