# Microglial Cell

**Location / identity:** Central nervous system resident immune population.

**Function:** Surveys tissue and clears material while interacting with neural circuits.

## Cellular machinery

Microglia have developmental and niche features distinct from freshly recruited blood monocytes. Receptor signalling, phagocytosis and inflammatory programmes change with local context; activation is not one binary phenotype.
