# Astrocyte

**Location / identity:** Central nervous system glia.

**Function:** Couples transmitter clearance, ion/water handling and metabolic support to neural activity.

## Cellular machinery

EAAT1/EAAT2-associated glutamate uptake, glutamine synthetase and ion-handling systems help maintain the extracellular environment. Endfeet interact with vessels, but endothelial cells form the primary blood-brain barrier.
