# Chondrocyte

**Location / identity:** Cartilage matrix-maintaining cell.

**Function:** Synthesises and remodells cartilage appropriate to tissue location.

## Cellular machinery

Articular and growth-plate chondrocytes have different programmes. Proteoglycans, collagen composition and mechanical sensing support compression resistance or developmental maturation in their respective settings.
