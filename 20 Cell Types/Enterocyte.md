# Enterocyte

**Location / identity:** Small-intestinal absorptive epithelial cell.

**Function:** Combines brush-border processing with polarised nutrient transport.

## Cellular machinery

Apical carriers/enzymes and basolateral pumps/exporters create vectorial transport. ER/Golgi lipid packaging gives long-chain lipid a route distinct from most soluble nutrients.
