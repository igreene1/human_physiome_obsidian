# Hypothalamic Neurosecretory Neuron

**Role:** Converts neural/internal-state information into endocrine output.

## Core mechanism

synaptic/chemical/osmotic input → neuronal integration → peptide synthesis and activity-dependent secretion into portal blood or posterior pituitary terminals
