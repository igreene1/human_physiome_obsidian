# Erythrocyte

**Location / identity:** Circulating mature red blood cell.

**Function:** Transports respiratory gases while maintaining deformability and redox integrity.

## Cellular machinery

Mature cells lack a nucleus and mitochondria. Glycolysis, PPP, membrane-skeleton interactions and specialised gas-handling proteins compensate for a limited capacity to replace damaged components.
