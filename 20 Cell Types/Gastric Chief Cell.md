# Gastric Chief Cell

**Location / identity:** Gastric gland secretory epithelial cell.

**Function:** Supplies pepsinogen and other gastric secretory products.

## Cellular machinery

A developed protein-secretory apparatus packages enzyme precursors for release. Pepsinogen activation occurs in acidic luminal conditions, helping separate synthesis from proteolytic action.
