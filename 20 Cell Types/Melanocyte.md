# Melanocyte

**Location / identity:** Pigment-producing epidermal/follicular cell.

**Function:** Synthesises melanin in melanosomes and transfers it to neighbouring cells.

## Cellular machinery

Tyrosinase-associated chemistry and organelle maturation determine pigment products. Transfer and persistence in keratinocytes influence appearance and photoprotection independently of melanocyte count.
