# Fibroblast

**Location / identity:** Diverse connective-tissue stromal populations.

**Function:** Maintains matrix and participates in repair and immune signalling.

## Cellular machinery

Matrix synthesis, protease/inhibitor balance and mechanical responses vary by organ and state. Myofibroblast differentiation is a context-dependent programme rather than a permanent universal fibroblast identity.
