# Endothelial Cell

**Role:** Lines blood vessels and controls barrier function, vascular tone, coagulation/inflammation interfaces and exchange.

## Core mechanism

shear/agonist sensing → mediators such as NO, prostacyclin/endothelin + regulated permeability/adhesion
