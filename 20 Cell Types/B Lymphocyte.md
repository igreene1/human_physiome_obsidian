# B Lymphocyte

**Location / identity:** Follicles, blood and other lymphoid compartments.

**Function:** Recognises native antigen and can become an antibody-secreting or memory cell.

## Cellular machinery

Surface immunoglobulin with CD79 signalling connects antigen binding to uptake and presentation. Differentiation changes surface receptors, trafficking and secretory machinery; not every B cell is already a plasma cell.
