# Oligodendrocyte

**Location / identity:** Central nervous system.

**Function:** Forms CNS myelin and supports axonal function.

## Cellular machinery

A mature oligodendrocyte can form multiple internodes on different axons. Membrane production, specialised proteins and axon-glia communication coordinate insulation and metabolic support.
