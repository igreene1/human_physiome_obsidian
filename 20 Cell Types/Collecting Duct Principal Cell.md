# Collecting Duct Principal Cell

**Role:** Fine-tunes sodium, potassium and water handling under aldosterone and ADH control.

## Core mechanism

aldosterone → [[Mineralocorticoid Receptor]] → ↑ ENaC/Na-K ATPase; ADH → [[V2 Receptor]] → cAMP/PKA → [[Aquaporin 2]] insertion
