# Leydig Cell

**Location / identity:** Testicular interstitial steroidogenic cell.

**Function:** Produces testosterone in response to LH-linked stimulation.

## Cellular machinery

LH receptor signalling increases steroidogenic substrate delivery/capacity. Cholesterol-derived steroid synthesis supports local spermatogenesis and systemic androgen actions with regulated feedback.
