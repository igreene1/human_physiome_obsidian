# Mast Cell

**Location / identity:** Tissue-resident granule-rich immune cell.

**Function:** Rapidly releases stored and newly synthesised mediators after appropriate activation.

## Cellular machinery

Fc-epsilon-RI-bound IgE cross-linking is one activation route. Histamine, proteases, lipid mediators and cytokines have different synthesis/release kinetics and tissue effects.
