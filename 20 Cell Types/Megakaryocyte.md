# Megakaryocyte

**Location / identity:** Marrow vascular niche.

**Function:** Produces platelets after specialised polyploid maturation.

## Cellular machinery

Thrombopoietin-MPL signalling, endomitosis and extensive cytoplasmic/membrane organisation support proplatelet formation. It is a large precursor cell, distinct from the platelet fragments it releases.
