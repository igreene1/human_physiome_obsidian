# Early Embryonic Development

**Function:** Builds the first cell lineages and body-plan precursors from the fertilised egg.

**Where:** Cleavage embryo, morula and blastocyst.

## Mechanism

1. Early cleavage partitions the conceptus into blastomeres with little increase in total size.
2. Compaction and polarity help distinguish outer from inner populations.
3. Blastocyst formation produces trophectoderm and inner-cell-mass lineages.
4. Epiblast and extraembryonic interactions prepare the embryo for implantation and later gastrulation.

## Control and constraints

Development is a changing network of signalling and gene regulation, not a preformed miniature growing larger. Human timing and lineage mechanisms cannot always be assumed identical to mouse models.
