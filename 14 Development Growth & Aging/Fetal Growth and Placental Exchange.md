# Fetal Growth and Placental Exchange

**Function:** Supports fetal metabolism and development through selective maternal-fetal transfer.

**Where:** Placental barrier, fetal circulation and fetal tissues.

## Mechanism

1. Concentration/pressure gradients support respiratory gas exchange.
2. Transport systems move nutrients such as glucose and amino acids across placental layers.
3. Fetal metabolism and endocrine signals direct growth and storage.
4. Placenta transfers waste toward maternal elimination and provides selected endocrine/immune functions.

## Control and constraints

Placental transport is not universally passive and the placenta is metabolically active. Fetal oxygen affinity and circulatory shunts support prenatal life; postnatal conditions require a major transition.
