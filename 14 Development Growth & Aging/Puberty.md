# Puberty

**Function:** Reactivates reproductive endocrine function and coordinates sexual maturation.

**Where:** Hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis and target tissues.

## Mechanism

1. Changing neural regulation permits stronger pulsatile GnRH output.
2. LH/FSH drive gonadal steroid and gamete-related development.
3. Sex steroids produce secondary sexual characteristics and interact with GH/IGF-1.
4. Skeletal maturation accelerates and eventually limits further linear growth.

## Control and constraints

Adrenarche is related in timing but distinct from gonadarche. Pubertal timing varies and reflects genetic, nutritional and health factors. Estradiol signalling contributes to growth-plate closure in all sexes.
