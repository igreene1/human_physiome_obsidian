# Postnatal Growth

**Function:** Increases tissue size and functional capacity after birth.

**Where:** Growth plates, muscle, organs and endocrine/nutritional systems.

## Mechanism

1. Adequate substrates permit cellular growth and proliferation.
2. GH/IGF-1, thyroid hormone, insulin and sex steroids influence different phases and tissues.
3. Growth-plate chondrocytes proliferate, hypertrophy and support endochondral bone formation.
4. Maturation progressively changes body proportions and eventually closes growth plates.

## Control and constraints

Growth velocity depends on age and stage; one fixed rate cannot describe childhood and puberty. Longitudinal bone growth differs from later bone remodelling and muscle hypertrophy.
