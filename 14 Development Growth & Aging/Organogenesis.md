# Organogenesis

**Function:** Transforms embryonic germ layers into patterned organ primordia.

**Where:** Embryonic tissues and their interacting signalling centres.

## Mechanism

1. Gastrulation produces ectoderm, mesoderm and endoderm in an organised spatial context.
2. Morphogen gradients and transcription-factor combinations specify regional identities.
3. Cell proliferation, migration, epithelial folding and selective death shape structures.
4. Reciprocal tissue interactions refine organ differentiation and connectivity.

## Control and constraints

The same pathways, including Wnt, Hedgehog, BMP and Notch, are reused with different outcomes by timing and location. An organ cannot usually be assigned to one signalling molecule or one germ-layer contribution without qualification.
