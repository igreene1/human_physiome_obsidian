# Cellular Senescence and Aging Biology

**Function:** Describes durable stress-associated growth arrest and broader age-related changes.

**Where:** Multiple tissues, stem-cell niches and immune/metabolic systems.

## Mechanism

1. DNA damage, oncogenic stress or other persistent signals can recruit p53/p21 and p16/RB arrest pathways.
2. Senescent cells change chromatin, metabolism and secretory programmes.
3. Immune clearance may remove such cells; persistence can alter neighbouring tissues.
4. Aging also includes changes in proteostasis, mitochondria, stem cells and intercellular communication.

## Control and constraints

Senescence can aid development, repair and tumour suppression in context; it is not uniformly harmful. Aging is not caused by one pathway, and human causal contributions remain an active research area.
