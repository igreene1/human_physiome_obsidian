# Acetyl-CoA Carboxylation - ACC

**Part of:** [[Fatty Acid Synthesis and Lipogenesis]]

## Transformation

Acetyl-CoA + bicarbonate + ATP → malonyl-CoA + ADP + Pi

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | ACACA or ACACB; biotin |
| Compartment | Cytosol and mitochondrial-associated cytosolic surfaces |
| Physiological direction | Provides malonyl-CoA for fatty-acid synthesis or local regulation of CPT1 |

## What controls this step

AMPK inhibits ACC by phosphorylation. Citrate favours assembly/activity; long-chain acyl-CoA and energy limitation restrain synthesis. ACACA and ACACB have different tissue roles.
