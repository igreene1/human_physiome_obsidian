# Thyroid Gland

**Role:** Synthesises and releases T4 and T3 under TSH control, influencing basal metabolism, thermogenesis, growth and development.

## Core mechanism

TSH → follicular cell iodide uptake → thyroglobulin iodination/coupling → colloid storage → endocytosis/proteolysis → T4/T3 release
