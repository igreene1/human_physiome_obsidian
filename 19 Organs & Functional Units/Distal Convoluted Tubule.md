# Distal Convoluted Tubule

**Role:** Fine-tunes NaCl and divalent-cation handling after the loop of Henle.

## Core mechanism

apical [[NCC]] moves NaCl into cell → basolateral [[Na-K ATPase]] maintains driving force; distal calcium entry is regulated by PTH

## Main structures / cell types

- distal convoluted tubule epithelial cell
- [[NCC]]
- TRPV5
- [[Na-K ATPase]]
