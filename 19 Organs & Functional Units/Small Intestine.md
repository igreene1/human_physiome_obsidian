# Small Intestine

**Role:** Primary site of enzymatic digestion and absorption of macronutrients, electrolytes, water and many micronutrients.

## Core mechanism

luminal digestion → brush-border processing → enterocyte uptake → portal blood or lymphatic transport

## Main structures / cell types

- villi
- crypts
- enterocytes
- goblet cells
- enteroendocrine cells
- lacteals
