# Alveolus

**Role:** Microscopic gas-exchange unit where air is placed next to a thin perfused capillary membrane.

## Core mechanism

O2 diffuses air → liquid/surfactant layer → [[Type I Pneumocyte]] → interstitium → capillary endothelium → blood; CO2 moves in the opposite direction
