# Vagus Nerve

**Role:** Cranial nerve X is the major parasympathetic conduit to thoracic and much of the abdominal viscera and also carries extensive visceral sensory information back to the brainstem.

## Afferent side

Visceral sensory fibres carry mechanical and chemical information from heart, lungs, gastrointestinal tract and other viscera. Many terminate in the [[Nucleus Tractus Solitarius]].

## Efferent side

Preganglionic parasympathetic fibres arise mainly from the [[Dorsal Motor Nucleus of Vagus]] and [[Nucleus Ambiguus]]. They synapse in ganglia near or within target organs, where [[Acetylcholine]] activates [[Nicotinic Neuronal Receptor|neuronal nicotinic receptors]]. Postganglionic cholinergic neurons then act mainly through muscarinic receptors.
