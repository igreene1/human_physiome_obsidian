# Posterior Pituitary

**Role:** Neurohaemal extension of the hypothalamus that stores and releases peptide hormones made by magnocellular hypothalamic neurons.

## Mechanism

[[Vasopressin ADH]] and [[Oxytocin]] are synthesised in hypothalamic neurons, especially the [[Supraoptic Nucleus]] and [[Paraventricular Hypothalamic Nucleus]]. Their axons descend to the posterior pituitary. Action potentials trigger Ca²⁺-dependent exocytosis from nerve terminals into systemic blood.

## Important distinction

The posterior pituitary **releases** these hormones but does not synthesise them in endocrine cells in the way the anterior pituitary synthesises ACTH, TSH, LH, FSH, GH and prolactin.
