# Pancreatic Islet

**Role:** Endocrine micro-organ that senses nutrient state and coordinates insulin, glucagon, somatostatin and other signals.

## Core mechanism

blood glucose/amino acids/autonomic input/incretins → islet cell sensing → hormone secretion → liver, muscle, adipose and other target tissues

- [[Pancreatic Alpha Cell]]
- delta cells
- PP cells
- islet capillaries
