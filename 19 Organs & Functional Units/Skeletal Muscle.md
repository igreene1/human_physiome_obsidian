# Skeletal Muscle

**Role:** Generates force and movement and acts as a major sink/source for glucose, amino acids, lactate and heat.

## Core mechanism

motor neuron AP → neuromuscular transmission → muscle AP → Ca2+ release → cross-bridge cycling; metabolic pathways regenerate ATP
