# Hypothalamus

**Role:** Integrates neural, endocrine, osmotic, thermal, energy and circadian information to coordinate homeostasis and behaviour.

## Core mechanism

sensory/internal-state inputs → specialised nuclei and neurosecretory neurons → autonomic outputs + pituitary releasing hormones + ADH/oxytocin

## Main structures / cell types

- [[Hypothalamic Neurosecretory Neuron]]
- supraoptic/paraventricular nuclei
- arcuate nucleus
- suprachiasmatic nucleus
- preoptic area
