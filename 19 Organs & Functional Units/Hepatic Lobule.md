# Hepatic Lobule

**Role:** Repeated microvascular/metabolic unit of the liver organised from portal triads toward central veins.

## Core mechanism

portal venous + arterial blood → sinusoids → progressively changing oxygen/substrate environment → hepatocyte zones → central vein; bile flows toward portal tracts

## Main structures / cell types

- periportal hepatocytes
- midzonal hepatocytes
- pericentral hepatocytes
- sinusoids
- central vein
