# Kidney

**Role:** Maintains extracellular fluid composition and volume by filtering plasma and selectively reabsorbing or secreting water and solutes; also performs endocrine functions.

## Core mechanism

renal blood flow → glomerular filtration → tubular reabsorption/secretion → concentration or dilution of urine → excretion

## Main structures / cell types

- [[Nephron]]
- renal vasculature
- renal interstitium
- juxtaglomerular apparatus
- collecting system
