# Heart

**Role:** Generates pressure and flow to perfuse tissues and return blood through pulmonary and systemic circuits.

## Core mechanism

electrical activation → coordinated contraction → pressure development → valve opening/closure → stroke volume → cardiac output

- atria
- AV node
- His-Purkinje system
