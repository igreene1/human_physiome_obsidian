# Collecting Duct

**Role:** Final regulated site for sodium, potassium, water and acid-base handling.

## Core mechanism

[[Collecting Duct Principal Cell]] controls ENaC-mediated Na+ uptake, K+ secretion and ADH-regulated water permeability; intercalated cells regulate H+/HCO3−
