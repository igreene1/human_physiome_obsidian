# Hydroxymethylbilane Formation - HMBS

**Part of:** [[Heme Synthesis and Degradation]]

## Transformation

4 Porphobilinogen → hydroxymethylbilane + ammonia release

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | HMBS, also called porphobilinogen deaminase |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Builds a linear tetrapyrrole |

## What controls this step

Flux depends on precursor delivery and enzyme function. The next enzyme must cyclise this intermediate in the correct arrangement for productive heme synthesis.
