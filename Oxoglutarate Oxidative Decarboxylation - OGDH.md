# Oxoglutarate Oxidative Decarboxylation - OGDH

**Part of:** [[TCA Cycle]]

## Transformation

2-Oxoglutarate + CoA-SH + NAD+ → succinyl-CoA + CO2 + NADH + H+

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | OGDH, DLST and DLD; TPP, lipoate, FAD, NAD+ and CoA |
| Compartment | Mitochondrial matrix |
| Physiological direction | Second oxidative decarboxylation; conserves energy in NADH and a thioester |

## What controls this step

NADH and succinyl-CoA inhibit; Ca2+ can favour activity in working tissues. The multienzyme chemistry resembles pyruvate dehydrogenase.
