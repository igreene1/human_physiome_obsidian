# Protoporphyrinogen IX Formation - CPOX

**Part of:** [[Heme Synthesis and Degradation]]

## Transformation

Coproporphyrinogen III → protoporphyrinogen IX by oxidative decarboxylation

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | CPOX; oxygen-dependent reaction |
| Compartment | Mitochondrial intermembrane-space region |
| Physiological direction | Converts two propionate side chains to vinyl groups |

## What controls this step

Depends on oxygen and precursor delivery. This oxidative chemistry precedes formation of the conjugated protoporphyrin ring.
