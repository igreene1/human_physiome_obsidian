# T4 Thyroxine

**Identity:** Major hormone secreted by thyroid; prohormone/reservoir for T3.

## Source

Thyroid follicular cells.

## Main trigger(s)

TSH-dependent synthesis from iodinated thyroglobulin.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Thyroid hormone receptors after conversion to T3.  
**Canonical signalling:** Deiodinase-dependent activation/inactivation; nuclear receptor transcription.

## Main physiological effects

Sets broad metabolic/developmental programmes and supports thermogenesis/cardiovascular responsiveness.

## Feedback / shut-off

Negative feedback at hypothalamus/pituitary.
