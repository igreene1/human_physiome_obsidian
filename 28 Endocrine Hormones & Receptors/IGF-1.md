# IGF-1

**Identity:** Insulin-like growth factor 1, major mediator of many GH-dependent growth effects.

## Source

Liver and many peripheral tissues.

## Main trigger(s)

Growth hormone plus nutritional state.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** IGF1 receptor, a receptor tyrosine kinase.  
**Canonical signalling:** PI3K/AKT and MAPK pathways.

## Main physiological effects

Cell growth, survival, protein synthesis and skeletal growth programmes.

## Feedback / shut-off

Feeds back to reduce GH axis drive.
