# CRH

**Identity:** Corticotropin-releasing hormone.

## Source

Paraventricular hypothalamic neurons.

## Main trigger(s)

Stress-related, circadian and limbic inputs; vasopressin can act synergistically.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** CRH1 receptors on pituitary corticotrophs.  
**Canonical signalling:** Gs → cAMP/PKA.

## Main physiological effects

ACTH synthesis/release.

## Feedback / shut-off

Cortisol feeds back at pituitary and hypothalamic levels.
