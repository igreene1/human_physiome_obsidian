# Renin

**Identity:** Protease initiating the renin–angiotensin system.

## Source

Juxtaglomerular granular cells of kidney.

## Main trigger(s)

Reduced renal perfusion pressure, reduced macula-densa NaCl delivery, β1 sympathetic stimulation.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Acts enzymatically on angiotensinogen rather than via a classic receptor.  
**Canonical signalling:** Angiotensinogen → angiotensin I → ACE → angiotensin II.

## Main physiological effects

Enables angiotensin-II/aldosterone responses that defend perfusion/volume.

## Feedback / shut-off

Restored pressure/NaCl and angiotensin II provide feedback suppression.
