# GnRH

**Identity:** Gonadotropin-releasing hormone.

## Source

Hypothalamic GnRH neurons.

## Main trigger(s)

Pulsatile neural/endocrine network; sex-steroid feedback; developmental activation at puberty.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** GnRH receptor on gonadotrophs.  
**Canonical signalling:** Gq/PLC/IP3/Ca²⁺/PKC.

## Main physiological effects

Pulsatile LH and FSH release.

## Feedback / shut-off

Sex steroids and inhibins provide feedback; continuous receptor stimulation causes desensitisation.
