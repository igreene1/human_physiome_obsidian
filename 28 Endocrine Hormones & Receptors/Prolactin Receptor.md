# Prolactin Receptor

**Role:** Class I cytokine receptor expressed in mammary gland and many other tissues.

## Mechanism

Prolactin → receptor → JAK2/STAT5 and additional signalling.

## Main consequence

Essential for mammary differentiation and milk-protein gene expression.
