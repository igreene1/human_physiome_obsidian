# Inhibin

**Identity:** Gonadal peptide hormone that selectively regulates FSH.

## Source

Granulosa cells and Sertoli cells.

## Main trigger(s)

Gonadal activity and gametogenic state.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Acts through TGF-β family co-receptor mechanisms to antagonise activin effects.  
**Canonical signalling:** Modifies SMAD-related signalling in pituitary.

## Main physiological effects

Suppresses FSH secretion.

## Feedback / shut-off

Participates in gonad–pituitary feedback.
