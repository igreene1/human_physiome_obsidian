# Leptin Receptor

**Role:** Cytokine-receptor-family receptor; long isoform Ob-Rb mediates major hypothalamic effects.

## Mechanism

Leptin → receptor-associated JAK2 → STAT3 with PI3K and other branches.

## Main consequence

Signals energy-store sufficiency and modifies feeding/endocrine/autonomic outputs.
