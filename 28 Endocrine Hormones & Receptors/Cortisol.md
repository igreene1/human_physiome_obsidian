# Cortisol

**Identity:** Principal human glucocorticoid.

## Source

Adrenal cortex zona fasciculata.

## Main trigger(s)

ACTH; circadian and stress-related HPA drive.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Glucocorticoid receptor.  
**Canonical signalling:** Intracellular/nuclear receptor regulation of transcription plus rapid non-genomic effects.

## Main physiological effects

Supports fasting/stress metabolism, vascular responsiveness, immune modulation and many tissue programs.

## Feedback / shut-off

Negative feedback suppresses CRH/ACTH; metabolism/inactivation in liver/kidney and peripheral tissues.
