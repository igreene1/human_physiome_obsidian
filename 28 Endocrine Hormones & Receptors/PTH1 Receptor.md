# PTH1 Receptor

**Role:** GPCR for PTH and PTH-related peptide in bone/kidney target cells.

## Mechanism

Can activate Gs/cAMP/PKA and Gq/PLC pathways.

## Main consequence

Coordinates renal calcium/phosphate handling and bone-cell signalling.
