# Insulin

**Identity:** Anabolic peptide hormone signalling nutrient abundance.

## Source

Pancreatic beta cells.

## Main trigger(s)

Elevated glucose, incretins, amino acids and autonomic inputs.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Insulin receptor.  
**Canonical signalling:** RTK → IRS → PI3K/AKT with MAPK branches.

## Main physiological effects

Promotes glucose uptake in muscle/adipose, glycogen/protein/lipid synthesis and suppresses hepatic glucose output/lipolysis.

## Feedback / shut-off

Falling glucose reduces secretion; target effects lower the initiating glucose signal.
