# Prolactin

**Identity:** Anterior-pituitary hormone central to mammary development and milk production.

## Source

Lactotrophs.

## Main trigger(s)

Tonic inhibition by hypothalamic dopamine; suckling reduces dopaminergic inhibition; TRH and other inputs can stimulate.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Prolactin receptor.  
**Canonical signalling:** JAK2/STAT5.

## Main physiological effects

Milk synthesis and reproductive-axis modulation.

## Feedback / shut-off

Prolactin promotes hypothalamic dopamine feedback; high prolactin can suppress GnRH.
