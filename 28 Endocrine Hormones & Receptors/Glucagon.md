# Glucagon

**Identity:** Pancreatic peptide hormone supporting fasting glucose availability.

## Source

Pancreatic alpha cells.

## Main trigger(s)

Low glucose, amino acids and autonomic inputs; secretion is restrained by insulin/somatostatin and high glucose.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Glucagon receptor, especially liver.  
**Canonical signalling:** Gs → cAMP/PKA.

## Main physiological effects

Hepatic glycogenolysis, gluconeogenesis and support of ketogenesis during fasting.

## Feedback / shut-off

Rising glucose/insulin suppress glucagon.
