# Leptin

**Identity:** Adipocyte-derived signal of long-term energy stores and nutritional sufficiency.

## Source

Adipocytes.

## Main trigger(s)

Secretion broadly correlates with fat mass and nutritional state.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Leptin receptor (Ob-Rb).  
**Canonical signalling:** JAK2/STAT3 plus PI3K and other pathways.

## Main physiological effects

Acts in hypothalamus to suppress orexigenic NPY/AgRP tone, support POMC pathways, and signal energy sufficiency to endocrine/reproductive systems.

## Feedback / shut-off

Weight loss lowers leptin, triggering adaptive hunger/endocrine responses.
