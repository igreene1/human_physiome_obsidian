# Glucocorticoid Receptor

**Role:** Intracellular steroid receptor NR3C1 activated by cortisol.

## Mechanism

Cortisol diffuses into cells, binds cytosolic/nuclear receptor complexes, changes receptor interactions with DNA and other transcription factors.

## Main consequence

Alters transcription over minutes to hours; effects depend strongly on cell type.
