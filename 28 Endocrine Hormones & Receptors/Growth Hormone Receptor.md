# Growth Hormone Receptor

**Role:** Class I cytokine-receptor-family receptor.

## Mechanism

GH binding promotes receptor rearrangement/activation of associated JAK2 → STAT and other pathways.

## Main consequence

Drives tissue-specific growth/metabolic gene programmes and IGF-1 production.
