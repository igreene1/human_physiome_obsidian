# Testosterone

**Identity:** Principal circulating androgen from testes.

## Source

Leydig cells; smaller adrenal/ovarian contributions.

## Main trigger(s)

LH.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Androgen receptor; some testosterone converts to DHT or estradiol.  
**Canonical signalling:** Nuclear receptor transcription.

## Main physiological effects

Male reproductive differentiation/function, anabolic effects, libido and feedback on HPG axis.

## Feedback / shut-off

Negative feedback on hypothalamus/pituitary.
