# GnRH Receptor

**Role:** Gq/11-coupled GPCR on anterior-pituitary gonadotrophs.

## Mechanism

Pulsatile GnRH → PLC/IP3/DAG/Ca²⁺ signalling → LH/FSH synthesis and secretion.

## Main consequence

Pulse frequency/pattern influences gonadotropin output; continuous stimulation produces receptor desensitisation.
