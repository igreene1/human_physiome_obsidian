# V1A Receptor

**Role:** Gq-coupled vasopressin receptor on vascular smooth muscle and other tissues.

## Mechanism

Vasopressin → V1A → PLC/IP3/Ca²⁺ → smooth-muscle contraction.

## Main consequence

Contributes to vasoconstriction, especially when vasopressin levels are high.
