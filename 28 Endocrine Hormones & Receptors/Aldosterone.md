# Aldosterone

**Identity:** Mineralocorticoid steroid controlling distal-nephron sodium retention and K⁺/H⁺ secretion.

## Source

Adrenal zona glomerulosa.

## Main trigger(s)

Angiotensin II and elevated extracellular K⁺; ACTH has a minor transient influence.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Mineralocorticoid receptor.  
**Canonical signalling:** Nuclear receptor transcription.

## Main physiological effects

Increases ENaC and Na-K ATPase-related sodium reabsorption and supports K⁺ secretion in principal cells.

## Feedback / shut-off

Volume/pressure restoration suppresses renin/angiotensin; potassium lowering reduces direct stimulus.
