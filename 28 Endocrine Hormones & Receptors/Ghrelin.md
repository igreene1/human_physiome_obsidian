# Ghrelin

**Identity:** Stomach-derived peptide that signals energy deficit and stimulates appetite and GH release.

## Source

Primarily gastric endocrine cells.

## Main trigger(s)

Rises before meals/with fasting in many contexts.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Growth hormone secretagogue receptor GHSR.  
**Canonical signalling:** Gq-family signalling.

## Main physiological effects

Promotes feeding and can stimulate GH secretion.

## Feedback / shut-off

Falls after feeding in many contexts.
