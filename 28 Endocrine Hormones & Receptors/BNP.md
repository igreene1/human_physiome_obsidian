# BNP

**Identity:** B-type natriuretic peptide released predominantly from ventricular myocardium with wall stress.

## Source

Cardiomyocytes.

## Main trigger(s)

Increased myocardial wall stress/stretch.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Natriuretic peptide receptor-A.  
**Canonical signalling:** cGMP/PKG.

## Main physiological effects

Natriuretic, vasodilatory and RAAS-opposing actions; clinically used as a biomarker of cardiac wall stress.

## Feedback / shut-off

Changes with cardiac loading conditions.
