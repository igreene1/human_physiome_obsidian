# ANP

**Identity:** Atrial natriuretic peptide released in response to atrial stretch.

## Source

Atrial cardiomyocytes.

## Main trigger(s)

Increased atrial wall stretch/volume.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Natriuretic peptide receptor-A / particulate guanylyl cyclase.  
**Canonical signalling:** cGMP/PKG.

## Main physiological effects

Promotes natriuresis/diuresis, vasodilation and suppression of renin/aldosterone.

## Feedback / shut-off

Reduced stretch lowers secretion.
