# Estradiol

**Identity:** Major biologically active estrogen during reproductive years.

## Source

Ovarian granulosa cells and other tissues via aromatisation.

## Main trigger(s)

FSH/LH-driven ovarian steroidogenesis; peripheral aromatase.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Estrogen receptors ERα/ERβ.  
**Canonical signalling:** Nuclear receptor transcription plus membrane-associated signalling.

## Main physiological effects

Reproductive tract function, bone maintenance, CNS/cardiovascular effects and feedback on HPG axis.

## Feedback / shut-off

Usually negative feedback; sustained high pre-ovulatory estradiol can switch to positive feedback generating LH surge.
