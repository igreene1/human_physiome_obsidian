# Calcitriol

**Identity:** 1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D; active vitamin-D hormone.

## Source

Kidney proximal tubule from 25-hydroxyvitamin D.

## Main trigger(s)

PTH stimulates renal 1α-hydroxylase; phosphate/FGF23 and feedback regulate production.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Vitamin D receptor.  
**Canonical signalling:** Nuclear receptor transcription.

## Main physiological effects

Increases intestinal calcium/phosphate absorption; participates in bone and parathyroid regulation.

## Feedback / shut-off

Calcitriol and FGF23 restrain production; metabolism inactivates hormone.
