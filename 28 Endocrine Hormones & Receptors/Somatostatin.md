# Somatostatin

**Identity:** Inhibitory peptide hormone/neuropeptide.

## Source

Hypothalamus, pancreatic delta cells and GI endocrine cells.

## Main trigger(s)

Nutrient/hormonal/neural context.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Somatostatin receptors SSTR1–5.  
**Canonical signalling:** Mostly Gi/o → ↓ cAMP and secretion.

## Main physiological effects

Inhibits GH and TSH; locally suppresses pancreatic/GI hormone secretion.

## Feedback / shut-off

Short-lived peptide; local feedback loops.
