# Vasopressin ADH

**Identity:** Arginine vasopressin; peptide hormone controlling water conservation and participating in vascular/stress regulation.

## Source

Synthesised mainly in supraoptic/paraventricular hypothalamic neurons; released from posterior pituitary.

## Main trigger(s)

Rising plasma osmolality; substantial fall in effective circulating volume/pressure.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** V2 receptors in kidney; V1A receptors in vascular smooth muscle and other tissues.  
**Canonical signalling:** V2: Gs/cAMP/PKA; V1A: Gq/Ca²⁺.

## Main physiological effects

AQP2 insertion and water reabsorption; at higher concentrations vasoconstriction; also supports ACTH axis via V1B receptors.

## Feedback / shut-off

Falling osmolality and restored volume suppress secretion.
