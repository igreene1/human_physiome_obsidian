# T3 Triiodothyronine

**Identity:** More transcriptionally active thyroid hormone.

## Source

Thyroid plus peripheral deiodination of T4.

## Main trigger(s)

T4 availability and deiodinase activity.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Thyroid hormone receptor.  
**Canonical signalling:** Nuclear receptor binding to DNA response elements with co-regulators.

## Main physiological effects

Increases expression of metabolic, developmental and adrenergic-response programmes in many tissues.

## Feedback / shut-off

Negative feedback reduces TRH/TSH.
