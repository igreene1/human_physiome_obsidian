# Glucagon Receptor

**Role:** Gs-coupled GPCR highly relevant in liver.

## Mechanism

Glucagon → receptor → adenylyl cyclase → cAMP → PKA → phosphorylation/transcriptional changes.

## Main consequence

Promotes hepatic glucose production and fasting fuel mobilisation.
