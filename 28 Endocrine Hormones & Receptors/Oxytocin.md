# Oxytocin

**Identity:** Hypothalamic peptide released from posterior pituitary and centrally.

## Source

Paraventricular/supraoptic neurons.

## Main trigger(s)

Cervical/uterine stretch and suckling; social/sensory inputs centrally.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Oxytocin receptor.  
**Canonical signalling:** Gq/PLC/IP3/Ca²⁺.

## Main physiological effects

Uterine contraction and milk ejection; central behavioural effects.

## Feedback / shut-off

Positive-feedback loops can occur during labour and suckling episodes.
