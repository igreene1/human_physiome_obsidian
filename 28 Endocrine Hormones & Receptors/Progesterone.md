# Progesterone

**Identity:** Steroid hormone central to luteal-phase and pregnancy physiology.

## Source

Corpus luteum; placenta during pregnancy.

## Main trigger(s)

LH supports corpus luteum; placental steroidogenesis later.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Progesterone receptor.  
**Canonical signalling:** Nuclear receptor transcription.

## Main physiological effects

Transforms endometrium for implantation, supports pregnancy, alters thermoregulation and feeds back on GnRH/LH.

## Feedback / shut-off

Metabolic clearance mainly hepatic; withdrawal contributes to menstruation when pregnancy does not occur.
