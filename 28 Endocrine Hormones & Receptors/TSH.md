# TSH

**Identity:** Thyroid-stimulating hormone.

## Source

Anterior-pituitary thyrotrophs.

## Main trigger(s)

TRH; inhibited by circulating thyroid hormone/somatostatin.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** TSH receptor on thyroid follicular cells.  
**Canonical signalling:** Gs/cAMP/PKA with additional pathways.

## Main physiological effects

Iodide uptake, thyroid-hormone synthesis/release and trophic effects.

## Feedback / shut-off

T3/T4 negative feedback.
