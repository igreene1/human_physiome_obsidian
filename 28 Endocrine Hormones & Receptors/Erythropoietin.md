# Erythropoietin

**Identity:** Glycoprotein hormone controlling red-cell production.

## Source

Renal interstitial fibroblast-like EPO-producing cells.

## Main trigger(s)

Reduced renal tissue oxygen availability via HIF signalling.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Erythropoietin receptor on erythroid progenitors.  
**Canonical signalling:** JAK2/STAT5.

## Main physiological effects

Promotes survival, proliferation and differentiation of erythroid precursors.

## Feedback / shut-off

Improved oxygen-carrying capacity reduces hypoxic drive.
