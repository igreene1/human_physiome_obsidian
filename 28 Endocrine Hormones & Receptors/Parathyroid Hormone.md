# Parathyroid Hormone

**Identity:** PTH; principal rapid endocrine defender of ionised extracellular calcium.

## Source

Parathyroid chief cells.

## Main trigger(s)

Low ionised Ca²⁺ sensed by CaSR; phosphate/vitamin-D context modifies secretion.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** PTH1 receptor in bone/kidney target cells.  
**Canonical signalling:** Gs/cAMP and Gq pathways.

## Main physiological effects

Raises renal Ca²⁺ reabsorption, lowers phosphate reabsorption, stimulates calcitriol production and indirectly promotes bone resorption when sustained.

## Feedback / shut-off

Rising Ca²⁺ and calcitriol suppress PTH.
