# Growth Hormone

**Identity:** Pituitary peptide hormone coordinating growth and nutrient partitioning.

## Source

Anterior-pituitary somatotrophs.

## Main trigger(s)

GHRH stimulates; somatostatin inhibits; ghrelin can stimulate; secretion is pulsatile.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** Growth-hormone receptor.  
**Canonical signalling:** JAK2/STAT and other pathways.

## Main physiological effects

Stimulates hepatic/tissue IGF-1, protein anabolism and lipolysis; has anti-insulin metabolic effects in some contexts.

## Feedback / shut-off

IGF-1 and GH feed back to hypothalamus/pituitary.
