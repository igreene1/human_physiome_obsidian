# Oxytocin Receptor

**Role:** Gq-coupled GPCR in uterine myometrium, mammary myoepithelium and other tissues.

## Mechanism

Oxytocin → PLC/IP3/Ca²⁺ signalling.

## Main consequence

Drives uterine and myoepithelial contraction.
