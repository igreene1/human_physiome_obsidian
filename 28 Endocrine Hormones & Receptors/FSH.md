# FSH

**Identity:** Follicle-stimulating hormone.

## Source

Anterior-pituitary gonadotrophs.

## Main trigger(s)

GnRH; activin/inhibin modulation.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** FSH receptor on granulosa and Sertoli cells.  
**Canonical signalling:** Gs/cAMP/PKA.

## Main physiological effects

Follicular development/aromatase activity; support of spermatogenesis.

## Feedback / shut-off

Inhibin selectively suppresses FSH; sex-steroid feedback also contributes.
