# Angiotensin II

**Identity:** Key effector peptide of RAAS.

## Source

Generated from angiotensin I mainly by ACE.

## Main trigger(s)

Renin activity determines upstream generation.

## Receptor / signalling

**Receptor:** AT1 receptor for most classic homeostatic actions.  
**Canonical signalling:** Gq and additional signalling.

## Main physiological effects

Vasoconstriction, aldosterone stimulation, thirst/ADH facilitation and renal sodium-retaining effects.

## Feedback / shut-off

Restoration of pressure/volume suppresses renin; angiotensin II directly restrains renin.
