# Protoporphyrin IX Formation - PPOX

**Part of:** [[Heme Synthesis and Degradation]]

## Transformation

Protoporphyrinogen IX → protoporphyrin IX by oxidation

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | PPOX; FAD-dependent enzyme |
| Compartment | Inner mitochondrial membrane-associated |
| Physiological direction | Creates the conjugated porphyrin structure before iron insertion |

## What controls this step

Produces oxidant by-products that must be managed. The product is a porphyrin without iron; it is not yet heme.
