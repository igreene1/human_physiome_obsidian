# Aldosterone Final Reactions - CYP11B2

**Part of:** [[Steroid Hormone Synthesis]]

## Transformation

Deoxycorticosterone → corticosterone → 18-hydroxycorticosterone → aldosterone

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | CYP11B2 (aldosterone synthase) |
| Compartment | Adrenal zona-glomerulosa mitochondria |
| Physiological direction | Performs successive oxidation steps in the terminal mineralocorticoid branch |

## What controls this step

Angiotensin II and extracellular potassium are major physiological regulators of aldosterone output. This sequence is a multi-reaction module, not one elementary chemical event.
