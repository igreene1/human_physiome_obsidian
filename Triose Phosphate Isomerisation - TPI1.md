# Triose Phosphate Isomerisation - TPI1

**Part of:** [[Glycolysis]]

## Transformation

Dihydroxyacetone phosphate ⇌ glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | TPI1 |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Allows both triose products to continue through the same lower glycolytic sequence |

## What controls this step

The equilibrium favours dihydroxyacetone phosphate; consumption of glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate by GAPDH supports forward net glycolytic flux.
