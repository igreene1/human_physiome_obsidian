# Splenic Blood Filtration

**Function:** Removes abnormal blood cells and supports responses to blood-borne antigens.

**Where:** Splenic red pulp, marginal regions and white-pulp lymphoid tissue.

## Mechanism

1. Blood passes through specialised splenic microcirculation.
2. Mechanical/recognition processes retain poorly deformable or altered erythrocytes.
3. Macrophages recover iron and process cellular material.
4. Lymphoid compartments coordinate responses to suitable blood-borne antigens.

## Control and constraints

The spleen does not filter lymph in the same manner as a lymph node. Red-cell quality control, iron recycling and immune surveillance coexist in different compartments.
