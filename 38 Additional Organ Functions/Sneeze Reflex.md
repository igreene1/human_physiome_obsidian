# Sneeze Reflex

**Function:** Expels irritants from upper nasal passages.

**Where:** Nasal trigeminal afferents and coordinated respiratory/upper-airway muscles.

## Mechanism

1. Nasal irritation activates sensory endings.
2. Brainstem patterning recruits an inspiratory preparatory phase.
3. Coordinated upper-airway and expiratory muscle activity creates rapid expulsion.
4. Reflex completion and removal of the irritant reduce drive.

## Control and constraints

This differs from the predominantly vagal airway inputs of cough. Nasal secretion and vascular responses may accompany sneezing through additional autonomic pathways.
