# Defecation Reflex

**Function:** Coordinates rectal filling signals with voluntary and autonomic outlet control.

**Where:** Rectum, anal sphincters, pelvic floor and sacral/central circuits.

## Mechanism

1. Rectal distension recruits afferent signals and reflex internal-sphincter relaxation.
2. External sphincter and puborectalis activity can defer expulsion.
3. When permitted, pelvic-floor/outlet relaxation accompanies propulsive rectal/colonic activity.
4. Abdominal pressure and posture can assist expulsion.

## Control and constraints

Internal sphincter smooth muscle and external sphincter skeletal muscle have different control. Continence relies on sensation, reservoir function, consistency and coordination, not one sphincter alone.
