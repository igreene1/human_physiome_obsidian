# Hepatic Zonation

**Function:** Distributes liver metabolic work along spatial gradients.

**Where:** Periportal-to-pericentral hepatocyte organisation.

## Mechanism

1. Portal/arterial blood supplies oxygen, substrates and signals to the lobule.
2. Gradients and local Wnt/other niche signals establish different enzyme programmes.
3. Periportal regions favour selected oxidative, ureagenic and gluconeogenic functions, while pericentral regions favour other programmes such as selected xenobiotic metabolism and glutamine synthesis.
4. Blood leaving regions carries altered substrate composition downstream.

## Control and constraints

Zonation is dynamic, pathway-specific and not an absolute binary split. Human disease and nutritional state can remodel it; a whole-liver average can hide regional differences.
