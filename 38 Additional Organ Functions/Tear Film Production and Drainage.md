# Tear Film Production and Drainage

**Function:** Maintains an optically smooth, lubricated and defended ocular surface.

**Where:** Lacrimal glands, goblet cells, meibomian glands, lids and drainage ducts.

## Mechanism

1. Lacrimal secretion contributes water, electrolytes and proteins.
2. Mucins support wetting and lipid components reduce evaporation/shape the film.
3. Blinking spreads and redistributes tears over corneal/conjunctival surfaces.
4. Puncta and nasolacrimal pathways drain excess fluid.

## Control and constraints

The film is a dynamic mixed structure rather than three perfectly separate static sheets. Surface sensation and autonomic reflexes influence secretion and blinking.
