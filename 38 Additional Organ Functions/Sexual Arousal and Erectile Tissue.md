# Sexual Arousal and Erectile Tissue

**Function:** Changes genital blood flow and tissue engorgement in response to integrated signals.

**Where:** Autonomic/sensory circuits and erectile vascular tissues.

## Mechanism

1. Central and peripheral inputs recruit nitrergic and other vasodilator signals.
2. NO activates soluble guanylyl cyclase and cGMP-associated smooth-muscle relaxation.
3. Arterial inflow increases and tissue expansion alters venous drainage in relevant structures.
4. PDE5-mediated cGMP breakdown and constrictor signals contribute to reversal.

## Control and constraints

Mechanisms apply with anatomical differences across genital tissues. Subjective desire, lubrication, engorgement and consent are distinct; a physiological response does not establish a person's wishes.
