# Aqueous Humor Dynamics

**Function:** Maintains the anterior eye's fluid environment and pressure balance.

**Where:** Ciliary epithelium, posterior/anterior chambers and drainage pathways.

## Mechanism

1. Ciliary processes secrete aqueous humour through ion transport and water movement.
2. Fluid passes around the lens through the pupil into the anterior chamber.
3. Trabecular/Schlemm-canal and uveoscleral routes provide outflow.
4. Production and outflow resistance determine pressure together.

## Control and constraints

Aqueous humour is distinct from vitreous gel and tear fluid. Normal pressure does not capture every aspect of ocular tissue health; this note describes fluid mechanics, not diagnosis.
