# Lymph Node Antigen Trafficking

**Function:** Brings antigen and immune cells together for surveillance and adaptive activation.

**Where:** Afferent lymphatics, lymph-node sinuses, cortex/paracortex and high endothelial venules.

## Mechanism

1. Tissue dendritic cells and soluble antigens reach draining nodes through lymph.
2. Chemokine gradients organise T-cell and B-cell regions.
3. Naive lymphocytes enter through specialised venules and scan presented antigens.
4. Activated cells differentiate and exit by regulated routes.

## Control and constraints

Cell entry/exit uses receptor and adhesion programmes, including S1P-related egress control. Nodes filter lymph, whereas spleen samples blood through a different architecture.
