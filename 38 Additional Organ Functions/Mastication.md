# Mastication

**Function:** Breaks and mixes food mechanically before swallowing.

**Where:** Jaw muscles, teeth, temporomandibular joints and brainstem/cortical circuits.

## Mechanism

1. Voluntary initiation recruits rhythmic brainstem patterning.
2. Jaw-muscle contractions move food against opposing teeth.
3. Periodontal, muscle and mucosal feedback adjusts force and timing.
4. Tongue/cheek movements mix particles with saliva and form a bolus.

## Control and constraints

Chewing force must adapt rapidly to texture and tooth contact. Mechanical processing increases accessible surface area but does not replace chemical digestion.
