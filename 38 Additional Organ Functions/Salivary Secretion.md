# Salivary Secretion

**Function:** Lubricates oral contents, begins selected digestion and protects oral tissues.

**Where:** Salivary acini, ducts and autonomic inputs.

## Mechanism

1. Acinar ion secretion draws water into a primary fluid.
2. Ducts modify sodium, chloride, potassium and bicarbonate composition.
3. Parasympathetic and sympathetic signals alter volume, protein content and gland perfusion.
4. Saliva delivers mucins, enzymes and antimicrobial components to the mouth.

## Control and constraints

Both autonomic divisions can stimulate secretion with different patterns; sympathetic activity is not simply an off switch. High flow permits less complete ductal modification.
