# Protein Phosphorylation and Dephosphorylation

**Function:** Reversibly changes protein interactions or activity using phosphate transfer.

**Where:** Cell-type-specific kinase/phosphatase networks.

## Mechanism

1. A protein kinase transfers phosphate from ATP to a suitable residue in a substrate context.
2. Charge/conformation changes alter binding, catalytic activity, localisation or stability.
3. Binding domains can recognise specific phosphorylated motifs.
4. Phosphatases remove phosphate to reset or redirect the state.

## Control and constraints

Phosphorylation can activate or inhibit; its sign cannot be inferred without the substrate/site. Kinases are not always active merely because their own phosphorylation increases.
