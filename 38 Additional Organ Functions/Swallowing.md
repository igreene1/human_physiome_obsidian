# Swallowing

**Function:** Transfers a bolus from mouth to stomach while protecting the airway.

**Where:** Oral, pharyngeal and oesophageal muscle with brainstem patterning.

## Mechanism

1. The oral phase positions and propels the bolus toward the pharynx.
2. Sensory input recruits an ordered pharyngeal programme with airway closure and upper-sphincter relaxation.
3. Pharyngeal contraction moves the bolus into the oesophagus.
4. Primary/secondary peristalsis and lower-sphincter relaxation support delivery to stomach.

## Control and constraints

Airway protection involves coordinated laryngeal elevation/closure, not the epiglottis alone. Voluntary and involuntary components interact; oesophageal muscle control differs along its length.
