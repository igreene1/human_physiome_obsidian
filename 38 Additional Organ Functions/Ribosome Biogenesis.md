# Ribosome Biogenesis

**Function:** Builds functional ribosomal subunits from rRNA and proteins.

**Where:** Nucleolus, nucleus and cytosol.

## Mechanism

1. RNA polymerase I makes a major pre-rRNA transcript; polymerase III supplies 5S rRNA.
2. Processing and snoRNP-guided modifications mature rRNA segments.
3. Imported ribosomal proteins assemble with rRNA through quality-controlled intermediates.
4. Pre-subunits are exported and complete maturation before productive translation.

## Control and constraints

Ribosome assembly is energy-intensive and coordinated with growth signals. Mitochondrial ribosomes have distinct components and biogenesis routes.
