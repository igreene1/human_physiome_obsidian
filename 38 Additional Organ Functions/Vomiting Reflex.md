# Vomiting Reflex

**Function:** Coordinates protective expulsion of upper-gut contents.

**Where:** Brainstem networks integrating area-postrema, visceral, vestibular and forebrain inputs.

## Mechanism

1. Emetic signals recruit distributed brainstem patterning.
2. Autonomic changes and upper-gut relaxation/motility changes prepare the tract.
3. Airway-protective and respiratory-muscle patterns are coordinated.
4. Abdominal/diaphragmatic forces and outlet changes expel contents when the programme is completed.

## Control and constraints

Nausea and vomiting are related but separable. No single isolated vomiting centre accounts for every trigger; area postrema is important for blood-borne signals but is not the only entry route.
