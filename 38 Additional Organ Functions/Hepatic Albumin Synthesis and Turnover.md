# Hepatic Albumin Synthesis and Turnover

**Function:** Maintains a major circulating binding protein and contributor to plasma oncotic pressure.

**Where:** Hepatocyte secretory pathway, plasma and catabolic tissues.

## Mechanism

1. Hepatocytes transcribe ALB and translate a secretory precursor.
2. ER/Golgi processing and secretion deliver albumin into blood.
3. Albumin binds multiple endogenous/exogenous ligands and distributes across vascular/extravascular pools.
4. Recycling and degradation determine persistence alongside new synthesis.

## Control and constraints

Plasma albumin concentration reflects inflammation, distribution, fluid balance and losses as well as synthesis. It is not a direct isolated measure of protein intake or liver production rate.
