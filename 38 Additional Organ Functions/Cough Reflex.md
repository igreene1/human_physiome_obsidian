# Cough Reflex

**Function:** Clears airway material through a coordinated high-flow expiratory event.

**Where:** Airway sensory afferents, brainstem and respiratory/laryngeal muscles.

## Mechanism

1. Mechanical/chemical airway stimuli activate relevant vagal afferents.
2. Inspiratory preparation increases lung volume.
3. Glottic closure with expiratory muscle contraction builds pressure in a canonical cough.
4. Rapid opening produces high flow that mobilises material.

## Control and constraints

Cough can be voluntarily modified and not every cough has identical phases. Sensory gain and mucus properties influence effectiveness; mucociliary clearance remains a parallel defence.
