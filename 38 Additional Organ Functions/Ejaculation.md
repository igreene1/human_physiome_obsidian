# Ejaculation

**Function:** Coordinates seminal emission and rhythmic expulsion.

**Where:** Male reproductive ducts/glands, urethra, pelvic muscles and spinal/brain circuits.

## Mechanism

1. Emission recruits autonomic smooth-muscle activity to move sperm and glandular secretions into the urethral pathway.
2. Bladder-neck/outlet coordination limits retrograde flow.
3. Somatic rhythmic pelvic-muscle activity contributes to expulsion.
4. Refractory and feedback changes reshape subsequent response.

## Control and constraints

Emission, expulsion and orgasm are related but separable. Sperm production and seminal-fluid production come from different structures and timescales.
