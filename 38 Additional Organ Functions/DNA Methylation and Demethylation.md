# DNA Methylation and Demethylation

**Function:** Changes selected cytosine states to regulate genome function and developmental identity.

**Where:** Nuclear DNA and chromatin.

## Mechanism

1. DNMT3-family enzymes establish many new methylation patterns.
2. DNMT1-associated machinery helps copy methylation after replication.
3. TET enzymes oxidise methylcytosine, enabling dilution or repair-linked removal routes.
4. Reader proteins and transcriptional context translate patterns into functional effects.

## Control and constraints

Promoter-associated methylation often correlates with repression, but function depends on genomic location. DNA methylation is not equivalent to histone methylation and does not change the DNA base sequence into a new genetic code.
