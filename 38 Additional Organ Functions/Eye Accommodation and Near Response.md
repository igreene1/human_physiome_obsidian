# Eye Accommodation and Near Response

**Function:** Adjusts optical focus for nearby objects.

**Where:** Ciliary muscle, zonules, lens and near-response neural circuits.

## Mechanism

1. Near-target processing recruits parasympathetic drive to ciliary muscle.
2. Ciliary contraction reduces zonular tension, allowing the lens to become more convex.
3. Convergence aligns the eyes and pupillary constriction can increase depth of focus.
4. Distance viewing reduces accommodative demand and changes lens tension.

## Control and constraints

Accommodation depends on lens biomechanics as well as neural drive. The near response is not identical to the light reflex, although both use overlapping parasympathetic output structures.
