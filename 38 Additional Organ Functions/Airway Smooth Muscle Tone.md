# Airway Smooth Muscle Tone

**Function:** Modulates conducting-airway calibre and resistance.

**Where:** Bronchial smooth muscle, epithelium and autonomic/local mediators.

## Mechanism

1. Vagal acetylcholine activates M3-calcium pathways to promote contraction.
2. Beta2 receptor signalling increases cAMP-associated relaxation mechanisms.
3. Inflammatory mediators and local epithelial signals alter calcium and sensitivity.
4. Changing radius modifies resistance and regional ventilation.

## Control and constraints

Airway calibre also depends on lung volume, tethering, mucus and wall structure. Circulating adrenaline and drugs can activate beta2 receptors; direct sympathetic innervation is not the sole or dominant control everywhere.
