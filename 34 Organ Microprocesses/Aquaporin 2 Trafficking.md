# Aquaporin 2 Trafficking

**Function:** Changes collecting-duct water permeability rapidly in response to ADH.

**Where:** Principal-cell apical membrane and intracellular vesicles.

## Mechanism

1. Vasopressin activates basolateral V2 receptors and Gs-adenylyl-cyclase signalling.
2. cAMP/PKA-associated phosphorylation alters AQP2 trafficking.
3. AQP2-containing vesicles add channels to the apical membrane.
4. Water moves down an osmotic gradient and exits basolaterally through other aquaporins.

## Control and constraints

AQP2 is a passive water channel, not an ATP-driven pump. Longer exposure alters abundance; withdrawal permits retrieval. A medullary osmotic gradient is required for net concentrating benefit.
