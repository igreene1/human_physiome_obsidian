# Bile Acid Synthesis and Feedback

**Function:** Converts cholesterol into bile acids while matching synthesis to return from intestine.

**Where:** Hepatic ER/mitochondria/peroxisomes and enterohepatic signalling.

## Mechanism

1. CYP7A1 begins the major classical synthetic route; alternative routes also contribute.
2. Additional hydroxylation, side-chain processing and conjugation generate bile salts.
3. Intestinal reabsorption returns most secreted bile acids to liver.
4. Ileal FXR induces FGF19 and hepatic signalling restrains new synthesis; hepatic FXR contributes local feedback.

## Control and constraints

Human bile-acid composition and FGF19 physiology differ from common mouse bile-acid/FGF15 models. Bile acids also act as signalling molecules.
