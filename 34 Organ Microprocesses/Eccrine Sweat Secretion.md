# Eccrine Sweat Secretion

**Function:** Generates hypotonic sweat under thermoregulatory sympathetic control.

**Where:** Eccrine secretory coil and reabsorptive duct.

## Mechanism

1. Acetylcholine activates M3-linked calcium signalling in secretory cells.
2. Basolateral ion uptake and apical anion secretion support primary fluid generation.
3. Sodium and water follow, producing relatively salt-rich primary fluid.
4. Ductal sodium/chloride reabsorption lowers final salt content without equivalent water reabsorption.

## Control and constraints

High flow reduces time for reabsorption. CFTR participates in sweat-duct chloride handling, whose direction/context differs from airway secretion. Evaporation determines actual cooling.
