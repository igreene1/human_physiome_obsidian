# Conjugation and Xenobiotic Export

**Function:** Adds selected chemical groups and transports products toward elimination.

**Where:** Cytosol, ER and plasma membranes of liver/kidney/intestine.

## Mechanism

1. UGTs, sulfotransferases, glutathione transferases and other enzymes conjugate suitable substrates.
2. Cofactors such as UDP-glucuronic acid, PAPS and glutathione provide donor groups.
3. ABC/SLC transport systems export or exchange products according to tissue polarity.
4. Bile or urine elimination depends on transport, flow and further handling.

## Control and constraints

Conjugation is not uniformly detoxifying, and some conjugates are deconjugated in the intestine. Phase I is not mandatory before every phase II reaction.
