# Enterocyte Nutrient Transport

**Function:** Moves digested nutrients vectorially from lumen toward blood or lymph.

**Where:** Apical brush border, cytosol and basolateral enterocyte membrane.

## Mechanism

1. SGLT1 imports glucose/galactose with Na+; GLUT5 supports fructose uptake.
2. Amino-acid carriers and H+-coupled PEPT1 import nitrogen-containing substrates.
3. Basolateral Na-K ATPase and carriers maintain gradients and export soluble nutrients.
4. Long-chain lipid products are repackaged and routed into lymph as chylomicrons.

## Control and constraints

Transporter localisation matters as much as identity. Different substrates compete or saturate differently; lipid transport follows a different intracellular route from most monosaccharides.
