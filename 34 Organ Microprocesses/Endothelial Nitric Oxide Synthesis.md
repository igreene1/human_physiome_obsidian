# Endothelial Nitric Oxide Synthesis

**Function:** Couples endothelial stimulation to local smooth-muscle relaxation.

**Where:** Endothelial NOS3 and neighbouring vascular smooth muscle.

## Mechanism

1. Shear stress or receptor signalling recruits NOS3 activity through calcium/calmodulin and phosphorylation.
2. NOS converts L-arginine and oxygen to NO and citrulline using cofactors including BH4 and NADPH.
3. NO diffuses to smooth-muscle soluble guanylyl cyclase.
4. cGMP/PKG-related effects lower contractile activation and promote relaxation.

## Control and constraints

NO is short-lived. Cofactor imbalance can uncouple NOS toward superoxide generation, so enzyme abundance alone does not prove NO bioavailability.
