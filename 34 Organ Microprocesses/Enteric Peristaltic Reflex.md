# Enteric Peristaltic Reflex

**Function:** Propels a local bolus through coordinated contraction and relaxation.

**Where:** Enteric sensory neurons, interneurons and circular/longitudinal smooth muscle.

## Mechanism

1. Distension and mucosal signals activate intrinsic sensory pathways.
2. Ascending excitatory pathways favour contraction on the oral side.
3. Descending inhibitory pathways using NO/VIP-associated mechanisms favour relaxation on the anal side.
4. Coordinated muscle activity advances contents while local feedback updates the pattern.

## Control and constraints

This is a useful canonical motif; actual propulsion varies by gut region and motor programme. Interstitial cells and smooth-muscle electrical state shape the response.
