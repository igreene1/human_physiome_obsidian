# Fever Prostaglandin Signalling

**Function:** Links immune activation to regulated heat-conserving/generating responses.

**Where:** Immune-to-brain signalling and preoptic thermoregulatory circuits.

## Mechanism

1. Inflammatory signals recruit prostaglandin E2 synthesis in relevant brain-associated endothelial contexts.
2. PGE2 acts on thermoregulatory receptors including EP3-linked networks.
3. Heat-conserving and heat-producing effectors are recruited relative to the changed control state.
4. As inflammatory/PGE2 drive falls, heat-loss responses can dominate.

## Control and constraints

Fever and environmental/exertional hyperthermia have different control mechanisms. A single temperature value does not identify the cause of a febrile response.
