# Cytochrome P450 Biotransformation

**Function:** Oxidises diverse endogenous and external substrates.

**Where:** Predominantly ER-associated CYP enzymes and selected mitochondrial CYPs.

## Mechanism

1. Substrate binds a CYP haem-containing active site.
2. Electron delivery from redox partners and oxygen binding support catalytic cycling.
3. Activated oxygen is inserted into or otherwise used to transform substrate.
4. Products enter further metabolism, conjugation or export.

## Control and constraints

CYPs can inactivate, activate or bioactivate compounds. CYP expression is tissue- and isoform-specific; competition and induction alter capacity. Steroidogenic mitochondrial CYPs use distinct electron-transfer partners from many ER CYPs.
