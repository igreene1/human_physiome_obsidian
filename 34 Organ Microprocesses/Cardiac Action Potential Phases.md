# Cardiac Action Potential Phases

**Function:** Organises excitation and refractoriness in a working ventricular myocyte.

**Where:** Sarcolemmal sodium, calcium and potassium channels.

## Mechanism

1. Fast Na+ influx drives phase 0, followed by early repolarising currents.
2. L-type Ca2+ entry balanced against outward currents supports the plateau.
3. Delayed K+ currents dominate repolarisation as inward currents decline.
4. IK1 supports the relatively stable resting phase.

## Control and constraints

This canonical waveform is not the SA/AV nodal waveform. Channel expression varies across regions; action-potential duration and refractory periods adapt to rate and signalling.
