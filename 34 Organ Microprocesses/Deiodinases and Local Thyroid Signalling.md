# Deiodinases and Local Thyroid Signalling

**Function:** Controls local activation/inactivation of thyroid hormones.

**Where:** Tissue-specific membrane-associated DIO enzymes.

## Mechanism

1. DIO1/DIO2 can remove an outer-ring iodine to generate active T3 from T4.
2. DIO3 removes an inner-ring iodine to inactivate T4/T3 toward other products.
3. Transporters such as MCT8 control cellular hormone access.
4. Intracellular T3 binding to nuclear receptors changes gene regulation.

## Control and constraints

Serum T4 does not fully specify intracellular T3 signalling. Deiodinases are selenoproteins with distinct locations and regulation; local hormone action can differ among tissues.
