# Epidermal Lipid Barrier

**Function:** Limits water loss through specialised intercellular lipid architecture.

**Where:** Granular keratinocyte lamellar bodies and stratum corneum.

## Mechanism

1. Lipids and processing enzymes are packaged into lamellar bodies.
2. Secretion deposits precursors between differentiating cells.
3. Extracellular processing generates ceramides, cholesterol and free-fatty-acid-rich lamellae.
4. Lipid organisation and surface acidity support low permeability and controlled shedding.

## Control and constraints

Total lipid quantity is insufficient to describe barrier quality; composition, chain structure and arrangement matter. Water-loss control and antimicrobial defence interact but are not identical.
