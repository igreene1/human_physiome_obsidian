# Cardiac Calcium Cycling

**Function:** Matches beat-by-beat force to electrical and adrenergic input.

**Where:** Cardiomyocyte dyads, SR and sarcomeres.

## Mechanism

1. CaV1.2-mediated trigger Ca2+ opens nearby RyR2 clusters.
2. SR release raises cytosolic calcium and activates troponin C.
3. SERCA2a pumps much calcium back into SR; phospholamban modulates uptake.
4. NCX and smaller routes extrude calcium to maintain balance across beats.

## Control and constraints

Calcium release depends on SR content, channel gating and local coupling. Excess spontaneous release can create depolarising NCX currents; this is a perturbation of normal cycling.
