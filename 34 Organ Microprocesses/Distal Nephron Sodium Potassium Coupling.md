# Distal Nephron Sodium Potassium Coupling

**Function:** Fine-tunes sodium uptake and potassium secretion through coupled electrical effects.

**Where:** Distal convoluted tubule, connecting tubule and collecting duct.

## Mechanism

1. NCC reabsorbs NaCl in the DCT and changes sodium delivery downstream.
2. ENaC permits electrogenic apical sodium entry in principal-like cells.
3. Basolateral Na-K ATPase loads intracellular K+ while the lumen-negative voltage favours apical K+ secretion.
4. ROMK and flow-sensitive BK pathways release K+ according to context.

## Control and constraints

Aldosterone-SGK1-NEDD4L signalling increases ENaC availability. WNK-SPAK signalling adjusts NCC with potassium status. Distal delivery/flow can be as important as aldosterone concentration.
