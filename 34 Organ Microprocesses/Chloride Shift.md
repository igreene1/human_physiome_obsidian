# Chloride Shift

**Function:** Exchanges bicarbonate and chloride during blood CO2 transport.

**Where:** Erythrocyte AE1/SLC4A1 in systemic and pulmonary capillaries.

## Mechanism

1. Tissue CO2 enters red cells and carbonic anhydrase generates bicarbonate plus H+.
2. Haemoglobin buffers much of the proton load.
3. AE1 exports bicarbonate in exchange for chloride in systemic capillaries.
4. In lungs net exchange reverses as bicarbonate is converted back to exhaled CO2.

## Control and constraints

AE1 exchange is electrically neutral. The chloride shift is distinct from the Bohr and Haldane effects, though all cooperate in respiratory gas transport.
