# Countercurrent Multiplication and Exchange

**Function:** Builds and preserves spatial osmotic gradients that support urine concentration.

**Where:** Loops of Henle, medulla and vasa recta.

## Mechanism

1. Ascending-limb salt transport creates a transverse osmotic difference without parallel water flow.
2. Countercurrent loop arrangement multiplies repeated local differences along the medullary axis.
3. Descending segments equilibrate water/solutes according to their segment-specific permeability.
4. Vasa-recta exchange carries away reabsorbed water while limiting gradient washout.

## Control and constraints

Different nephron segments and depths behave differently. Inner-medullary gradient generation is more complex and less completely settled than the standard outer-medullary model.
