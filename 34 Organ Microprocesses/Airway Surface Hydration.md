# Airway Surface Hydration

**Function:** Maintains mucus/periciliary fluid suitable for clearance.

**Where:** Airway epithelial apical/basolateral ion transport.

## Mechanism

1. CFTR and other anion pathways contribute chloride/bicarbonate secretion.
2. ENaC and other absorptive routes modify sodium/fluid movement.
3. Basolateral pumps and cotransporters sustain driving forces.
4. Osmotically coupled water movement sets surface hydration and mucus properties.

## Control and constraints

Mucin expansion depends partly on ionic and bicarbonate conditions, not just total water volume. Transport balance varies by airway region and inflammatory state.
