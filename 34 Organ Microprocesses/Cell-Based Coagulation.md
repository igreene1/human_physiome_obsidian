# Cell-Based Coagulation

**Function:** Explains clotting as local initiation, amplification and propagation on cellular surfaces.

**Where:** Tissue-factor-bearing cells and activated platelets.

## Mechanism

1. TF-FVIIa produces initial FXa and thrombin.
2. Thrombin activates platelets and cofactors V/VIII, supporting assembly on suitable phospholipid surfaces.
3. FIXa-FVIIIa tenase and FXa-FVa prothrombinase produce a thrombin burst.
4. Thrombin forms fibrin and activates XIII; inhibitors and flow limit spread.

## Control and constraints

Laboratory contact activation is not a complete model of physiological initiation. Vitamin-K-dependent Gla domains and calcium enable selected factors to bind membranes.
