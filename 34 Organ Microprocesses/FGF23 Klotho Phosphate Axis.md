# FGF23 Klotho Phosphate Axis

**Function:** Coordinates phosphate excretion and vitamin D metabolism.

**Where:** Osteocytes/osteoblasts, renal FGFR-alpha-Klotho complexes and endocrine feedback.

## Mechanism

1. Bone-derived FGF23 enters circulation in response to integrated mineral-related signals.
2. Renal receptor signalling reduces proximal sodium-phosphate transporter abundance.
3. Renal calcitriol production is restrained and inactivation pathways are promoted.
4. Reduced absorption and increased excretion help limit phosphate load.

## Control and constraints

FGF23 is not simply a direct instantaneous phosphate meter. Iron, inflammation, cleavage and other factors affect its circulating forms and actions. Klotho supports endocrine receptor specificity.
