# Bilirubin Processing

**Function:** Disposes of heme-derived pigment through conjugation and biliary excretion.

**Where:** Macrophages, plasma, hepatocytes and gut.

## Mechanism

1. Heme oxygenase opens the heme ring to biliverdin, releasing iron and carbon monoxide.
2. Biliverdin reductase forms unconjugated bilirubin, carried albumin-bound in plasma.
3. Hepatic UGT1A1 conjugates bilirubin with glucuronic acid.
4. Canalicular export and intestinal microbial metabolism lead to pigment elimination with some reabsorption.

## Control and constraints

Unconjugated and conjugated bilirubin differ in water solubility and routes of elimination. Hemolysis, conjugation and bile transport are different points in the same broader pathway.
