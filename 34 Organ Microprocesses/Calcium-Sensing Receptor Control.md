# Calcium-Sensing Receptor Control

**Function:** Transduces extracellular ionised calcium into secretion/transport responses.

**Where:** Parathyroid chief cells and several renal/other cell types.

## Mechanism

1. Extracellular calcium stabilises active CaSR signalling states.
2. G-protein pathways alter intracellular calcium, kinase and secretion machinery.
3. In parathyroids, higher calcium suppresses PTH secretion and can affect synthesis/cell growth over longer times.
4. Lower calcium relieves this restraint and increases PTH drive.

## Control and constraints

CaSR detects ionised rather than protein-bound calcium. Receptor activity is modulated by other ligands/conditions, and renal CaSR effects differ from parathyroid effects.
