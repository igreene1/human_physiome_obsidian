# Canalicular Bile Transport

**Function:** Exports selected hepatocyte products into the bile canaliculus.

**Where:** Hepatocyte apical membrane and supporting basolateral transport.

## Mechanism

1. BSEP/ABCB11 exports bile salts.
2. ABCB4 supports phosphatidylcholine delivery and ABCG5/8 export sterols.
3. MRP2/ABCC2 exports conjugated bilirubin and selected other organic anions.
4. Osmotic water movement and ductal modification contribute bile fluid.

## Control and constraints

The apical and basolateral membranes have different transport roles. Conjugation changes a substrate's handling but does not itself move it into bile.
