# Contraction-Stimulated Glucose Uptake

**Function:** Increases muscle glucose transport during activity through partly insulin-independent signalling.

**Where:** Skeletal muscle surface membranes and GLUT4 vesicles.

## Mechanism

1. Contraction changes calcium, energy charge and mechanical signals.
2. AMPK and other pathways influence trafficking regulators including TBC1D1/TBC1D4-associated systems.
3. GLUT4 moves to sarcolemmal/T-tubule membranes.
4. Glucose entry and phosphorylation support use or subsequent glycogen replenishment.

## Control and constraints

No single AMPK-only pathway explains all contraction-induced uptake. Insulin and contraction share some downstream trafficking machinery while using different upstream inputs.
