# Endothelial Barrier Regulation

**Function:** Adjusts movement of fluid, solutes and cells across vessel walls.

**Where:** Endothelial junctions, glycocalyx and cytoskeleton.

## Mechanism

1. VE-cadherin-associated junctions maintain intercellular cohesion.
2. Inflammatory or mechanical signals alter phosphorylation, trafficking and actomyosin tension.
3. Changes in junctions and transcellular pathways alter permeability.
4. Barrier-restoring signals and glycocalyx maintenance limit continued leakage.

## Control and constraints

Permeability differs among vessel beds; fenestrated and sinusoidal endothelia are specialised. Leukocyte passage and bulk fluid leakage are related but not identical events.
