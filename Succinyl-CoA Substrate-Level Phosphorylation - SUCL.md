# Succinyl-CoA Substrate-Level Phosphorylation - SUCL

**Part of:** [[TCA Cycle]]

## Transformation

Succinyl-CoA + GDP or ADP + Pi ⇌ succinate + GTP or ATP + CoA-SH

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | SUCLG1 with SUCLG2 (GDP-forming) or SUCLA2 (ADP-forming) |
| Compartment | Mitochondrial matrix |
| Physiological direction | Converts thioester energy into a nucleoside triphosphate |

## What controls this step

Tissue expression determines nucleotide specificity. This reaction does not require the electron transport chain directly, although sustained cycle flux depends on redox regeneration.
