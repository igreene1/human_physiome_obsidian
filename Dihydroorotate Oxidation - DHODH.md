# Dihydroorotate Oxidation - DHODH

**Part of:** [[Pyrimidine and Thymidylate Synthesis]]

## Transformation

Dihydroorotate + ubiquinone → orotate + ubiquinol

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | DHODH; FMN cofactor |
| Compartment | Inner mitochondrial membrane, catalytic domain toward intermembrane space |
| Physiological direction | Oxidises the pyrimidine ring and transfers electrons to the Q pool |

## What controls this step

Links de novo pyrimidine synthesis to respiratory redox capacity. It does not pump protons directly and is not respiratory complex II.
