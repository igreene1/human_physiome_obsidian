# Phosphoglycerate Rearrangement - PGAM

**Part of:** [[Glycolysis]]

## Transformation

3-Phosphoglycerate ⇌ 2-phosphoglycerate

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | PGAM1 or PGAM2; phosphohistidine intermediate |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Moves phosphate within the substrate before formation of phosphoenolpyruvate |

## What controls this step

Reversible reaction driven by metabolite concentrations. 2,3-Bisphosphoglycerate supports catalytic priming of the human cofactor-dependent enzyme.
