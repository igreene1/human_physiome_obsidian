# UDP-Glucose Formation - UGP2

**Part of:** [[Glycogen Metabolism]]

## Transformation

Glucose 1-phosphate + UTP → UDP-glucose + PPi

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | UGP2; inorganic pyrophosphatase supports forward drive |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Activates glucose for glycogen and other glycosylation pathways |

## What controls this step

PPi hydrolysis favours net activation. UDP-glucose is a shared donor and is not exclusively committed to glycogen.
