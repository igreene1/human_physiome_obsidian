# Methionine Regeneration - MTR

**Part of:** [[Folate One-Carbon Metabolism]]

## Transformation

Homocysteine + 5-methyl-THF → methionine + THF

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | MTR (methionine synthase); methylcobalamin and reactivation machinery |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Links folate one-carbon transfer with methionine/methyl-donor metabolism |

## What controls this step

Requires vitamin B12-derived cofactor; impaired activity can leave folate trapped in the 5-methyl form. Liver/kidney also have a betaine-dependent alternative remethylation route.
