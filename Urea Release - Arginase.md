# Urea Release - Arginase

**Part of:** [[Amino Acid Metabolism and Urea Cycle]]

## Transformation

Arginine + H2O → ornithine + urea

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | ARG1; manganese-dependent enzyme |
| Compartment | Cytosol of hepatocytes; ARG2 is a distinct mitochondrial isoenzyme |
| Physiological direction | Releases urea and regenerates ornithine |

## What controls this step

Urea passes into blood and is excreted predominantly by kidneys. The complete cycle consumes three ATP but four high-energy phosphate bonds per urea.
