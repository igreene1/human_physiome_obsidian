# Enoyl-CoA Hydration

**Part of:** [[Lipolysis and Beta Oxidation]]

## Transformation

Trans-2-enoyl-CoA + H2O → L-3-hydroxyacyl-CoA

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | ECHS1 or HADHA hydratase domain |
| Compartment | Mitochondrial matrix or inner-membrane-associated trifunctional protein |
| Physiological direction | Adds water to the double bond |

## What controls this step

Chain length determines enzyme usage. Unsaturated fatty acids can require auxiliary isomerase/reductase steps before rejoining the standard spiral.
