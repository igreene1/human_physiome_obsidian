# Complex I Electron Transfer and Proton Pumping

**Part of:** [[Electron Transport and Oxidative Phosphorylation]]

## Transformation

NADH electrons → FMN → iron-sulfur centres → ubiquinone; protons move matrix → intermembrane space

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | NADH dehydrogenase complex I; nuclear NDUF subunits and mitochondrial ND subunits |
| Compartment | Inner mitochondrial membrane |
| Physiological direction | Oxidises matrix NADH and pumps approximately 4 H+ per electron pair transferred to ubiquinone |

## What controls this step

Requires oxidised ubiquinone and a permissive proton-motive force. High reduction pressure can promote reactive oxygen species; reverse electron transport occurs under specific conditions.
