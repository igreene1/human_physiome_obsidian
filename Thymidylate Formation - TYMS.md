# Thymidylate Formation - TYMS

**Part of:** [[Pyrimidine and Thymidylate Synthesis]]

## Transformation

dUMP + 5,10-methylene-THF → dTMP + dihydrofolate

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | TYMS |
| Compartment | Cytosolic/nuclear-associated synthesis contexts |
| Physiological direction | Creates the methylated pyrimidine precursor used in DNA |

## What controls this step

Dihydrofolate reductase must regenerate reduced folate using NADPH. Imbalanced nucleotide supply can impair DNA replication/repair even when some pools remain adequate.
