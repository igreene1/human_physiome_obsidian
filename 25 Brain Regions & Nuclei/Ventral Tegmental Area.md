# Ventral Tegmental Area

**Role:** Midbrain dopaminergic region central to reward prediction, motivation and reinforcement learning.

## Inputs

Prefrontal, limbic, brainstem and hypothalamic inputs.

## Outputs / effects

Mesolimbic and mesocortical dopamine projections, notably to nucleus accumbens and prefrontal cortex.

## Core mechanism

Phasic dopamine signals can encode discrepancies between expected and obtained outcomes.
