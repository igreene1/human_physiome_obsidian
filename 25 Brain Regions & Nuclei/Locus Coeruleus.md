# Locus Coeruleus

**Role:** Major brainstem noradrenergic nucleus regulating arousal, attention and stress responsiveness.

## Inputs

Sensory salience, stress, sleep-state and hypothalamic inputs.

## Outputs / effects

Diffuse noradrenergic projections to cortex, limbic system, cerebellum and spinal cord.

## Core mechanism

Firing state changes cortical gain and behavioural arousal; strongly linked to wakefulness.
