# Area Postrema

**Role:** Circumventricular sensory structure in the medulla with relatively permeable blood–brain barrier.

## Inputs

Circulating hormones, toxins and metabolic signals.

## Outputs / effects

Projects to NTS and other autonomic centres; participates in nausea/vomiting and cardiovascular/endocrine responses.

## Core mechanism

Samples blood-borne signals that many brain regions cannot directly detect.
