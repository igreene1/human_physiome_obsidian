# Preoptic Area

**Role:** Anterior hypothalamic region important for thermoregulation, sleep and reproductive neuroendocrine control.

## Inputs

Temperature-sensitive neurons, circadian and hormonal signals.

## Outputs / effects

Coordinates heat-loss/heat-production responses; subsets promote sleep; contains GnRH-associated circuitry.

## Core mechanism

Compares thermal and physiological inputs and recruits autonomic/endocrine effectors.
