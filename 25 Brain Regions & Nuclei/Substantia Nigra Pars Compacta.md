# Substantia Nigra Pars Compacta

**Role:** Midbrain dopaminergic nucleus crucial for basal-ganglia motor function and reinforcement learning.

## Inputs

Basal ganglia and brainstem inputs.

## Outputs / effects

Nigrostriatal dopamine projections to the dorsal striatum.

## Core mechanism

Dopamine differentially modulates D1-like and D2-like receptor-expressing striatal pathways.
