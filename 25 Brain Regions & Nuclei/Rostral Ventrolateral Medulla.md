# Rostral Ventrolateral Medulla

**Role:** Key medullary premotor region maintaining sympathetic vasomotor tone.

## Inputs

Receives inhibitory/excitatory input from baroreflex and higher autonomic circuits.

## Outputs / effects

Excites sympathetic preganglionic neurons in the spinal cord.

## Core mechanism

Changes in activity alter vascular resistance and contribute to arterial-pressure regulation.
