# Nucleus Ambiguus

**Role:** Brainstem nucleus containing cardiovagal parasympathetic neurons and branchial motor neurons.

## Inputs

Receives reflex input from the NTS and higher centres.

## Outputs / effects

Vagal efferents slow sinoatrial and atrioventricular nodal activity.

## Core mechanism

Important in rapid beat-to-beat parasympathetic control of heart rate.
