# Basal Forebrain

**Role:** Forebrain arousal and attention system containing prominent cholinergic projections.

## Inputs

Sleep-state, limbic and brainstem inputs.

## Outputs / effects

Acetylcholine projections to cortex and hippocampus help regulate cortical activation, attention and memory.

## Core mechanism

Activity is state-dependent and interacts with orexin, histamine, monoamines and GABAergic sleep circuits.
