# Prefrontal Cortex

**Role:** Association cortex supporting executive control, working memory, planning and top-down regulation.

## Inputs

Sensory, limbic, thalamic and neuromodulatory inputs.

## Outputs / effects

Influences motor, limbic, hypothalamic and brainstem systems.

## Core mechanism

Maintains context-dependent representations and regulates behaviour through distributed networks.
