# Lateral Hypothalamic Area

**Role:** Distributed hypothalamic region involved in arousal, motivated behaviour and energy balance.

## Inputs

Metabolic, limbic and circadian inputs.

## Outputs / effects

Contains orexin/hypocretin neurons that project widely to arousal systems and autonomic centres.

## Core mechanism

Orexin signalling stabilises wakefulness and links motivational/metabolic state to arousal.
