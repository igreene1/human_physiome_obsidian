# Arcuate Nucleus

**Role:** Hypothalamic sensor and controller for energy balance and several pituitary axes.

## Inputs

Circulating leptin, insulin, ghrelin and nutrient-related signals; local neural inputs.

## Outputs / effects

POMC/CART neurons generally promote satiety; NPY/AgRP neurons promote feeding. Arcuate neuroendocrine neurons also include GHRH and tuberoinfundibular dopamine populations.

## Core mechanism

Integrates peripheral energy signals with endocrine and behavioural output.
