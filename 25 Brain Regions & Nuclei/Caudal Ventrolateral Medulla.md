# Caudal Ventrolateral Medulla

**Role:** Medullary inhibitory relay in the arterial baroreflex.

## Inputs

Excitatory input from the nucleus tractus solitarius after baroreceptor activation.

## Outputs / effects

Inhibits the rostral ventrolateral medulla, reducing sympathetic vasomotor drive.

## Core mechanism

Increased arterial pressure → increased baroreceptor firing → NTS → CVLM → inhibition of RVLM.
