# Ventromedial Hypothalamic Nucleus

**Role:** Hypothalamic node participating in energy balance, glucose sensing, autonomic regulation and reproductive behaviour.

## Inputs

Nutrient, hormonal and neural signals.

## Outputs / effects

Influences feeding, sympathetic output and endocrine responses.

## Core mechanism

Functions as one component of a distributed hypothalamic network rather than a single “satiety centre.”
