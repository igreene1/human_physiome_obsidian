# Amygdala

**Role:** Limbic network hub involved in threat/salience learning, emotion and autonomic/endocrine responses.

## Inputs

Sensory cortex/thalamus, hippocampus and prefrontal networks.

## Outputs / effects

Projects to hypothalamus and brainstem autonomic centres as well as cortical/striatal circuits.

## Core mechanism

Associates biologically relevant stimuli with affective and bodily responses.
