# Periaqueductal Gray

**Role:** Midbrain integrative region for defensive behaviour, pain modulation and autonomic patterning.

## Inputs

Cortex, amygdala, hypothalamus and ascending nociceptive inputs.

## Outputs / effects

Projects to medullary and spinal circuits controlling pain, autonomic and behavioural responses.

## Core mechanism

Coordinates context-specific defensive responses and descending analgesia.
