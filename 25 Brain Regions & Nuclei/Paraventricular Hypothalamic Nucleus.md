# Paraventricular Hypothalamic Nucleus

**Role:** Major hypothalamic integration nucleus for endocrine, autonomic and homeostatic control.

## Inputs

Receives limbic, brainstem, osmotic, metabolic and circadian information.

## Outputs / effects

Parvocellular neurons release CRH and other hypophysiotropic factors; magnocellular neurons contribute oxytocin and vasopressin; descending projections influence autonomic centres.

## Core mechanism

Links perceived/physiological state to pituitary and autonomic output.
