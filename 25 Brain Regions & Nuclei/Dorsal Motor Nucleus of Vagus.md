# Dorsal Motor Nucleus of Vagus

**Role:** Brainstem source of many preganglionic parasympathetic fibres to thoracic and abdominal viscera.

## Inputs

NTS and hypothalamic/brainstem inputs.

## Outputs / effects

Vagal output influences gastrointestinal motility/secretion and other visceral functions.

## Core mechanism

Part of vagovagal reflex circuits and longer homeostatic loops.
