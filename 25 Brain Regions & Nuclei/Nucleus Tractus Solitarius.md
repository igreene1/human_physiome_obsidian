# Nucleus Tractus Solitarius

**Role:** Major brainstem sensory nucleus for visceral afferent information.

## Inputs

Baroreceptor, chemoreceptor and visceral afferents, especially through cranial nerves IX and X.

## Outputs / effects

Projects to medullary autonomic centres and hypothalamus to adjust sympathetic and parasympathetic output.

## Core mechanism

Central relay in cardiovascular, respiratory and gastrointestinal reflexes.
