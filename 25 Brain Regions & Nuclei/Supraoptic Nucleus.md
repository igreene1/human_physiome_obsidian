# Supraoptic Nucleus

**Role:** Hypothalamic magnocellular nucleus specialised mainly for vasopressin synthesis, with some oxytocin production.

## Inputs

Osmoreceptor and volume-related signals strongly regulate activity.

## Outputs / effects

Axons project to the posterior pituitary where vasopressin/oxytocin are released into blood.

## Core mechanism

Increased plasma osmolality and major volume depletion increase vasopressin release.
