# Raphe Nuclei

**Role:** Brainstem groups containing many serotonergic neurons.

## Inputs

Widespread neural and homeostatic inputs.

## Outputs / effects

Serotonergic projections modulate mood, pain, sleep, autonomic function and other circuits.

## Core mechanism

Different raphe subdivisions project to distinct targets; serotonin acts through multiple receptor families.
