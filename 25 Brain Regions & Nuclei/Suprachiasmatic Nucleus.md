# Suprachiasmatic Nucleus

**Role:** Master circadian pacemaker in the anterior hypothalamus.

## Inputs

Light information reaches it from intrinsically photosensitive retinal ganglion cells through the retinohypothalamic tract.

## Outputs / effects

Synchronises daily rhythms in sleep–wake timing, autonomic tone, endocrine secretion and body temperature; influences melatonin timing through a multisynaptic pathway to the pineal gland.

## Core mechanism

Cell-autonomous transcription–translation feedback loops generate near-24-hour oscillations and are entrained by environmental light.
