# Median Eminence

**Role:** Neurovascular interface through which hypothalamic releasing/inhibiting hormones enter the hypophyseal portal circulation.

## Inputs

Axon terminals from hypothalamic neurosecretory neurons.

## Outputs / effects

CRH, TRH, GnRH, GHRH, somatostatin and dopamine reach the anterior pituitary through portal blood.

## Core mechanism

Allows small quantities of hypothalamic hormones to control pituitary cells without first diluting into systemic circulation.
