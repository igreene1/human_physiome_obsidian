# Thyroid Hormone Physiology

**Function:** Adjusts development, metabolic capacity and tissue responsiveness through thyroid-hormone signalling.

**Where:** Thyroid follicular cells, circulation and target tissues.

## Mechanism

1. TRH stimulates TSH; TSH receptor signalling promotes iodide handling and hormone synthesis.
2. NIS imports iodide; apical transport and thyroid peroxidase support organification/coupling on thyroglobulin.
3. Endocytosis and proteolysis release mainly T4 and some T3.
4. Transporters and deiodinases control local T3 access to nuclear thyroid receptors.

## Control and constraints

T3/T4 feedback suppresses TRH/TSH. Binding proteins affect total hormone differently from free hormone. Thyroid hormones influence gene expression and adrenergic responsiveness, rather than simply converting all calories directly to heat.
