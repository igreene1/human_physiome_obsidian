# Reproductive Endocrine Axes

**Function:** Regulates gonadal function, steroid production and gametogenesis.

**Where:** Kisspeptin/GnRH networks, gonadotrophs and gonads.

## Mechanism

1. Pulsatile GnRH stimulates LH and FSH secretion.
2. Gonadotropins act on complementary gonadal cell populations to coordinate steroids and gametes.
3. Sex steroids and inhibins provide feedback with sex- and cycle-dependent patterns.
4. In the ovarian cycle, sustained high estradiol in the appropriate context promotes positive feedback and an LH surge.

## Control and constraints

Continuous GnRH can downregulate pituitary responses. FSH and LH have distinct cellular targets; testosterone alone is not the entire spermatogenic support system. Reproductive output depends on energy balance and life stage.
