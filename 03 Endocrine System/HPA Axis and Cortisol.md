# HPA Axis and Cortisol

**Function:** Coordinates a pulsatile, circadian glucocorticoid response to physiological demand.

**Where:** Hypothalamic PVN, pituitary corticotrophs and adrenal zona fasciculata.

## Mechanism

1. CRH, with vasopressin modulation, drives corticotroph POMC processing and ACTH release.
2. ACTH activates MC2R signalling and promotes adrenal cholesterol delivery and steroid synthesis.
3. Cortisol enters blood and acts through intracellular glucocorticoid receptors in many tissues.
4. Feedback at pituitary, hypothalamic and other brain sites restrains further activation.

## Control and constraints

Cortisol affects fuel availability, vascular responsiveness and immune regulation. Time of day, pulses and binding proteins affect measured values. Tissue 11beta-HSD enzymes alter local glucocorticoid exposure.
