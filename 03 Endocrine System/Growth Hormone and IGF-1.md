# Growth Hormone and IGF-1

**Function:** Coordinates growth, tissue maintenance and nutrient-dependent anabolic signalling.

**Where:** Hypothalamus, somatotrophs, liver, growth plates and other tissues.

## Mechanism

1. GHRH promotes and somatostatin restrains pulsatile GH release; sleep and nutritional state modify secretion.
2. GH receptor activates JAK2-STAT pathways in liver and peripheral tissues.
3. IGF-1 from endocrine and local sources supports proliferation and growth-related processes.
4. IGF-1 and GH contribute feedback to hypothalamic-pituitary control.

## Control and constraints

GH also promotes lipolysis and can oppose insulin action. IGF-binding proteins regulate distribution and availability. Linear growth requires open growth plates, adequate nutrition and other hormones.
