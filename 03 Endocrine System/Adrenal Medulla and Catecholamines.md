# Adrenal Medulla and Catecholamines

**Function:** Releases catecholamines into blood during sympathetic activation.

**Where:** Adrenal chromaffin cells.

## Mechanism

1. Preganglionic sympathetic fibres release acetylcholine onto nicotinic receptors.
2. Depolarisation and Ca2+ entry trigger chromaffin-granule exocytosis.
3. Adrenaline and noradrenaline activate adrenergic receptors according to concentration and tissue expression.
4. Uptake and enzymatic metabolism terminate effects.

## Control and constraints

Cortical glucocorticoid exposure supports PNMT expression and adrenaline synthesis. Circulating catecholamines complement direct sympathetic nerves; receptor subtype determines constriction, dilation, cardiac stimulation or metabolic effects.
