# Hypothalamic-Pituitary Integration

**Function:** Connects neural state with coordinated endocrine outputs.

**Where:** Hypothalamus, portal circulation, anterior and posterior pituitary.

## Mechanism

1. Hypothalamic neurons integrate circadian, stress, metabolic and reproductive signals.
2. Releasing/inhibiting hormones enter portal blood and regulate anterior pituitary cell types.
3. Pituitary trophic hormones regulate peripheral glands; peripheral hormones feed back at multiple levels.
4. Oxytocin and vasopressin are synthesised in hypothalamic neurons and released from posterior pituitary axons.

## Control and constraints

Pulses and timing carry information: sustained versus pulsatile exposure can produce different receptor responses. The posterior pituitary releases hypothalamically made hormones rather than synthesising them in glandular cells.
