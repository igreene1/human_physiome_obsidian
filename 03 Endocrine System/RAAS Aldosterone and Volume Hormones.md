# RAAS Aldosterone and Volume Hormones

**Function:** Adjusts renal sodium handling, vascular tone and effective circulation.

**Where:** Juxtaglomerular apparatus, circulation, adrenal cortex and distal nephron.

## Mechanism

1. Reduced renal perfusion, low macula-densa NaCl or beta1 sympathetic input promotes renin secretion.
2. Renin generates angiotensin I; ACE supports angiotensin II formation.
3. AT1 signalling promotes vasoconstriction, aldosterone release and sodium-retaining responses.
4. Aldosterone increases distal sodium transport; natriuretic peptides oppose volume expansion through complementary pathways.

## Control and constraints

Potassium directly stimulates aldosterone independently of renin. Effective arterial filling can be low despite excess total extracellular fluid. Sodium balance must be distinguished from free-water balance.
