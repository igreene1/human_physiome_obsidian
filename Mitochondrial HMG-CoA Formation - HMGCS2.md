# Mitochondrial HMG-CoA Formation - HMGCS2

**Part of:** [[Ketone Body Metabolism]]

## Transformation

Acetoacetyl-CoA + acetyl-CoA + H2O → HMG-CoA + CoA-SH

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | HMGCS2 |
| Compartment | Hepatic mitochondrial matrix |
| Physiological direction | Forms the intermediate for ketone synthesis |

## What controls this step

Fasting-associated transcription and substrate supply promote ketogenesis. Cytosolic HMGCS1 belongs to cholesterol synthesis and should not be conflated with HMGCS2.
