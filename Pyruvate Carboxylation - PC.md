# Pyruvate Carboxylation - PC

**Part of:** [[Gluconeogenesis]]

## Transformation

Pyruvate + bicarbonate + ATP → oxaloacetate + ADP + Pi

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | PC; biotin cofactor |
| Compartment | Mitochondrial matrix |
| Physiological direction | Adds carbon to pyruvate for gluconeogenesis or anaplerosis |

## What controls this step

Acetyl-CoA activates pyruvate carboxylase. Oxaloacetate does not freely cross the inner mitochondrial membrane; malate, aspartate, or phosphoenolpyruvate routes connect compartments.
