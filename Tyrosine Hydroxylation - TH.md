# Tyrosine Hydroxylation - TH

**Part of:** [[Catecholamine Synthesis and Clearance]]

## Transformation

Tyrosine + BH4 + O2 → L-DOPA + oxidised pterin products + H2O

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | TH; iron-containing enzyme and tetrahydrobiopterin |
| Compartment | Cytosol of catecholaminergic cells |
| Physiological direction | Introduces a hydroxyl group at the major regulated entry to catecholamine synthesis |

## What controls this step

Activity responds to phosphorylation, substrate/cofactor supply and catecholamine feedback. BH4 must be regenerated; dietary tyrosine supply alone does not determine synaptic dopamine.
