# Na-K ATPase Transport Cycle

**Part of:** [[Membrane Transport]]

## Transformation

1 ATP hydrolysed: 3 Na+ move out and 2 K+ move in per cycle

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | ATP1A catalytic, ATP1B accessory and tissue-specific FXYD proteins |
| Compartment | Plasma membrane |
| Physiological direction | Electrogenic primary active transport sustains Na+ and K+ gradients |

## What controls this step

Intracellular Na+, extracellular K+, ATP supply and regulation determine turnover. Most rapid action-potential repolarisation is channel-mediated; the pump maintains gradients over time.
