# Steroid 21-Hydroxylation - CYP21A2

**Part of:** [[Steroid Hormone Synthesis]]

## Transformation

Progesterone → deoxycorticosterone; 17-hydroxyprogesterone → 11-deoxycortisol

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | CYP21A2 with POR-supported electron transfer |
| Compartment | Adrenal endoplasmic reticulum |
| Physiological direction | Provides precursors for mineralocorticoid/glucocorticoid branches |

## What controls this step

Substrate and zonal enzyme expression determine downstream products. This is not a sex-steroid aromatisation reaction.
