# Micronutrient Absorption

**Function:** Acquires vitamins and minerals through substrate-specific uptake systems.

**Where:** Predominantly small intestine, with distinct regional specialisations.

## Mechanism

1. Luminal digestion or binding-protein exchange releases nutrients in absorbable forms.
2. Iron is handled by reduction, uptake and ferroportin-mediated export; calcium uptake uses transcellular and paracellular routes.
3. Intrinsic-factor-bound vitamin B12 is taken up in terminal ileum after earlier processing.
4. Fat-soluble vitamins depend substantially on normal bile-supported lipid absorption.

## Control and constraints

There is no single universal micronutrient transporter. Deficits can result from intake, luminal processing, transport, tissue use or losses; serum values may not directly reflect all intracellular pools.
