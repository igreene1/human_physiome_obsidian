# Bile Formation and Enterohepatic Cycling

**Function:** Excretes selected substances and recycles bile acids used in lipid absorption.

**Where:** Hepatocytes, bile ducts, gallbladder and terminal ileum.

## Mechanism

1. Hepatocytes synthesise bile acids from cholesterol and conjugate them for secretion.
2. Canalicular transporters secrete bile salts, phospholipids, cholesterol and conjugated bilirubin.
3. Meals stimulate gallbladder emptying and bile delivery to the intestine.
4. Ileal uptake and portal return recycle bile acids; feedback restrains new synthesis.

## Control and constraints

Bile acids are recycled multiple times and are not digestive enzymes. FXR-FGF19 signalling links intestinal sensing to hepatic synthesis. Biliary secretion is an energy-dependent vectorial process.
