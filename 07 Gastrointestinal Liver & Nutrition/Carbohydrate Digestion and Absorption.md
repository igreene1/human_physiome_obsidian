# Carbohydrate Digestion and Absorption

**Function:** Converts dietary carbohydrate into absorbable monosaccharides.

**Where:** Oral/small-intestinal lumen and enterocyte brush border.

## Mechanism

1. Amylases cleave accessible starch alpha-1,4 linkages into smaller oligosaccharides.
2. Brush-border enzymes including sucrase-isomaltase and lactase release monosaccharides.
3. SGLT1 couples glucose/galactose uptake to sodium; GLUT5 supports fructose entry.
4. Basolateral export, prominently through GLUT2, delivers sugars to portal blood.

## Control and constraints

Humans do not digest cellulose using their own cellulase. Apical SGLT1 depends indirectly on basolateral Na-K ATPase. Transport capacity and substrate amount influence whether carbohydrate reaches the colon.
