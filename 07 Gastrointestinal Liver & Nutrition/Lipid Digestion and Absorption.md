# Lipid Digestion and Absorption

**Function:** Packages dietary lipid for uptake and distribution.

**Where:** Small-intestinal lumen, enterocytes, lacteals and blood.

## Mechanism

1. Bile salts emulsify lipid and support mixed-micelle formation.
2. Pancreatic lipase with colipase hydrolyses triglycerides, with other enzymes processing phospholipids and cholesterol esters.
3. Enterocytes absorb lipid products and re-esterify many long-chain fatty acids.
4. ApoB48/MTP-dependent assembly produces chylomicrons that enter lymph before systemic blood.

## Control and constraints

Shorter-chain fatty acids are more likely to enter portal blood directly. Micelles deliver lipid to the membrane; intact micelles are not absorbed wholesale. Lipoprotein lipase later releases fatty acids from circulating triglycerides.
