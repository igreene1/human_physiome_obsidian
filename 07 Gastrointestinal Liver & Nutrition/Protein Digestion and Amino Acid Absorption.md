# Protein Digestion and Amino Acid Absorption

**Function:** Converts food proteins into amino acids and small peptides for uptake.

**Where:** Stomach, pancreatic secretions and small-intestinal epithelium.

## Mechanism

1. Gastric acid denatures proteins and pepsin begins cleavage.
2. Pancreatic proteases and brush-border peptidases generate amino acids and small peptides.
3. Amino-acid transporters and proton-coupled PEPT1 move substrates across the apical membrane.
4. Intracellular peptidases finish much peptide digestion and basolateral carriers export amino acids.

## Control and constraints

Different amino-acid classes use different transport systems. PEPT1 handles di/tripeptides rather than intact dietary proteins in bulk. Absorption provides nitrogen and carbon substrates for several pathways.
