# Gastric Secretion and Digestion

**Function:** Acidifies and processes a meal while protecting the stomach wall.

**Where:** Parietal, chief, mucus, G, ECL and D cells.

## Mechanism

1. Vagal and local food signals stimulate gastrin and ECL-cell histamine.
2. H2, M3 and CCK-B pathways converge on parietal-cell acid secretion through the H-K ATPase.
3. Chief-cell pepsinogen becomes active in the acidic lumen, beginning protein digestion.
4. Mucus, bicarbonate, tight junctions and mucosal perfusion protect the epithelium.

## Control and constraints

Low antral pH promotes somatostatin-mediated restraint. Intrinsic factor, also produced by parietal cells, supports later ileal B12 absorption. Acid secretion and gastric emptying are linked but distinct controls.
