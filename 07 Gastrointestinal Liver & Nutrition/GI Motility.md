# GI Motility

**Function:** Mixes, stores and propels luminal contents while coordinating sphincters.

**Where:** Smooth muscle, interstitial cells of Cajal and enteric circuits along the gut.

## Mechanism

1. Interstitial cells of Cajal generate slow-wave activity that biases smooth-muscle excitability.
2. Stretch and chemical signals recruit enteric sensory/interneuron pathways.
3. Excitatory cholinergic/tachykinin pathways contract selected regions while inhibitory NO/VIP pathways relax others.
4. Local patterns combine into segmentation, peristalsis and region-specific storage or emptying.

## Control and constraints

Slow waves do not guarantee contraction; spike activity and calcium entry are also important. Extrinsic autonomic and hormonal signals modulate enteric programmes rather than creating every movement directly.
