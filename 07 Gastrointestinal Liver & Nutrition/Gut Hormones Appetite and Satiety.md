# Gut Hormones Appetite and Satiety

**Function:** Coordinates digestion, pancreatic secretion and food-related behaviour.

**Where:** Enteroendocrine cells, vagal pathways, hypothalamus and reward circuits.

## Mechanism

1. Luminal nutrients and mechanical signals recruit different enteroendocrine cell types.
2. GLP-1 and GIP potentiate glucose-dependent insulin secretion; CCK and PYY contribute meal-related feedback.
3. Vagal/brainstem signals and circulating hormones influence gastric emptying and food intake.
4. Longer-term adiposity signals such as leptin interact with hypothalamic and behavioural networks.

## Control and constraints

Appetite is not a simple switch controlled by a single hormone. Ghrelin patterns, learned cues, sleep and energy expenditure also matter. Incretin effects depend on glucose and receptor context.
