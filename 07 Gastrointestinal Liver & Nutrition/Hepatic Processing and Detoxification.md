# Hepatic Processing and Detoxification

**Function:** Transforms nutrients and endogenous/exogenous compounds while regulating blood composition.

**Where:** Hepatocytes with lobular zonation; non-parenchymal liver cells also contribute.

## Mechanism

1. Portal delivery exposes hepatocytes to absorbed nutrients and compounds.
2. Phase I enzymes may oxidise, reduce or hydrolyse substrates, sometimes creating more reactive intermediates.
3. Phase II reactions conjugate selected substrates with glucuronide, sulfate, glutathione or other groups.
4. Transporters direct products toward bile or blood for elimination.

## Control and constraints

Detoxification is not a single reaction and does not always make a molecule harmless. Enzyme expression, cofactors, flow and zonation determine handling; liver also synthesises albumin and many clotting proteins.
