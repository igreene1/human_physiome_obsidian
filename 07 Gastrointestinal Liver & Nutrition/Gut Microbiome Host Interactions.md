# Gut Microbiome Host Interactions

**Function:** Exchanges microbial metabolites and immune signals with the host.

**Where:** Gut lumen, mucus, epithelial barrier, immune cells and portal circulation.

## Mechanism

1. Microbes ferment substrates that escape host digestion, generating short-chain fatty acids and gases.
2. Microbial enzymes modify bile acids, dietary compounds and some drugs.
3. Epithelial receptors and immune networks respond to microbial patterns and metabolites.
4. Host mucus, immunity, transit and diet reshape microbial niches.

## Control and constraints

Associations between a taxon and a disease do not establish causation. Effects depend on strain, substrate, site and host context. Short-chain fatty acids are microbial products used or sensed by host tissues, not exclusively host-synthesised metabolites.
