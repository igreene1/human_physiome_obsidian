# Pancreatic Exocrine Secretion

**Function:** Supplies digestive enzymes and bicarbonate to the duodenum.

**Where:** Pancreatic acinar and duct cells.

## Mechanism

1. Meal-related CCK and vagal acetylcholine stimulate acinar Ca2+-dependent secretion of enzyme-rich granules.
2. Acid in the duodenum promotes secretin release and ductal cAMP signalling.
3. Ductal ion transport, including CFTR-dependent processes, produces bicarbonate-rich fluid.
4. Duodenal enteropeptidase activates trypsinogen, and trypsin activates several other zymogens.

## Control and constraints

Proteases are packaged largely as inactive precursors and protected by inhibitors such as SPINK1. Amylase and lipase do not all require proteolytic activation. Bicarbonate creates a suitable luminal environment for enzymes.
