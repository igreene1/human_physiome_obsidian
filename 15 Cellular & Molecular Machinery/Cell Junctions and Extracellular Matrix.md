# Cell Junctions and Extracellular Matrix

**Function:** Connects cells, controls barriers and transmits mechanical/biochemical signals.

**Where:** Epithelia, endothelium and connective tissues.

## Mechanism

1. Tight junctions restrict selected paracellular routes and preserve membrane polarity.
2. Cadherin-based adherens junctions and desmosomes transmit force between cells.
3. Integrins connect cytoskeleton to matrix and activate intracellular signalling.
4. Matrix synthesis and proteolysis remodel the extracellular environment.

## Control and constraints

Gap junctions exchange small molecules/ions and differ from sealing or anchoring junctions. Matrix is not inert scaffolding: stiffness, ligands and stored factors influence cell behaviour.
