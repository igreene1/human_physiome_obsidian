# Gene Transcription

**Function:** Produces RNA from a DNA template under regulated promoter/enhancer control.

**Where:** Nucleus; mitochondria have their own transcription apparatus.

## Mechanism

1. Chromatin state and transcription-factor binding influence access to regulatory DNA.
2. General factors recruit RNA polymerase and establish initiation.
3. Polymerase elongates RNA while coordinating processing and chromatin transitions.
4. Termination and RNA maturation release a transcript for its subsequent fate.

## Control and constraints

RNA polymerase II mainly produces mRNA and many regulatory RNAs; polymerases I and III have different major products. A gene being accessible does not ensure high transcription, and mRNA abundance also depends on decay.
