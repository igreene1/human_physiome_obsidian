# Lysosomes and Autophagy

**Function:** Degrades internalised material and selected intracellular components for recycling.

**Where:** Endosomal-lysosomal system and autophagosomes.

## Mechanism

1. V-ATPase and counterion pathways acidify lysosomal compartments.
2. Endocytosis/phagocytosis deliver external cargo; macroautophagy encloses intracellular cargo in double membranes.
3. Autophagosome-lysosome fusion exposes cargo to hydrolases.
4. Breakdown products return to cellular pools through transporters.

## Control and constraints

More autophagosomes can mean more initiation or blocked degradation; abundance is not equivalent to flux. Selective autophagy uses cargo receptors, while chaperone-mediated autophagy follows a different entry route.
