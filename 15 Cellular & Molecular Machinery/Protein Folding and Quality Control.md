# Protein Folding and Quality Control

**Function:** Promotes functional protein conformations and removes persistent defects.

**Where:** Cytosol, ER, mitochondria and other compartments.

## Mechanism

1. Chaperones assist folding and prevent inappropriate aggregation.
2. Compartment-specific chemistry supports disulfide formation, glycosylation and assembly.
3. Misfolded products can be retained, refolded or directed to degradation.
4. Stress pathways adjust synthesis, chaperones and clearance capacity.

## Control and constraints

Sequence, environment and partners determine folding. Chaperones do not encode a replacement amino-acid sequence. Persistent ER stress can progress from adaptation toward cell death if homeostasis cannot be restored.
