# Cytoskeleton and Cell Motility

**Function:** Provides shape, intracellular transport and force for movement/division.

**Where:** Actin filaments, microtubules and intermediate filaments.

## Mechanism

1. Nucleation factors assemble polar actin or microtubule networks.
2. Myosin, kinesin and dynein motors couple nucleotide hydrolysis to motion.
3. Adhesion complexes link intracellular force to neighbouring cells or matrix.
4. Coordinated protrusion, adhesion turnover and contraction move a cell or reshape tissue.

## Control and constraints

Intermediate filaments mainly provide mechanical resilience and do not share the same motor-track system. Cytoskeletal dynamics are spatially regulated by small GTPases and other signals.
