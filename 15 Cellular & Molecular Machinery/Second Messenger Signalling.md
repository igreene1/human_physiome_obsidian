# Second Messenger Signalling

**Function:** Distributes receptor information through regulated intracellular molecules.

**Where:** Membranes, cytosol and local signalling microdomains.

## Mechanism

1. Adenylyl cyclases form cAMP; phospholipase C generates IP3 and DAG from PIP2.
2. IP3 releases ER calcium; calcium binds sensors such as calmodulin.
3. Kinases and other effectors decode concentration, localisation and timing.
4. Phosphodiesterases, pumps and phosphatases terminate signals.

## Control and constraints

Calcium oscillation frequency and local nanodomains can carry information beyond average concentration. Messengers are not uniformly mixed throughout a cell; compartmentation supports specificity.
