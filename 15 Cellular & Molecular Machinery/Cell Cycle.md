# Cell Cycle

**Function:** Coordinates genome duplication, chromosome segregation and cell division.

**Where:** Cycling eukaryotic cells.

## Mechanism

1. Mitogenic/nutrient signals influence entry through G1 and the restriction point.
2. S phase duplicates DNA while licensing controls prevent routine re-replication.
3. G2 and mitotic programmes prepare and segregate chromosomes.
4. Cytokinesis separates daughters and cyclin destruction resets the cycle.

## Control and constraints

Cyclin-CDK activity is gated by inhibitors, phosphorylation and proteolysis. Many adult cells are quiescent or terminally differentiated rather than continuously cycling.
