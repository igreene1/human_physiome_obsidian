# Apoptosis and Regulated Cell Death

**Function:** Removes cells through controlled programmes with different execution mechanisms.

**Where:** Cell-type-specific signalling, mitochondria and membranes.

## Mechanism

1. Intrinsic stress can shift BCL2-family balance toward mitochondrial outer-membrane permeabilisation.
2. Cytochrome c supports apoptosome formation and initiator-caspase activation.
3. Executioner caspases dismantle cellular structures and expose clearance signals.
4. Death receptors can activate extrinsic apoptosis; other contexts recruit necroptosis, pyroptosis or ferroptosis.

## Control and constraints

These death modes are not interchangeable. Apoptosis often preserves membrane integrity until clearance; inflammatory outcomes depend on context and whether dying cells are removed promptly.
