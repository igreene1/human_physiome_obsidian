# DNA Replication and Repair

**Function:** Copies genomic information and limits accumulation of harmful sequence changes.

**Where:** Nucleus; mitochondrial genome uses partly distinct machinery.

## Mechanism

1. Origin licensing and activation establish replication forks.
2. Helicases separate strands while polymerases synthesize new DNA with leading/lagging coordination.
3. Proofreading and mismatch repair reduce copying errors.
4. Base, nucleotide, double-strand-break and crosslink repair pathways address different lesion classes.

## Control and constraints

Replication and repair overlap but are not the same process. Repair pathway choice depends on lesion, chromatin and cell-cycle phase. Molecular fidelity is high but not perfect, and some tolerance pathways bypass rather than remove lesions.
