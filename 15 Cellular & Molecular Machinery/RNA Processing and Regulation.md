# RNA Processing and Regulation

**Function:** Determines the sequence, stability, localisation and translation potential of RNA.

**Where:** Nucleus, nuclear pores, cytosol and RNA granules.

## Mechanism

1. Pre-mRNA receives a 5-prime cap and undergoes spliceosomal intron removal.
2. Cleavage and polyadenylation establish the 3-prime end of most mRNAs.
3. Quality-control and export systems select mature messenger ribonucleoproteins.
4. RNA-binding proteins, microRNAs and decay machinery regulate later expression.

## Control and constraints

Alternative splicing can create different products, but not every transcript produces a stable protein. Some RNAs are noncoding functional molecules. Processing is often co-transcriptional rather than strictly after transcription ends.
