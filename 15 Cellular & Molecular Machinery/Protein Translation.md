# Protein Translation

**Function:** Decodes mRNA to synthesise polypeptides using aminoacyl-tRNA and ribosomes.

**Where:** Cytosolic/free or ER-associated ribosomes; separate mitochondrial ribosomes.

## Mechanism

1. Aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases attach the correct amino acids to tRNAs.
2. Initiation factors position a ribosome at an appropriate start codon.
3. Elongation cycles select tRNAs, form peptide bonds and translocate along mRNA.
4. Release factors recognise stop codons and permit product release and ribosome recycling.

## Control and constraints

Translation consumes ATP for tRNA charging and GTP for several ribosomal steps. Start-site selection, elongation rate and quality control influence output. Protein targeting can begin before synthesis finishes.
