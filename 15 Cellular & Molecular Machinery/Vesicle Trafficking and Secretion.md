# Vesicle Trafficking and Secretion

**Function:** Moves selected cargo between compartments and to or from the cell surface.

**Where:** ER, Golgi, endosomes, lysosomes and plasma membrane.

## Mechanism

1. Cargo sorting recruits coats/adaptors and membrane curvature machinery.
2. Vesicles or carriers bud and travel along cytoskeletal routes.
3. Rab GTPases and tethering factors promote target recognition.
4. SNARE-mediated fusion transfers cargo; membrane and machinery are recycled.

## Control and constraints

Constitutive and regulated secretion differ. Coat proteins generally support budding/sorting, whereas SNAREs support fusion specificity and mechanics. Lipid composition and small GTPase cycles help preserve organelle identity.
