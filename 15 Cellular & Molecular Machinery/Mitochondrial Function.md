# Mitochondrial Function

**Function:** Coordinates oxidative metabolism, signalling and selected biosynthetic reactions.

**Where:** Matrix, inner/outer membranes and intermembrane space.

## Mechanism

1. Nuclear-encoded proteins are imported while mitochondrial genes supply selected respiratory components.
2. TCA, beta-oxidation and other pathways generate substrates/reducing equivalents.
3. Inner-membrane electron transport and metabolite carriers couple oxidation to ATP generation.
4. Dynamics, protein surveillance and mitophagy maintain organelle quality.

## Control and constraints

Mitochondria also influence calcium handling, apoptosis and metabolism beyond ATP. Different cell types have different abundance and structure; mature erythrocytes lack them.
