# Receptor Signalling

**Function:** Translates ligand binding into cell-specific biochemical responses.

**Where:** Plasma membrane, cytosol, endosomes and nucleus.

## Mechanism

1. A receptor binds a ligand and changes conformation or oligomeric organisation.
2. Associated enzymes, G proteins or channels transmit the signal.
3. Scaffolded cascades amplify and distribute information to effectors.
4. Phosphatases, ligand removal, trafficking and feedback attenuate or reshape the response.

## Control and constraints

Affinity, receptor abundance and efficacy are different properties. The same receptor can signal differently across compartments and cell states; one linear diagram represents a selected branch.
