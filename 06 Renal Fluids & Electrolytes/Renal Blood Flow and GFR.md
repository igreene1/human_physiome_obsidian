# Renal Blood Flow and GFR

**Function:** Filters plasma while maintaining sufficient renal oxygen and substrate delivery.

**Where:** Renal vessels, glomerular capillaries and Bowman space.

## Mechanism

1. Afferent flow and glomerular pressure support ultrafiltration across the filtration barrier.
2. Water and small solutes cross while blood cells and most large proteins are retained.
3. Myogenic responses and macula-densa feedback modify arteriolar resistance.
4. Hormonal and neural signals alter perfusion and filtration according to systemic state.

## Control and constraints

GFR depends on filtration coefficient and net filtration pressure, not blood flow alone. Moderate versus severe efferent constriction can have different effects; a simple one-direction rule is inadequate.
