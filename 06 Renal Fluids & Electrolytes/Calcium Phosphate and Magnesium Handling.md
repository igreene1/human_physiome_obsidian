# Calcium Phosphate and Magnesium Handling

**Function:** Adjusts renal mineral excretion according to systemic needs.

**Where:** Proximal tubule, thick ascending limb and distal nephron.

## Mechanism

1. Proximal transport reabsorbs phosphate through sodium-phosphate carriers and much calcium with water/solute movement.
2. The thick ascending limb supports paracellular calcium/magnesium transport through its lumen-positive voltage and claudins.
3. Distal calcium uptake uses TRPV5, intracellular binding proteins and basolateral export.
4. PTH, calcitriol and FGF23 coordinate transport capacity and systemic supply.

## Control and constraints

Magnesium regulation also involves distal TRPM6. PTH can conserve calcium while wasting phosphate. Total serum calcium depends partly on albumin; ionised calcium is the directly sensed fraction.
