# Erythropoietin and Renal Endocrine Functions

**Function:** Links renal oxygen sensing and metabolic processing to blood and mineral physiology.

**Where:** Renal interstitial cells, juxtaglomerular cells and proximal tubules.

## Mechanism

1. Oxygen-dependent hydroxylation normally targets HIF-alpha for degradation.
2. Hypoxia stabilises HIF signalling in appropriate renal interstitial cells and promotes erythropoietin production.
3. EPO supports erythroid progenitor survival and differentiation in marrow.
4. Separate renal cell systems produce renin and activate vitamin D.

## Control and constraints

The kidney's endocrine functions are distributed across different cell types. EPO response reflects local oxygen supply/demand, not simply a direct sensor of red-cell number. More red cells feed back by improving oxygen delivery.
