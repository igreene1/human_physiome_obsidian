# Potassium Balance

**Function:** Maintains extracellular potassium compatible with excitability while matching intake and losses.

**Where:** Muscle/liver cellular pools and distal nephron.

## Mechanism

1. Insulin and beta2 stimulation shift potassium into cells through mechanisms including Na-K ATPase recruitment.
2. Aldosterone and high plasma K+ favour distal potassium secretion.
3. Principal-cell basolateral uptake, ENaC-dependent voltage and apical ROMK/BK channels support secretion.
4. Low intake recruits conservation mechanisms, including collecting-duct potassium reabsorption.

## Control and constraints

Distal sodium delivery and flow strongly affect potassium secretion. Acid-base shifts are context-dependent and differ for organic versus mineral acidosis. Plasma concentration represents only a small fraction of total-body potassium.
