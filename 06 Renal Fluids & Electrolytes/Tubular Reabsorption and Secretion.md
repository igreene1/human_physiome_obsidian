# Tubular Reabsorption and Secretion

**Function:** Selectively returns filtered material to blood and transfers other substances into urine.

**Where:** Polarised nephron epithelium and peritubular circulation.

## Mechanism

1. Basolateral Na-K ATPase powers many secondary transport processes.
2. Proximal segments reclaim much of filtered sodium, water, bicarbonate and nutrients.
3. Loop, distal tubule and collecting duct alter the remaining composition using segment-specific channels/transporters.
4. Secretion adds organic solutes, H+ or K+ to tubular fluid according to context.

## Control and constraints

Excretion equals filtration minus reabsorption plus secretion. Transport maximum, filtered load, electrical gradients and water permeability differ by segment. A low urinary concentration alone does not determine the excretion rate.
