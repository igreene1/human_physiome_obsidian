# Micturition

**Function:** Alternates low-pressure urine storage with coordinated emptying.

**Where:** Bladder detrusor, urethral sphincters, spinal cord and pontine/cortical circuits.

## Mechanism

1. During filling, compliant detrusor and outlet activity support storage; stretch afferents report volume/tension.
2. Central assessment permits or defers voiding.
3. Parasympathetic acetylcholine activates detrusor M3 pathways while outlet resistance falls.
4. Pudendal somatic drive to the external sphincter is reduced during coordinated voiding.

## Control and constraints

Bladder volume is not sensed by a single binary switch. Sympathetic beta3/alpha1 and somatic pathways support storage, while pontine coordination prevents contraction against a closed outlet.
