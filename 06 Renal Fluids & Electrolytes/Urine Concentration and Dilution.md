# Urine Concentration and Dilution

**Function:** Varies water excretion while continuing to excrete solutes.

**Where:** Loops of Henle, medullary interstitium, vasa recta and collecting ducts.

## Mechanism

1. Water-impermeable ascending limbs reabsorb salt and help generate a corticomedullary gradient.
2. Descending limbs and countercurrent geometry support osmotic multiplication.
3. Vasa recta exchange limits washout; urea recycling contributes to inner medullary osmotic conditions.
4. ADH-regulated AQP2 insertion changes collecting-duct water permeability.

## Control and constraints

ADH needs an intact medullary gradient to concentrate urine effectively. Inner medullary concentrating mechanisms remain incompletely resolved. Dilute urine requires solute reabsorption without equivalent water reabsorption.
