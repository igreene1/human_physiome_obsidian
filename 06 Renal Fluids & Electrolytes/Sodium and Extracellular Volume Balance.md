# Sodium and Extracellular Volume Balance

**Function:** Matches sodium excretion to intake and thereby supports extracellular volume.

**Where:** Nephron segments, renal circulation and volume-regulating hormones.

## Mechanism

1. Most filtered sodium is reabsorbed upstream; distal segments adjust the final fraction.
2. Reduced effective circulation activates sympathetic, angiotensin and aldosterone signals.
3. Higher perfusion pressure and natriuretic signals can increase sodium excretion.
4. Changes in sodium content influence extracellular water retention and circulation.

## Control and constraints

Plasma sodium concentration reflects water relative to exchangeable sodium/potassium, not simply total sodium stores. Distal fine-tuning matters even though it handles a smaller fraction of the filtered load.
