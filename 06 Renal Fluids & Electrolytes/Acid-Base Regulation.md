# Acid-Base Regulation

**Function:** Balances nonvolatile acid production, carbon dioxide elimination and bicarbonate availability.

**Where:** Blood buffers, lungs and kidneys.

## Mechanism

1. Buffers limit immediate pH changes without eliminating net acid.
2. Ventilation adjusts PaCO2 within minutes.
3. Kidneys reclaim filtered bicarbonate and excrete acid as ammonium and titratable acid.
4. Net renal acid excretion generates new bicarbonate to replace buffer consumed by nonvolatile acids.

## Control and constraints

Bicarbonate reclamation is not the same as new bicarbonate generation. Renal adaptation takes hours to days. Compensation constrains but does not generally erase the primary disturbance; mixed processes require separate reasoning.
