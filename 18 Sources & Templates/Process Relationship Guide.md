# Process Relationship Guide

Use explicit relationship verbs when reading or extending maps.

| Relationship | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| contains / part of | A hierarchy, not necessarily a time sequence | Glycolysis contains the PFK1 reaction |
| occurs in | Cellular or anatomical location | TCA enzymes operate in the mitochondrial matrix, with the SDH exception |
| senses | A signal reflects a quantity or proxy | Macula densa senses tubular NaCl delivery |
| converts | Chemical transformation | LDH interconverts pyruvate and lactate |
| supplies | Material or reducing equivalents become available | PPP supplies NADPH |
| stimulates / inhibits | Context-specific regulation | Malonyl-CoA inhibits CPT1 |
| transports | Movement across a defined boundary | AQP2 allows osmotic water movement |
| measured by | An observation related to a process | PaCO2 reflects production relative to alveolar ventilation |
| feeds back to | An effect modifies its controller | Cortisol restrains HPA-axis drive |
| related | Navigation without a claimed causal direction | Two notes share a tissue or regulator |

Arrow labels in the new canvases carry these meanings. A link in an index or a previous/next reading link is **not automatically a causal edge**. Chemical direction must come from the reaction and physiological context, not the position of a card on a canvas.
