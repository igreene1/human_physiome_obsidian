# Expansion Roadmap

This edition replaces the original short organ-system outlines with mechanisms and adds a substantial cellular/reaction layer. Work can now proceed by **deepening specific branches**, with [[Coverage and Gaps]] as the current inventory.

| Priority | Next useful work | Completion criterion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Source audit of retained v3 summaries and new detailed notes | Specific primary evidence, human/model distinction and source version for each claim |
| 2 | Full reaction graphs for additional metabolic branches | Every intermediate, transport boundary, enzyme and cofactor represented without invented links |
| 3 | Cell/organ localisation and isoforms | Tissue-specific executor, relevant gene/protein form and expression context |
| 4 | Quantitative physiology | Units, equations, assumptions, parameter ranges and a validated model source |
| 5 | Developmental and state variants | Pregnancy, neonatal, puberty, aging, exercise and fasting differences explicitly linked |
| 6 | Disease and drug perturbations | A defined normal node altered, causal consequences and reliable evidence |

Do not measure completeness by note count alone. A thousand enzyme names without reactions and relationships would be less useful than a well-supported connected pathway. Atomistic detail requires a separate chemistry/structural-biophysics layer and will remain selective rather than an exhaustive map of all molecular motions.
