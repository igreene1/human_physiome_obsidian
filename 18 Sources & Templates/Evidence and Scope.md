# Evidence and Scope

This vault is a **large educational map of normal human physiology**, with connected system, cellular and molecular explanations. It is not a complete inventory of every human process, a clinical decision system or a peer-reviewed textbook.

## What the depth labels mean

| Label | What is present | What it does not promise |
|---|---|---|
| Control loop | Variable, sensors/integration, effectors, feedback and time scale | A calibrated mathematical model |
| Mechanism | Ordered causal explanation, location, controls and linked detail | Every intermediate or cell-specific variant |
| Cell mechanism | Cell function and processes it performs | Complete transcriptomic or marker atlas |
| Molecular function | A protein's transport/receptor role in context | Full protein structure or all isoforms |
| Reaction | Named transformation, enzyme/machinery, compartment and control | An elementary quantum-chemical event |
| Reference summary | An inherited concise v3 note retained for continuity | Comprehensive new scientific review |

The **110 reaction notes** include explicitly labelled multi-reaction modules where a single enzyme executes several chemical steps. Reaction formulas are explanatory biochemical notation and can omit protonation states, bound metals and solvent terms. They are not a machine-validated atom/charge-balanced reaction database. No universal ATP yield, time constant or concentration is implied when none is supplied.

## How sources were used

New prose is an explanatory synthesis. References point to relevant Reactome pathway entries and OpenStax chapters, with targeted research references for selected corrections. They are not sentence-by-sentence provenance or evidence that every source's full underlying literature was independently reappraised. [[Source Register]] provides the links. Reactome pathways themselves contain source literature and species/evidence annotations for deeper work.

The linked databases are much larger than this vault; **their complete datasets have not been imported**. All core note text and Canvas files are local, but external reference pages need an internet connection. Previously supplied v3 reference notes remain and are marked as inherited summaries where appropriate.

## Human context and uncertainty

- Human biology is the target. Common rodent simplifications have been corrected where identified, particularly beta-cell glucose transport. This does not amount to a complete species audit of every inherited sentence.
- Cell type, organ, age, sex-related anatomy, developmental stage, circadian phase and physiological state alter pathways. A canonical chain is a selected explanation rather than a claim that all cells execute it.
- The inner renal medullary concentrating mechanism, aspects of brain-fluid exchange and complex neural computation have unresolved details. Notes flag important uncertainties rather than assigning false precision.
- Microbial reactions are described as host-microbe interactions; they are not claimed to be reactions encoded by human cells.
- Clinical measurements illustrate observability. A changed value can have several causes and does not identify a diagnosis on its own.

## What remains incomplete

Full enzyme isoforms, post-translational modification sites, every receptor complex, all metabolites/compartments, quantitative kinetics, atomistic mechanisms, disease/drug perturbations and many cell/developmental subtypes remain incomplete. See [[Coverage and Gaps]]. Structural validation checks links, JSON and metadata; it does not prove physiological truth.
