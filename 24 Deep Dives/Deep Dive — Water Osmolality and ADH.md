# Deep Dive — Water Osmolality and ADH

## Question

How does the body stabilise plasma tonicity when water intake or water loss changes?

## Mechanism

↑ [[Serum Osmolality]] / effective osmotic stimulus → hypothalamic osmoreceptor activation → thirst + vasopressin (ADH) release → [[V2 Receptor]] on [[Collecting Duct Principal Cell]] → [[cAMP PKA Signalling]] → [[Aquaporin 2]] insertion into apical membrane → ↑ collecting-duct water permeability → water reabsorption down the medullary osmotic gradient → more concentrated urine ([[Urine Osmolality]]) + tendency for plasma osmolality to fall toward baseline.

Low osmotic drive suppresses ADH, reducing collecting-duct water permeability and allowing excretion of more dilute urine.

## Required kidney architecture

The ADH response depends on the medullary concentration gradient generated/maintained by the loop of Henle and associated countercurrent processes, especially [[Thick Ascending Limb]] salt reabsorption.
