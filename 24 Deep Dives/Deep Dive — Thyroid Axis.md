# Deep Dive — Thyroid Axis

## Question

How does the hypothalamic-pituitary-thyroid axis control thyroid hormone availability and downstream metabolic programmes?

## Axis

[[Hypothalamus]] TRH-producing neurons → portal TRH → [[Anterior Pituitary]] thyrotrophs → TSH → [[TSH Receptor]] on [[Thyroid Follicular Cell]] → [[cAMP PKA Signalling]] → iodide capture via [[Sodium Iodide Symporter]] + thyroid hormone synthesis/release → circulating T4/T3 → tissue conversion and [[Thyroid Hormone Receptor]] → [[Nuclear Hormone Receptor Signalling]] → altered transcription in target tissues.

## Feedback

Higher effective circulating thyroid hormone suppresses hypothalamic/pituitary drive; lower hormone availability removes part of that negative feedback.

## Whole-body outputs

- [[Energy Balance and Body Weight Regulation]]
- [[Core Temperature Homeostasis]]
- cardiovascular responsiveness / heart-rate effects
- growth and neurodevelopmental programmes

## Measurement window

[[TSH and Free T4]] gives you one practical window into the feedback loop.
