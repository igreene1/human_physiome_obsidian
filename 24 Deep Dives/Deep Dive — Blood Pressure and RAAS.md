# Deep Dive — Blood Pressure and RAAS

## Question

How do rapid neural control and slower renal-endocrine control cooperate to regulate arterial pressure?

## Fast control

change in arterial stretch → baroreceptor firing changes → medullary autonomic integration → sympathetic/parasympathetic output → changes in [[Heart Rate]], cardiac contractility and [[Vascular Tone and Systemic Resistance]] → change in arterial pressure.

## Slower volume/RAAS control

↓ renal perfusion / ↓ tubular NaCl delivery / sympathetic drive → renin → angiotensin II → [[AT1 Receptor]] → vasoconstriction + aldosterone support → [[Mineralocorticoid Receptor]] → [[ENaC]] / [[Na-K ATPase]] in [[Collecting Duct Principal Cell]] → Na+ retention → water retention → ↑ extracellular/circulating volume → supports venous return/cardiac output and pressure.

## Important relation

Mean arterial pressure is not controlled by one organ. It emerges from cardiac output, systemic resistance, arterial properties and blood volume, each under overlapping neural, local, endocrine and renal control.
