# Deep Dive — Glucose Homeostasis

## Question

How does the body keep circulating glucose available without allowing large uncontrolled swings?

## Fed-state chain

carbohydrate ingestion → intestinal glucose absorption → ↑ [[Serum Glucose]] → [[Pancreatic Beta Cell]] glucose metabolism → insulin secretion → [[Insulin Receptor]] → [[RTK PI3K AKT Signalling]] → [[GLUT4]] recruitment in [[Skeletal Myocyte]] and [[Adipocyte]] + changes in hepatic enzyme activity → ↑ glucose uptake/storage and ↓ hepatic glucose production → glucose returns toward baseline.

## Fasting-state chain

↓ circulating nutrient availability / lower insulin-to-glucagon ratio → [[Pancreatic Alpha Cell]] glucagon output + autonomic/endocrine support → [[Glycogen Metabolism]] shifts toward hepatic glycogenolysis → [[Gluconeogenesis]] increases → adipose [[Lipolysis and Beta Oxidation]] provides fatty acids/glycerol → prolonged fasting supports [[Ketone Body Metabolism]].

## Main tissues

- [[Pancreatic Islet]] — senses and signals
- [[Liver]] — buffers and produces glucose
- [[Skeletal Muscle]] — large glucose sink, glycogen store for local use
- [[Adipose Tissue]] — switches between storage and mobilisation
- gut — nutrient delivery and incretin signalling

## Measurements

- [[Serum Glucose]]
- [[HbA1c]]
