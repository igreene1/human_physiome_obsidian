# Autonomic GI Control

**Role:** Extrinsic autonomic modulation of enteric circuits, motility, secretion and blood flow.

## Sensor / initiating signal

Vagal/pelvic parasympathetic pathways, sympathetic pathways and visceral afferents.

## Integrator / pathway

Muscarinic and adrenergic receptors plus enteric neurotransmitters.

## Effector

Multiple.

## Mechanism

Parasympathetic activity generally supports digestion; sympathetic activity generally restrains motility/secretion and alters vascular/sphincter tone; enteric circuits can operate locally.
