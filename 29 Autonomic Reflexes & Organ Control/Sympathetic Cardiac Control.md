# Sympathetic Cardiac Control

**Role:** Autonomic increase in heart rate, conduction and contractile performance.

## Sensor / initiating signal

Cardiac sympathetic nerves and circulating catecholamines.

## Integrator / pathway

β1 receptors on sinoatrial, AV-nodal and myocardial cells.

## Effector

Gs/cAMP/PKA.

## Mechanism

Increases If and Ca²⁺ currents in pacemaker cells and enhances Ca²⁺ cycling in cardiomyocytes.
