# Parasympathetic Cardiac Control

**Role:** Rapid vagal slowing of sinoatrial rate and AV conduction.

## Sensor / initiating signal

Cardiovagal neurons, chiefly via vagus nerve.

## Integrator / pathway

M2 receptors in nodal tissue.

## Effector

Gi/o + GIRK K⁺ channels; reduced cAMP.

## Mechanism

Hyperpolarises/slows pacemaker depolarisation and reduces nodal Ca²⁺ currents.
