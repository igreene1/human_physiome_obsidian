# Baroreceptor Reflex

**Role:** Rapid negative-feedback control of arterial pressure.

## Sensor / initiating signal

Stretch receptors in carotid sinus and aortic arch.

## Integrator / pathway

Glossopharyngeal/vagal afferents → NTS → CVLM/RVLM plus cardiovagal nuclei.

## Effector

Sympathetic and parasympathetic output to heart and vessels.

## Mechanism

↑ pressure → ↑ baroreceptor firing → ↓ sympathetic + ↑ vagal cardiac output; ↓ pressure produces the reverse.
