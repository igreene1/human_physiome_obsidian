# Visceral Afferent Signalling

**Role:** Sensory limb of autonomic homeostasis carrying information from organs to CNS.

## Sensor / initiating signal

Mechanoreceptors, chemoreceptors, osmoreceptors and nociceptors in viscera/vessels.

## Integrator / pathway

Vagal, glossopharyngeal and spinal visceral afferents.

## Effector

NTS, spinal cord and higher interoceptive networks.

## Mechanism

Enables reflex control and conscious/interoceptive sensations such as fullness, nausea or visceral pain.
