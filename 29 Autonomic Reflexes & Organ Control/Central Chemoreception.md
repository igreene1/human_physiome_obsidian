# Central Chemoreception

**Role:** Brainstem sensing that strongly links ventilation to CO₂-derived changes in brain extracellular fluid pH.

## Sensor / initiating signal

Chemosensitive brainstem networks including retrotrapezoid-region neurons and distributed sensors.

## Integrator / pathway

Respiratory pattern-generating networks.

## Effector

Ventilatory drive.

## Mechanism

CO₂ crosses the blood–brain barrier → changes H⁺ in CNS fluid → ventilation adjusts to regulate PaCO₂/pH.
