# Sympathetic Vascular Control

**Role:** Neural regulation of vascular resistance and capacitance.

## Sensor / initiating signal

Sympathetic vasoconstrictor nerves; circulating catecholamines.

## Integrator / pathway

Mostly α1 receptors for constriction; β2 effects in selected beds are especially responsive to circulating adrenaline.

## Effector

α1 Gq/Ca²⁺; β2 Gs/cAMP.

## Mechanism

Changes arteriolar resistance and venous capacitance to redistribute flow and regulate pressure.
