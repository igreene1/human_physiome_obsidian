# Peripheral Chemoreceptor Reflex

**Role:** Reflex response to arterial hypoxaemia and changes in CO₂/H⁺.

## Sensor / initiating signal

Carotid bodies and aortic bodies.

## Integrator / pathway

Afferents to NTS and respiratory/autonomic centres.

## Effector

Ventilation plus cardiovascular/autonomic responses.

## Mechanism

Low PaO₂, increased H⁺ and raised CO₂ increase carotid-body activity, strongly stimulating ventilation.
