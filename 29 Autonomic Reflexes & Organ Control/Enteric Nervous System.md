# Enteric Nervous System

**Role:** Intrinsic neural network of the gastrointestinal tract capable of organising local reflexes semi-independently of CNS input.

## Sensor / initiating signal

Sensory neurons in gut wall plus central autonomic modulation.

## Integrator / pathway

Myenteric and submucosal plexuses.

## Effector

ACh, NO, serotonin, ATP and many peptides.

## Mechanism

Coordinates motility, secretion, absorption and local blood flow.
