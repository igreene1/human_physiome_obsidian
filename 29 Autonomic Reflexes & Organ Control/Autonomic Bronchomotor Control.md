# Autonomic Bronchomotor Control

**Role:** Neural/hormonal modulation of airway smooth-muscle tone and secretions.

## Sensor / initiating signal

Parasympathetic vagal reflexes and circulating catecholamines.

## Integrator / pathway

M3 receptors promote bronchoconstriction/secretions; β2 receptors promote relaxation.

## Effector

M3 Gq/Ca²⁺ versus β2 cAMP/PKA.

## Mechanism

Balances airway defence/reflex constriction with smooth-muscle relaxation mechanisms.
