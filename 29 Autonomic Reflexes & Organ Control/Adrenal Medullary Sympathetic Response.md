# Adrenal Medullary Sympathetic Response

**Role:** Endocrine extension of sympathetic activation.

## Sensor / initiating signal

Preganglionic sympathetic neurons.

## Integrator / pathway

Nicotinic neuronal receptors on adrenal chromaffin cells.

## Effector

Ligand-gated depolarisation → Ca²⁺-dependent exocytosis.

## Mechanism

Releases adrenaline and noradrenaline into circulation, amplifying whole-body stress responses.
