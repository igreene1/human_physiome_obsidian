# Folate One-Carbon Metabolism

**Function:** Transfers one-carbon units for nucleotide synthesis and methyl-group metabolism.

**Where:** Cytosolic and mitochondrial folate networks.

## Mechanism

1. Serine/glycine-related reactions load one-carbon units onto tetrahydrofolate.
2. Interconversion creates folate forms needed for purine and thymidylate synthesis.
3. MTHFR supplies 5-methyl-THF for B12-dependent methionine synthase.
4. Methionine supports SAM-dependent methyl transfer, while folate cofactor is recycled.

## Control and constraints

Folate forms are chemically distinct and compartmentation matters. B12 deficiency can trap folate in a methylated form; this does not make B12 and folate interchangeable nutrients.
