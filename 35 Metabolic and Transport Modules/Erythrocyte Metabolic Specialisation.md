# Erythrocyte Metabolic Specialisation

**Function:** Supports gas transport in a cell without nucleus or mitochondria.

**Where:** Mature red-cell cytosol and membrane.

## Mechanism

1. Glycolysis supplies ATP for membrane transport and structural maintenance.
2. The oxidative pentose phosphate pathway supplies NADPH for reducing systems.
3. A 2,3-BPG branch modulates haemoglobin affinity at a cost to ATP yield.
4. NADH-dependent cytochrome-b5-reductase pathways reduce methemoglobin.

## Control and constraints

Mature red cells cannot use oxidative phosphorylation or synthesise proteins in the ordinary nucleated-cell manner. Their finite repair capacity contributes to eventual clearance.
