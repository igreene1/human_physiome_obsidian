# Transamination and Nitrogen Transfer

**Function:** Redistributes amino groups without releasing free ammonia at every transfer.

**Where:** Cytosol and mitochondria, depending on aminotransferase.

## Mechanism

1. A pyridoxal-phosphate-dependent enzyme accepts an amino group from an amino acid.
2. The enzyme transfers that group to a keto-acid acceptor.
3. Common reactions connect alanine/pyruvate or aspartate/oxaloacetate with glutamate/2-oxoglutarate.
4. Glutamate/glutamine pathways then route nitrogen into biosynthesis, transport or disposal.

## Control and constraints

Transamination is reversible and does not by itself complete nitrogen excretion. Different amino acids require different catabolic routes beyond a simple universal transaminase step.
