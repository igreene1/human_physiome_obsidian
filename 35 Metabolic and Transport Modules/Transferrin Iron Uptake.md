# Transferrin Iron Uptake

**Function:** Delivers safely bound plasma iron to cells.

**Where:** Transferrin receptor endosomes and cytosolic iron pools.

## Mechanism

1. Iron-loaded transferrin binds transferrin receptor and is endocytosed.
2. Endosomal acidification releases iron from transferrin.
3. Reduction and DMT1-associated transport deliver iron across the endosomal membrane.
4. Apotransferrin/receptor recycle to the surface; cellular iron is used, stored or exported.

## Control and constraints

Ferritin sequesters iron and IRP/IRE regulation adjusts selected uptake/storage transcripts. Free labile iron must be constrained because it can promote harmful radical chemistry.
