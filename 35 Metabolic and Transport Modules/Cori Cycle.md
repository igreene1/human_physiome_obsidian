# Cori Cycle

**Function:** Exchanges lactate carbon between glycolytic tissues and glucose-producing organs.

**Where:** Muscle/red cells, blood, liver and kidney.

## Mechanism

1. Glycolysis reduces pyruvate to lactate while regenerating NAD+.
2. Lactate enters blood through monocarboxylate transport pathways.
3. Liver/kidney can oxidise lactate to pyruvate and use gluconeogenesis to form glucose.
4. Glucose can return to glycolytic tissues.

## Control and constraints

The inter-organ cycle transfers energy cost to glucose-producing tissues; it does not create net free energy. Lactate also fuels direct oxidation elsewhere, so not all circulating lactate completes a Cori cycle.
