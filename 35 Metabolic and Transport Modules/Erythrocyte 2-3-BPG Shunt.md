# Erythrocyte 2-3-BPG Shunt

**Function:** Adjusts haemoglobin oxygen affinity through a glycolytic side branch.

**Where:** Red-cell cytosol.

## Mechanism

1. Bisphosphoglycerate mutase diverts 1,3-BPG toward 2,3-BPG.
2. 2,3-BPG binds preferentially to deoxygenated adult haemoglobin and stabilises lower-affinity conformations.
3. Phosphatase activity converts 2,3-BPG to 3-phosphoglycerate.
4. Carbon rejoins glycolysis downstream of the phosphoglycerate-kinase ATP-producing step.

## Control and constraints

Diversion sacrifices potential ATP from the bypassed reaction. Fetal haemoglobin binds 2,3-BPG less strongly, contributing to its higher oxygen affinity in the relevant context.
