# Hepcidin Ferroportin Regulation

**Function:** Controls release of iron from enterocytes and storage/recycling cells.

**Where:** Hepatic hepcidin and ferroportin-expressing membranes.

## Mechanism

1. Iron-related BMP-SMAD signals and inflammatory IL-6-STAT3 signals influence hepatic hepcidin production.
2. Hepcidin binds ferroportin and reduces cell-surface iron export.
3. Lower export restrains dietary absorption and macrophage iron release into plasma.
4. Erythropoietic signals, including erythroferrone, can suppress hepcidin to support marrow demand.

## Control and constraints

Inflammation can trap iron in stores despite low circulating availability. The same hormone coordinates several tissue sources, so plasma iron does not directly report total stores.
