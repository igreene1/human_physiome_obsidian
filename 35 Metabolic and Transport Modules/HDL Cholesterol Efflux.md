# HDL Cholesterol Efflux

**Function:** Moves excess cellular cholesterol into extracellular acceptor pathways.

**Where:** Peripheral cells, HDL-associated particles and liver.

## Mechanism

1. ABCA1 transfers lipids to lipid-poor apolipoprotein A-I.
2. ABCG1 and other routes support further cholesterol exchange with suitable acceptors.
3. LCAT esterifies HDL-associated cholesterol, affecting particle maturation.
4. Hepatic selective uptake and interparticle transfer return cholesterol to broader disposal/recycling pathways.

## Control and constraints

HDL cholesterol concentration does not fully measure flux or function. Humans have CETP-mediated exchange, which differs from some common experimental species.
