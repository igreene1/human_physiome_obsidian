# Adipocyte Lipolysis

**Function:** Mobilises stored triglyceride into fatty acids and glycerol.

**Where:** Adipocyte lipid droplets.

## Mechanism

1. Catecholamine/cAMP signalling phosphorylates regulatory proteins including perilipin and HSL-associated targets.
2. ATGL, supported by cofactors such as ABHD5, initiates triglyceride hydrolysis.
3. HSL preferentially processes diacylglycerol and monoglyceride lipase completes hydrolysis.
4. Released fatty acids leave or are re-esterified; glycerol enters circulation.

## Control and constraints

Insulin restrains lipolytic signalling through several mechanisms including cAMP control. Net fatty-acid release equals hydrolysis minus re-esterification and local use; lipolysis is not the same as mitochondrial oxidation.
