# Peroxisomal Fatty Acid Processing

**Function:** Shortens selected fatty acids and handles specialised lipid substrates.

**Where:** Peroxisomal membrane and matrix.

## Mechanism

1. Transport systems admit activated or activation-linked substrates according to route.
2. Acyl-CoA oxidases transfer electrons directly to oxygen, producing peroxide rather than respiratory-chain ATP.
3. Hydration, oxidation and thiolysis shorten chains.
4. Products can be exported for further mitochondrial oxidation or other metabolism; catalase manages peroxide.

## Control and constraints

Peroxisomal beta-oxidation differs energetically from mitochondrial oxidation. Branched substrates may require alpha-oxidation; very-long-chain fatty acids are an important substrate class.
