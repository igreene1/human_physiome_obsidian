# Pyrimidine and Thymidylate Synthesis

**Function:** Builds pyrimidine nucleotides and supports DNA thymidine supply.

**Where:** Cytosolic CAD/UMPS reactions and mitochondrial DHODH.

## Mechanism

1. CAD carries the initial steps using glutamine, bicarbonate and aspartate-derived carbon/nitrogen.
2. DHODH oxidises dihydroorotate and connects to ubiquinone.
3. UMPS attaches/finishes the ring-ribose relationship to produce UMP; phosphorylation and amination supply other pyrimidines.
4. Thymidylate synthase uses methylene-THF to convert dUMP to dTMP, with DHFR-dependent folate recycling.

## Control and constraints

Pyrimidine synthesis builds the ring before attachment to ribose, unlike de novo purine synthesis. Mitochondrial participation links some nucleotide production to respiratory-chain function.
