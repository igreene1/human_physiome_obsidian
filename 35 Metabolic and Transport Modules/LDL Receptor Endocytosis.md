# LDL Receptor Endocytosis

**Function:** Delivers lipoprotein cholesterol to cells and regulates plasma clearance.

**Where:** LDLR, coated pits, endosomes and lysosomes.

## Mechanism

1. LDL receptor binds appropriate ApoB100/ApoE-containing particles.
2. Clathrin-mediated uptake internalises receptor-ligand complexes.
3. Acidic endosomes dissociate cargo from receptors for recycling.
4. Lysosomal lipases release cholesterol; cellular sensing adjusts synthesis, esterification and receptor expression.

## Control and constraints

PCSK9 can divert LDLR toward degradation, reducing recycling. Receptor-mediated uptake is regulated rather than a passive diffusion process.
