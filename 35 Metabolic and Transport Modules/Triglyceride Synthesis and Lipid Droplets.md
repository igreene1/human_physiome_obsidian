# Triglyceride Synthesis and Lipid Droplets

**Function:** Stores fatty acyl chains in neutral lipid for later use.

**Where:** ER-associated synthesis and lipid droplets.

## Mechanism

1. Glycerol 3-phosphate or monoacylglycerol routes provide glycerol backbones in tissue-specific contexts.
2. Acyltransferases build phosphatidic acid/diacylglycerol intermediates.
3. DGAT enzymes add a final acyl chain to form triglyceride.
4. Neutral lipid accumulates between ER membrane leaflets and is packaged into droplets with a phospholipid monolayer.

## Control and constraints

Adipose and intestinal synthesis emphasise different substrate routes. Droplets are regulated organelles with surface proteins, not passive oil pools; synthesis and lipolysis can occur concurrently.
