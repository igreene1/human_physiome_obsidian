# Anaplerosis and Cataplerosis

**Function:** Balances replenishment and withdrawal of TCA intermediates.

**Where:** Mitochondrial/cytosolic metabolic networks.

## Mechanism

1. Biosynthesis removes intermediates such as citrate or oxaloacetate-related carbon.
2. Pyruvate carboxylase replenishes oxaloacetate; amino-acid and propionyl-CoA routes add other intermediates.
3. Compartmental shuttles distribute carbon and redox equivalents.
4. Net supply must match withdrawal for sustained cycle function.

## Control and constraints

Acetyl-CoA entry alone does not create net oxaloacetate in the ordinary human TCA cycle. Anaplerosis is replenishment, while cataplerosis is removal; neither is identical to overall cycle turnover.
