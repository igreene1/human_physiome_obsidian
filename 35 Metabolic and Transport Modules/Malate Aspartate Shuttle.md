# Malate Aspartate Shuttle

**Function:** Transfers cytosolic reducing equivalents into the matrix without transporting NADH directly.

**Where:** Cytosolic/matrix enzymes and inner-membrane carriers.

## Mechanism

1. Cytosolic MDH1 uses NADH to reduce oxaloacetate to malate.
2. Malate transport and matrix MDH2 regenerate oxaloacetate while producing matrix NADH.
3. Transaminase reactions convert oxaloacetate-related carbon to aspartate for transport.
4. Carrier exchange and cytosolic reactions close the cycle.

## Control and constraints

The shuttle transfers redox equivalents, not net NADH molecules across the membrane. Carrier gradients, calcium-sensitive regulation in selected isoforms and metabolite availability shape capacity.
