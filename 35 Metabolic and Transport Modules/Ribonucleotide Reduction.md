# Ribonucleotide Reduction

**Function:** Supplies deoxyribonucleotide precursors for DNA synthesis and repair.

**Where:** Ribonucleotide reductase complexes in appropriate cellular states.

## Mechanism

1. RNR uses radical chemistry to reduce the ribose 2-prime position in ribonucleotide diphosphates.
2. Thioredoxin/glutaredoxin systems restore enzyme reducing capacity using NADPH-linked pathways.
3. Allosteric sites control overall activity and substrate preference.
4. Kinases and salvage routes help establish balanced dNTP pools.

## Control and constraints

Balanced pools matter as much as total abundance; dATP can inhibit overall activity. Cell-cycle and DNA-damage contexts alter subunit use and demand.
