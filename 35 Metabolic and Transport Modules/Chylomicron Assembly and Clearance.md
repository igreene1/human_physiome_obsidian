# Chylomicron Assembly and Clearance

**Function:** Distributes intestinally absorbed long-chain lipids.

**Where:** Enterocyte ER/Golgi, lymph and capillary beds.

## Mechanism

1. ApoB48 is lipidated through MTP-dependent assembly in enterocytes.
2. Particles enter lacteals and reach blood via lymphatic drainage.
3. Capillary lipoprotein lipase, supported by GPIHBP1 and apolipoprotein regulation, hydrolyses triglyceride.
4. Remnants enriched in residual components are cleared predominantly by liver.

## Control and constraints

ApoB48 differs from hepatic ApoB100 through tissue-specific RNA editing. Chylomicrons enter systemic circulation before most hepatic first-pass handling of their lipid cargo.
