# Glycerol Phosphate Shuttle

**Function:** Transfers cytosolic NADH-derived electrons to the ubiquinone pool.

**Where:** Cytosolic GPD1 and inner-membrane-associated GPD2.

## Mechanism

1. GPD1 reduces dihydroxyacetone phosphate using cytosolic NADH.
2. Mitochondrial GPD2 oxidises glycerol 3-phosphate using bound FAD.
3. Electrons reach ubiquinone, bypassing complex I.
4. Dihydroxyacetone phosphate returns to the cytosolic reaction.

## Control and constraints

This route gives a lower conventional ATP yield per cytosolic NADH than matrix-NADH entry through the malate-aspartate shuttle. It is not the triglyceride-synthesis pathway despite a shared metabolite.
