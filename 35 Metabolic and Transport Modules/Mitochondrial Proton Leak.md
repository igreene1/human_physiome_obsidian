# Mitochondrial Proton Leak

**Function:** Dissipates part of the proton-motive force without ATP synthesis.

**Where:** Inner mitochondrial membrane.

## Mechanism

1. Respiratory electron transport builds membrane potential and pH gradients.
2. Basal or regulated conductance permits protons to return without ATP-synthase coupling.
3. More substrate oxidation may be needed to maintain ATP output.
4. In thermogenic adipocytes, UCP1 supports regulated heat production.

## Control and constraints

Leak is not uniformly pathological. UCP1 is stimulated in an appropriate fatty-acid/signalling context and restrained by purine nucleotides. Other uncoupling-protein family members should not all be assigned identical thermogenic roles.
