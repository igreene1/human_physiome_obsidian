# Purine Synthesis and Salvage

**Function:** Builds or reuses purine rings to maintain nucleotide pools.

**Where:** Cytosolic multienzyme networks.

## Mechanism

1. PRPP supplies an activated ribose platform for de novo ring assembly.
2. Nitrogen/carbon contributions from glutamine, glycine, aspartate, bicarbonate and formyl-THF generate IMP.
3. Branch reactions convert IMP to AMP or GMP.
4. HPRT1/APRT salvage selected bases, while feedback restrains unnecessary synthesis.

## Control and constraints

AMP and GMP branches use different nitrogen/energy donors. Salvage saves biosynthetic cost. Purine catabolism in humans ends largely at uric acid because functional uricase is absent.
