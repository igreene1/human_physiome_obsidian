# Heme Synthesis and Degradation

**Function:** Builds haemoprotein cofactor and recycles its iron/pigment components.

**Where:** Mitochondria/cytosol of synthesising cells and macrophage/hepatic clearance systems.

## Mechanism

1. ALAS initiates synthesis from glycine/succinyl-CoA; cytosolic steps build a tetrapyrrole.
2. Mitochondrial finishing reactions generate protoporphyrin IX and insert Fe2+ through FECH.
3. Heme is incorporated into globins, cytochromes and other proteins.
4. Heme oxygenase and biliverdin reductase begin degradation; iron is recycled and bilirubin routed to excretion.

## Control and constraints

Biosynthesis and degradation are distinct branches, not a reversible eight-step pathway. ALAS1 and erythroid ALAS2 have different regulatory controls; free heme is potentially damaging.
