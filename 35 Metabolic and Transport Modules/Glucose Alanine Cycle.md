# Glucose Alanine Cycle

**Function:** Transfers muscle nitrogen and carbon toward hepatic disposal/glucose production.

**Where:** Skeletal muscle, circulation and liver.

## Mechanism

1. Muscle transamination transfers amino nitrogen to pyruvate, forming alanine.
2. Alanine travels to liver.
3. Alanine aminotransferase regenerates pyruvate and glutamate in the hepatic context.
4. Pyruvate supports gluconeogenesis while nitrogen can enter ureagenesis.

## Control and constraints

This is an inter-organ flux pattern rather than a unique isolated enzyme pathway. Glutamine provides another major route for nitrogen transport with different tissue destinations.
