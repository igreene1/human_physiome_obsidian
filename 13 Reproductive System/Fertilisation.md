# Fertilisation

**Function:** Combines parental genomes and activates early embryonic development.

**Where:** Usually the uterine tube, involving oocyte and capacitated sperm.

## Mechanism

1. Sperm undergo capacitation and appropriate acrosomal changes to interact with oocyte investments.
2. Sperm-oocyte membrane recognition/fusion involves complementary proteins including IZUMO1 and JUNO.
3. Sperm-derived activation signalling triggers oocyte calcium oscillations.
4. Cortical granule release modifies the zona; meiosis II completes and pronuclear programmes precede the first mitosis.

## Control and constraints

Only a small fraction of sperm reach the fertilisation site. Mammalian polyspermy prevention is not adequately described by a universal fast electrical block; zona and membrane mechanisms are important.
