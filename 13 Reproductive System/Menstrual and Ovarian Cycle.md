# Menstrual and Ovarian Cycle

**Function:** Coordinates follicle development, ovulation and a receptive endometrium.

**Where:** Hypothalamic-pituitary-ovarian axis and uterine endometrium.

## Mechanism

1. Rising FSH supports follicular growth; estradiol and inhibin modify feedback and follicle selection.
2. Sustained high estradiol in the appropriate context promotes an LH surge.
3. Ovulation is followed by corpus-luteum progesterone/estradiol secretion and secretory endometrial change.
4. Without a maintained pregnancy signal, luteal regression leads to steroid withdrawal and menstruation.

## Control and constraints

Cycle duration and ovulation timing vary. Progesterone withdrawal affects inflammatory, vascular and matrix-remodelling processes; menstruation is not simply a passive overflow of blood. This map is not a diagnostic guide to bleeding.
