# Implantation and Placentation

**Function:** Establishes maternal-embryonic attachment and an exchange interface.

**Where:** Blastocyst trophectoderm, receptive endometrium and developing placental villi.

## Mechanism

1. Blastocyst hatching permits apposition and attachment to receptive epithelium.
2. Trophoblast differentiation generates invasive and exchange-associated cell populations.
3. Decidual cells and immune populations regulate invasion and local tissue responses.
4. Villi and maternal vascular remodelling establish exchange with fetal circulation.

## Control and constraints

Maternal and fetal blood normally remain separated by placental tissues rather than freely mixing. Invasion is spatially and temporally controlled; placental transfer selectivity differs among gases, nutrients, antibodies and drugs.
