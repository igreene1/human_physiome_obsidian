# Parturition

**Function:** Coordinates cervical remodelling and uterine contractions for birth.

**Where:** Myometrium, cervix, fetal membranes, placenta and neuroendocrine circuits.

## Mechanism

1. Near-term tissue signalling increases uterine excitability and gap-junction coupling.
2. Prostaglandins and local remodelling soften/dilate the cervix.
3. Oxytocin and other uterotonic signals increase myometrial Ca2+ and contraction.
4. Cervical stretch can reinforce oxytocin release until delivery removes the feedback stimulus.

## Control and constraints

The onset of human labour is multifactorial; it is not explained by a simple fall in circulating progesterone alone. Mechanical, inflammatory, fetal and placental signals interact.
