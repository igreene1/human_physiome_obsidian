# Lactation

**Function:** Produces milk and ejects it in response to infant demand.

**Where:** Mammary alveolar epithelial and myoepithelial cells; hypothalamic-pituitary circuits.

## Mechanism

1. Pregnancy prepares secretory tissue; placental hormone withdrawal after birth permits copious secretion.
2. Prolactin supports milk synthesis, with nutrient uptake and lactose/lipid/protein production in alveolar cells.
3. Suckling reduces dopaminergic restraint of prolactin and recruits oxytocin release.
4. Oxytocin contracts myoepithelial cells to eject milk; milk removal helps sustain production.

## Control and constraints

Milk synthesis and milk ejection are separate processes. Local breast feedback and removal are important alongside hormones. Colostrum and mature milk differ in composition.
