# Pregnancy Physiology

**Function:** Supports fetal growth while adapting maternal circulation, metabolism and organ function.

**Where:** Maternal endocrine, cardiovascular, renal, respiratory and reproductive systems.

## Mechanism

1. Placental/endocrine signals maintain pregnancy and modify maternal tissue responses.
2. Plasma volume and cardiac output rise while vascular resistance changes.
3. Renal filtration and ventilatory drive increase, altering several baseline measurements.
4. Later metabolic adaptation directs more nutrient availability toward the fetoplacental unit.

## Control and constraints

Changes vary by gestational stage and individual. Plasma-volume expansion can lower haemoglobin concentration despite increased total red-cell mass. Maternal adaptations cannot be inferred from one nonpregnant reference range alone.
