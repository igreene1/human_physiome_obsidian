# Oogenesis and Folliculogenesis

**Function:** Maintains and matures oocytes within hormonally coordinated follicles.

**Where:** Ovarian follicles and surrounding stromal/theca cells.

## Mechanism

1. Oocytes enter meiosis before birth and normally arrest in prophase I within primordial follicles.
2. Follicular recruitment and growth involve oocyte-granulosa signalling before and during gonadotropin-sensitive stages.
3. Theca and granulosa cells cooperate in steroid synthesis.
4. The LH surge supports meiotic resumption, ovulation and luteinisation; the secondary oocyte arrests again at metaphase II.

## Control and constraints

Completion of meiosis II normally follows fertilisation. Follicular atresia is common and regulated. The two-cell, two-gonadotropin model captures steroid cooperation but not every local signal.
