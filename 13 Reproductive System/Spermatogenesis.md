# Spermatogenesis

**Function:** Produces haploid sperm with specialised structure and chromatin.

**Where:** Seminiferous epithelium with Sertoli-cell support.

## Mechanism

1. Spermatogonia maintain stem/progenitor pools and produce differentiating cells.
2. Primary spermatocytes undergo meiosis to generate haploid spermatids.
3. Spermiogenesis reshapes nuclei, acrosomes, flagella and cytoplasm.
4. Sperm released into the lumen mature functionally during epididymal transit.

## Control and constraints

LH supports Leydig-cell testosterone; FSH and intratesticular androgens support Sertoli functions. Sperm production, epididymal maturation and capacitation after ejaculation are distinct stages.
