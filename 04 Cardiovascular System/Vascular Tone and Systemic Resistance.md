# Vascular Tone and Systemic Resistance

**Function:** Controls regional perfusion and the relationship between flow and pressure.

**Where:** Arteriolar smooth muscle, endothelium and perivascular nerves.

## Mechanism

1. Contractile stimuli increase smooth-muscle cytosolic Ca2+ and/or calcium sensitivity.
2. Ca2+-calmodulin activates myosin light-chain kinase, promoting cross-bridge cycling.
3. Endothelial nitric oxide activates smooth-muscle cGMP pathways and favours relaxation.
4. Metabolites, myogenic responses, hormones and sympathetic activity set local tone.

## Control and constraints

Radius has a strong effect on resistance, but ideal Poiseuille assumptions do not fully describe pulsatile living networks. Veins primarily influence capacitance as well as resistance.
