# Regional Blood Flow

**Function:** Distributes cardiac output according to local needs and organ-specific priorities.

**Where:** Coronary, cerebral, renal, splanchnic, muscle and skin beds.

## Mechanism

1. Local metabolism generates signals that couple arteriolar dilation to demand.
2. Pressure-induced myogenic responses help stabilise flow across pressure changes.
3. Autonomic and endocrine signals redistribute flow during stress or exercise.
4. Organ-specific constraints, such as coronary compression during systole, shape delivery.

## Control and constraints

Autoregulation operates over a finite range and is not identical across tissues. Cerebral flow responds strongly to CO2; renal blood flow also serves filtration; skin flow participates in heat exchange.
