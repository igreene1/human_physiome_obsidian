# Cardiac Cycle

**Function:** Produces directional blood flow through coordinated pressure changes and passive valves.

**Where:** Atria, ventricles and valves.

## Mechanism

1. Ventricular relaxation lowers pressure; AV valves open when atrial pressure exceeds ventricular pressure.
2. Filling includes an early passive phase and, in sinus rhythm, an atrial contribution.
3. Ventricular pressure rises with contraction, closing AV valves before semilunar opening.
4. Ejection ends as arterial pressure exceeds ventricular pressure; semilunar valves close and isovolumetric relaxation begins.

## Control and constraints

Valves open and close because of pressure differences, not active valve muscle. Preload, afterload, contractility, compliance and heart rate alter pressure-volume behaviour.
