# Cardiac Electrophysiology

**Function:** Coordinates rhythmic excitation so chambers contract in an effective sequence.

**Where:** SA node, atria, AV node, His-Purkinje system and ventricular myocardium.

## Mechanism

1. Pacemaker cells undergo spontaneous diastolic depolarisation through interacting membrane and calcium-clock mechanisms.
2. Atrial excitation reaches the AV node, where conduction slows.
3. His-Purkinje conduction rapidly distributes excitation through ventricles.
4. Working myocytes use fast Na+ upstrokes, calcium-supported plateaus and K+-mediated repolarisation.

## Control and constraints

Nodal upstrokes depend mainly on Ca2+ current. Refractoriness constrains re-excitation and supports ordered contraction. An ECG records summed electrical activity, not contraction force itself.
