# Blood Pressure Regulation

**Function:** Maintains organ perfusion across changes in posture, activity and fluid balance.

**Where:** Arterial sensors, medulla, autonomic effectors and kidneys.

## Mechanism

1. Arterial stretch changes baroreceptor firing through glossopharyngeal/vagal afferents.
2. Brainstem circuits adjust cardiac sympathetic/parasympathetic drive and vascular sympathetic tone.
3. Cardiac output and vascular resistance change arterial pressure within seconds.
4. Renal pressure-natriuresis and endocrine regulation adjust volume on longer timescales.

## Control and constraints

Mean arterial pressure is approximately cardiac output times systemic vascular resistance plus central venous pressure. Baroreflexes adapt/reset and do not alone explain long-term pressure regulation.
