# Excitation-Contraction Coupling in Heart

**Function:** Converts a cardiac action potential into reversible force generation.

**Where:** Ventricular/atrial cardiomyocyte membrane, sarcoplasmic reticulum and sarcomeres.

## Mechanism

1. L-type Ca2+ channels open during depolarisation and admit trigger calcium.
2. RyR2 channels release additional calcium from the sarcoplasmic reticulum.
3. Calcium binds troponin C, allowing actin-myosin cycling.
4. SERCA2a, sodium-calcium exchange and other routes lower cytosolic calcium for relaxation.

## Control and constraints

Beta1 signalling increases calcium cycling and accelerates relaxation through phosphorylation targets including phospholamban. Unlike skeletal muscle, cardiac SR release normally depends strongly on extracellular trigger calcium.
