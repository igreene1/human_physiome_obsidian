# Microcirculation and Capillary Exchange

**Function:** Exchanges gases, nutrients and water while maintaining interstitial fluid balance.

**Where:** Capillary endothelium, glycocalyx, interstitium and lymphatics.

## Mechanism

1. Diffusion moves gases and solutes according to gradients, surface area and permeability.
2. Hydrostatic and oncotic forces across the endothelial barrier govern fluid filtration.
3. The endothelial glycocalyx and subglycocalyx protein environment influence effective oncotic gradients.
4. Lymphatics return much of the net filtered fluid and escaped protein to circulation.

## Control and constraints

Sustained venous-end capillary reabsorption is not the default for most tissues under the revised Starling model; transient reabsorption and specialised beds are exceptions. Inflammation changes permeability, not just pressure.
