# Pentose Phosphate Pathway

**Function:** Supplies NADPH and ribose phosphates while redistributing carbohydrate carbon.

**Where:** Cytosol.

## Mechanism

1. G6PD initiates oxidative processing of glucose 6-phosphate.
2. PGLS and PGD complete the oxidative branch, yielding ribulose 5-phosphate, two NADPH and CO2.
3. Isomerase/epimerase reactions provide ribose and xylulose phosphates.
4. Transketolase and transaldolase reversibly reconnect pentose carbon to glycolysis.

## Control and constraints

The pathway does not directly produce ATP. Oxidative versus non-oxidative flux changes with requirements for NADPH and ribose. Erythrocyte antioxidant defence is especially dependent on oxidative PPP capacity.
