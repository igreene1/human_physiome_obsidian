# Nucleotide Metabolism

**Function:** Produces, recycles and degrades nucleotides for nucleic acids, signalling and energy transfer.

**Where:** Cytosol and mitochondria with pathway-specific compartmentation.

## Mechanism

1. Purine synthesis builds a ring on PRPP; pyrimidine synthesis builds a ring before attachment to ribose phosphate.
2. Salvage enzymes reuse bases and nucleosides, reducing synthetic cost.
3. Ribonucleotide reductase supplies deoxyribonucleotides; folate-dependent chemistry supports thymidylate synthesis.
4. Catabolism disposes of bases; human purine breakdown terminates largely as uric acid.

## Control and constraints

Feedback balances nucleotide pools; both shortage and imbalance can damage replication fidelity. Human cells lack functional uricase. ATP/GTP have roles beyond being DNA/RNA precursors.
