# Ketone Body Metabolism

**Function:** Transfers hepatic fatty-acid-derived carbon to other tissues as water-soluble fuels.

**Where:** Liver mitochondria for synthesis; extrahepatic mitochondria for utilisation.

## Mechanism

1. Acetyl-CoA is condensed to acetoacetyl-CoA and mitochondrial HMG-CoA.
2. HMGCL releases acetoacetate; BDH1 interconverts it with beta-hydroxybutyrate.
3. Ketones enter circulation and are transported into consuming tissues.
4. OXCT1 and thiolase generate acetyl-CoA for oxidation outside liver.

## Control and constraints

Liver produces ketones but lacks appreciable SCOT-mediated utilisation. Brain ketone use rises with prolonged fasting; mature red cells cannot use them. Physiological ketosis and ketoacidosis are not interchangeable concepts.
