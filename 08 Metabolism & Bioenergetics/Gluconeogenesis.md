# Gluconeogenesis

**Function:** Produces glucose from non-carbohydrate precursors when required.

**Where:** Liver and kidney; reactions span mitochondria, cytosol and ER.

## Mechanism

1. Lactate, glycerol and glucogenic amino-acid carbon enter at different points.
2. PC and PEPCK bypass pyruvate kinase; reversible glycolytic steps run toward hexose phosphate.
3. FBP1 bypasses PFK1 and G6PC1 releases free glucose.
4. Hormonal, substrate and redox signals coordinate production with systemic demand.

## Control and constraints

Making one glucose from two pyruvate requires four ATP, two GTP and two NADH. Even-chain fatty acids do not supply net glucose carbon via acetyl-CoA; triglyceride glycerol can. The kidney's contribution increases in relevant fasting/acid-base states.
