# Pyruvate and Lactate Metabolism

**Function:** Distributes glycolytic carbon among oxidation, glucose production and inter-tissue redox exchange.

**Where:** Cytosol, mitochondrial matrix and circulating lactate pools.

## Mechanism

1. LDH interconverts lactate and pyruvate according to redox and substrate conditions.
2. The mitochondrial pyruvate carrier delivers pyruvate for PDH or pyruvate carboxylase.
3. PDH produces acetyl-CoA irreversibly; PC produces oxaloacetate for anaplerosis or gluconeogenesis.
4. Lactate can circulate to oxidative tissues or glucose-producing organs.

## Control and constraints

Lactate production does not prove absence of oxygen. Humans cannot obtain net glucose from acetyl-CoA through the TCA cycle, but lactate/pyruvate carbon can support gluconeogenesis. Tissue flux depends on demand and transport.
