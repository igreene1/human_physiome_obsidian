# Electron Transport and Oxidative Phosphorylation

**Function:** Converts reducing power into ATP through membrane energy coupling.

**Where:** Inner mitochondrial membrane and matrix.

## Mechanism

1. Complex I and other entry routes deliver electrons to ubiquinone.
2. Complex III transfers electrons to cytochrome c; complex IV reduces oxygen to water.
3. Complexes I, III and IV contribute to proton translocation; complex II does not pump protons.
4. ATP synthase uses the gradient while nucleotide/phosphate carriers connect synthesis to cellular use.

## Control and constraints

Approximate P/O values are 2.5 ATP per matrix NADH and 1.5 per Q-level electron pair under common accounting. These are not fixed measured yields in every cell. Cytosolic NADH transfers reducing equivalents through shuttles rather than crossing intact.
