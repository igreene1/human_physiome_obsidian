# Thermogenesis and Energy Expenditure

**Function:** Converts chemical energy into heat and supports whole-body energy use.

**Where:** Skeletal muscle, brown/beige adipose tissue and all metabolically active organs.

## Mechanism

1. Basal ion pumping, biosynthesis and turnover dissipate energy as heat.
2. Shivering increases skeletal-muscle ATP turnover.
3. Sympathetic activation can recruit UCP1-mediated mitochondrial proton conductance in thermogenic adipocytes.
4. Diet, activity, body composition and endocrine state modify total expenditure.

## Control and constraints

UCP1 uncouples substrate oxidation from ATP synthesis; it does not itself generate ATP. Human brown-fat contribution varies with age and exposure. Temperature regulation also depends on heat loss and behaviour.
