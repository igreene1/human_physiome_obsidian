# Glycolysis

**Function:** Converts glucose to pyruvate through ten principal reactions.

**Where:** Cytosol of nearly all cell types.

## Mechanism

1. Hexokinase/glucokinase and PFK1 consume two ATP in the investment phase.
2. Aldolase and triose-phosphate isomerase generate two glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate molecules per glucose.
3. GAPDH forms NADH; phosphoglycerate kinase and pyruvate kinase together generate four ATP.
4. Pyruvate enters oxidative metabolism or is reduced to lactate to regenerate NAD+.

## Control and constraints

Net yield is two ATP and two NADH per glucose to pyruvate. Oxygen is not a direct glycolytic substrate. PFK1, substrate supply, redox availability and isoenzyme-specific regulation shape flux; the ten reaction notes below provide the detailed chain.
