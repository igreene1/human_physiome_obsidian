# Lipolysis and Beta Oxidation

**Function:** Mobilises stored fat and oxidises fatty acyl chains for energy.

**Where:** Adipose lipid droplets and mitochondria of oxidative tissues.

## Mechanism

1. ATGL, HSL and monoglyceride lipase sequentially release fatty acids and glycerol from stored triglyceride.
2. Fatty acids travel largely albumin-bound and are activated to acyl-CoA.
3. Long-chain acyl groups use the carnitine shuttle to enter the mitochondrial matrix.
4. Repeated oxidation, hydration, oxidation and thiolysis yield acetyl-CoA and reducing equivalents.

## Control and constraints

Insulin restrains adipose lipolysis; catecholamine signalling promotes it. Peroxisomes handle selected very-long-chain/branched substrates differently. Odd-chain oxidation yields propionyl-CoA as well as acetyl units.
