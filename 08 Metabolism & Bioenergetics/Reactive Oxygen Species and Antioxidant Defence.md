# Reactive Oxygen Species and Antioxidant Defence

**Function:** Uses and constrains reactive species to preserve signalling and molecular integrity.

**Where:** Mitochondria, cytosol, peroxisomes, membranes and extracellular spaces.

## Mechanism

1. Electron leakage, oxidases and other reactions generate superoxide or peroxide.
2. SOD converts superoxide into hydrogen peroxide.
3. Peroxiredoxin, glutathione-peroxidase and catalase systems remove peroxide in different compartments.
4. NADPH-dependent recycling and NRF2-linked transcription replenish reducing capacity and protective proteins.

## Control and constraints

Reactive oxygen species are not uniformly harmful; location, concentration and duration determine effects. Removal must be coupled to reducing-power supply. Lipid peroxide control includes GPX4 and relates to ferroptosis.
