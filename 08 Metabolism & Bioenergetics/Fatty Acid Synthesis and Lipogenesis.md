# Fatty Acid Synthesis and Lipogenesis

**Function:** Converts excess carbon and reducing power into fatty acids and storage lipid.

**Where:** Cytosol, ER and lipid droplets, especially liver/adipose tissue.

## Mechanism

1. Citrate export and ATP-citrate lyase supply cytosolic acetyl-CoA.
2. ACC forms malonyl-CoA using ATP and biotin.
3. FASN repeats condensation and NADPH-dependent processing to produce palmitate.
4. Elongation, desaturation and esterification generate diverse lipids and triglycerides.

## Control and constraints

Insulin and nutrient-responsive transcription promote capacity; AMPK restrains synthesis when energy is limited. Malonyl-CoA inhibits CPT1. Humans cannot introduce all double-bond positions, making certain fatty acids dietary essentials.
