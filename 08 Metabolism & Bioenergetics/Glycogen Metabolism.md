# Glycogen Metabolism

**Function:** Stores glucose in a branched polymer and mobilises it according to tissue need.

**Where:** Cytosolic glycogen particles, especially in liver and skeletal muscle.

## Mechanism

1. PGM1 and UGP2 provide UDP-glucose for glycogen synthase.
2. Synthase extends alpha-1,4 chains and GBE1 adds alpha-1,6 branches.
3. Phosphorylase releases glucose 1-phosphate until branch structures require AGL.
4. Liver can release glucose through G6PC1; muscle uses most glycogen-derived carbon locally.

## Control and constraints

Insulin generally favours synthesis; catecholamines promote mobilisation. Glucagon directly regulates liver much more than skeletal muscle. Ca2+ and AMP connect muscle glycogenolysis to contraction and energy demand.
