# TCA Cycle

**Function:** Oxidises acetyl units and supplies intermediates for biosynthesis.

**Where:** Mitochondrial matrix, with succinate dehydrogenase in the inner membrane.

## Mechanism

1. Citrate synthase condenses acetyl-CoA with oxaloacetate.
2. Aconitase and oxidative decarboxylations generate 2-oxoglutarate and succinyl-CoA.
3. Succinyl-CoA synthetase forms GTP or ATP; succinate oxidation feeds ubiquinone.
4. Fumarase and malate dehydrogenase regenerate oxaloacetate.

## Control and constraints

Per acetyl-CoA, the canonical cycle yields two CO2, three NADH, one electron pair via enzyme-bound FAD, and one GTP/ATP. Oxygen is required indirectly through respiratory redox recycling. Withdrawn intermediates require anaplerotic replacement.
