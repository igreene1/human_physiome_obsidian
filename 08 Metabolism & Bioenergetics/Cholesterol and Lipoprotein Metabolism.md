# Cholesterol and Lipoprotein Metabolism

**Function:** Supplies membranes and steroid/bile precursors while distributing insoluble lipids.

**Where:** Liver, intestine, peripheral tissues and circulating lipoproteins.

## Mechanism

1. HMG-CoA reductase controls a major early step in sterol synthesis.
2. Chylomicrons distribute dietary triglycerides; hepatic VLDL distributes endogenous triglycerides.
3. Lipoprotein lipase removes triglyceride, and remnant/LDL uptake returns lipid to cells.
4. HDL-associated pathways participate in cholesterol efflux and redistribution.

## Control and constraints

LDL receptor trafficking, PCSK9, apolipoproteins and particle turnover affect concentrations. Cholesterol and triglycerides are different molecules. HDL concentration alone is not a complete measure of reverse cholesterol transport function.
