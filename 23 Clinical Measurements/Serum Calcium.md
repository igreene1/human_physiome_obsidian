# Serum Calcium

**Typical units:** mmol/L

## What it represents

Circulating calcium, partitioned into ionised, protein-bound and complexed fractions; tightly related to PTH/vitamin D, kidney, gut and bone.

## Physiological links

- [[Calcium and Phosphate Homeostasis]]
- [[Calcium PTH Vitamin D Axis]]
- [[Bone Mineral Homeostasis]]

## Interpretation rule

Treat this measurement as an observation of a physiological system, not as the system itself. Always ask what processes can move the value and what compensations may keep it deceptively normal.
