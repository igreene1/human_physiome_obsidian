# PaO2

**Typical units:** mmHg or kPa

## What it represents

Arterial partial pressure of dissolved oxygen, reflecting alveolar oxygen transfer and gas-exchange performance rather than total blood oxygen content alone.

## Physiological links

- [[Alveolar Gas Exchange]]
- [[Ventilation Perfusion Matching]]
- [[Oxygen Transport]]

## Interpretation rule

Treat this measurement as an observation of a physiological system, not as the system itself. Always ask what processes can move the value and what compensations may keep it deceptively normal.
