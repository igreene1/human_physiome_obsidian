# TSH and Free T4

**Typical units:** mIU/L and pmol/L/ng/dL depending lab

## What it represents

Paired measurements that reflect hypothalamic-pituitary-thyroid feedback: TSH is the pituitary signal and free T4 is a major circulating thyroid hormone measurement.

## Interpretation rule

Treat this measurement as an observation of a physiological system, not as the system itself. Always ask what processes can move the value and what compensations may keep it deceptively normal.
