# Serum Osmolality

**Typical units:** mOsm/kg

## What it represents

Concentration of osmotically active particles in plasma; sensed centrally to regulate thirst and vasopressin.

## Physiological links

- [[Water and Osmolality Homeostasis]]
- [[V2 Receptor]]
- [[Aquaporin 2]]
- [[Urine Osmolality]]

## Interpretation rule

Treat this measurement as an observation of a physiological system, not as the system itself. Always ask what processes can move the value and what compensations may keep it deceptively normal.
