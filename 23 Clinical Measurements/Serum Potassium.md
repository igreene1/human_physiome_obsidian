# Serum Potassium

**Typical units:** mmol/L

## What it represents

Extracellular K+ concentration determined by intake/excretion and shifts between intracellular and extracellular compartments.

## Physiological links

- [[Potassium Homeostasis]]
- [[Collecting Duct Principal Cell]]
- [[Na-K ATPase]]
- [[Resting Membrane Potential]]

## Interpretation rule

Treat this measurement as an observation of a physiological system, not as the system itself. Always ask what processes can move the value and what compensations may keep it deceptively normal.
