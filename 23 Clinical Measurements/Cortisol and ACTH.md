# Cortisol and ACTH

**Typical units:** lab/time dependent

## What it represents

Measurements of adrenal glucocorticoid output and pituitary drive; interpretation depends strongly on circadian timing, stress state and sampling context.

## Interpretation rule

Treat this measurement as an observation of a physiological system, not as the system itself. Always ask what processes can move the value and what compensations may keep it deceptively normal.
