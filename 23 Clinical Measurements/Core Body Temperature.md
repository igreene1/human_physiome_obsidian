# Core Body Temperature

**Typical units:** °C

## What it represents

Integrated outcome of heat production, heat transfer and heat loss under hypothalamic/autonomic/behavioural control.

## Physiological links

- [[Core Temperature Homeostasis]]

## Interpretation rule

Treat this measurement as an observation of a physiological system, not as the system itself. Always ask what processes can move the value and what compensations may keep it deceptively normal.
