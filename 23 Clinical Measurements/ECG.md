# ECG

**Typical units:** electrical voltage vs time

## What it represents

Surface recording of summed cardiac electrical activity. P wave, PR interval, QRS complex, ST-T pattern and rhythm provide windows into impulse formation/conduction and ventricular depolarisation/repolarisation.

## Interpretation rule

Treat this measurement as an observation of a physiological system, not as the system itself. Always ask what processes can move the value and what compensations may keep it deceptively normal.
