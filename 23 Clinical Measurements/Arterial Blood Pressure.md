# Arterial Blood Pressure

**Typical units:** mmHg

## What it represents

Pressure within systemic arteries. Mean pressure is strongly influenced by cardiac output and systemic vascular resistance, while pulsatile values also depend on arterial compliance and stroke dynamics.

## Interpretation rule

Treat this measurement as an observation of a physiological system, not as the system itself. Always ask what processes can move the value and what compensations may keep it deceptively normal.
