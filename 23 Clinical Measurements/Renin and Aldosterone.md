# Renin and Aldosterone

**Typical units:** assay dependent

## What it represents

Paired measurements used to examine the RAAS; posture, sodium intake, medications, potassium and volume state can materially alter levels.

## Interpretation rule

Treat this measurement as an observation of a physiological system, not as the system itself. Always ask what processes can move the value and what compensations may keep it deceptively normal.
