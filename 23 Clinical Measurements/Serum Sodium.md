# Serum Sodium

**Typical units:** mmol/L

## What it represents

Major extracellular cation concentration; clinically reflects water balance relative to exchangeable body Na+/K+ more than total-body sodium alone.

## Physiological links

- [[Sodium Homeostasis]]
- [[Water and Osmolality Homeostasis]]
- [[Serum Osmolality]]

## Interpretation rule

Treat this measurement as an observation of a physiological system, not as the system itself. Always ask what processes can move the value and what compensations may keep it deceptively normal.
