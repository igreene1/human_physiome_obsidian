# Serum Glucose

**Typical units:** mmol/L

## What it represents

Concentration of glucose in circulating serum/plasma; instantaneous snapshot of the balance between intestinal input, hepatic production, tissue uptake and hormonal regulation.

## Physiological links

- [[Blood Glucose Homeostasis]]
- [[Insulin and Glucagon]]

- [[Gluconeogenesis]]

## Interpretation rule

Treat this measurement as an observation of a physiological system, not as the system itself. Always ask what processes can move the value and what compensations may keep it deceptively normal.
