# PaCO2

**Typical units:** mmHg or kPa

## What it represents

Arterial partial pressure of CO2, strongly determined by alveolar ventilation relative to CO2 production.

## Physiological links

- [[Carbon Dioxide Transport]]
- [[Control of Breathing]]
- [[Pulmonary Ventilation]]
- [[Arterial pH and Bicarbonate]]

## Interpretation rule

Treat this measurement as an observation of a physiological system, not as the system itself. Always ask what processes can move the value and what compensations may keep it deceptively normal.
