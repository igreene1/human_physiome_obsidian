# HbA1c

**Typical units:** % or mmol/mol

## What it represents

Glycated haemoglobin reflecting integrated glucose exposure over the lifespan of circulating erythrocytes, weighted toward more recent weeks rather than a single moment.

- [[Blood Glucose Homeostasis]]

- [[Haemoglobin and Red Cell Function]]

## Interpretation rule

Treat this measurement as an observation of a physiological system, not as the system itself. Always ask what processes can move the value and what compensations may keep it deceptively normal.
