# Creatinine and eGFR

**Typical units:** µmol/L or mg/dL; mL/min/1.73 m²

## What it represents

Serum creatinine is influenced by creatinine generation and renal clearance; eGFR equations use creatinine and demographic inputs to estimate filtration rather than directly measure it.

## Interpretation rule

Treat this measurement as an observation of a physiological system, not as the system itself. Always ask what processes can move the value and what compensations may keep it deceptively normal.
