# Urine Osmolality

**Typical units:** mOsm/kg

## What it represents

Concentration of osmotically active particles in urine; reflects the kidney’s concentrating/diluting response to water balance and solute handling.

- [[Urine Concentration and Dilution]]

## Interpretation rule

Treat this measurement as an observation of a physiological system, not as the system itself. Always ask what processes can move the value and what compensations may keep it deceptively normal.
