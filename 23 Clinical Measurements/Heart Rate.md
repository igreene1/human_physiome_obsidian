# Heart Rate

**Typical units:** beats/min

## What it represents

Frequency of cardiac cycles; reflects sinoatrial pacing modified by autonomic, endocrine, temperature, activity and other influences.

## Physiological links

- [[Sinoatrial Node]]
- [[Pacemaker Cell]]
- [[Autonomic Nervous System]]
- [[Cardiac Output and Venous Return]]

## Interpretation rule

Treat this measurement as an observation of a physiological system, not as the system itself. Always ask what processes can move the value and what compensations may keep it deceptively normal.
