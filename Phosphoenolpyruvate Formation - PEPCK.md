# Phosphoenolpyruvate Formation - PEPCK

**Part of:** [[Gluconeogenesis]]

## Transformation

Oxaloacetate + GTP → phosphoenolpyruvate + GDP + CO2

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | PCK1 (cytosol) or PCK2 (mitochondria) |
| Compartment | Cytosol or mitochondrial matrix, depending on isoenzyme |
| Physiological direction | Decarboxylation and phosphorylation bypass pyruvate kinase together with PC |

## What controls this step

Hormonal transcriptional control is important in liver. Substrate route and redox balance determine use of mitochondrial versus cytosolic reactions; enzyme amount alone does not define total glucose output.
