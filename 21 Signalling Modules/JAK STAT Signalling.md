# JAK STAT Signalling

**Function:** Transmits cytokine and selected peptide-hormone signals to gene regulation.

**Where:** Receptors with associated JAK kinases and STAT proteins.

## Mechanism

1. Ligand changes receptor-subunit organisation and activates receptor-associated JAKs.
2. Receptor phosphorylation creates STAT docking sites.
3. Phosphorylated STATs form active complexes and enter the nucleus.
4. Target transcription changes immune, growth or metabolic programmes; SOCS and phosphatases restrain signalling.

## Control and constraints

Many cytokine receptors lack intrinsic tyrosine-kinase domains and depend on associated JAKs. Different receptors recruit different JAK/STAT combinations and do not all produce the same transcriptional output.
