# Calcium Calmodulin Signalling

**Function:** Decodes transient calcium changes into tissue-specific enzyme activity.

**Where:** Calcium microdomains and calmodulin-associated proteins.

## Mechanism

1. Calcium binding changes calmodulin conformation and target affinity.
2. Calmodulin activates targets including selected kinases, calcineurin, NOS and MLCK.
3. Target substrates change contraction, secretion, transcription or metabolism.
4. Calcium extrusion/sequestration and target deactivation restore baseline.

## Control and constraints

The same calcium rise can recruit different targets according to location, amplitude and duration. Calcineurin is a phosphatase, whereas CaMKs are kinases; their actions are not interchangeable.
