# Gq IP3 DAG Calcium Signalling

**Function:** Splits a membrane lipid signal into calcium and kinase-related branches.

**Where:** Plasma membrane PIP2, ER IP3 receptors and cytosolic effectors.

## Mechanism

1. Gq/11 activates PLCbeta in the canonical GPCR route.
2. PLC cleaves PIP2 into soluble IP3 and membrane-associated DAG.
3. IP3 opens ER calcium-release channels; DAG and calcium recruit conventional PKC isoforms in the appropriate context.
4. IP3 metabolism, DAG turnover and calcium pumping reduce the signal.

## Control and constraints

Not every PKC isoform requires calcium/DAG in the same way. PLCgamma can generate the same messengers downstream of tyrosine-kinase-linked pathways without Gq as the upstream trigger.
