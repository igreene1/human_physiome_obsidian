# mTOR Signalling

**Function:** Coordinates growth/nutrient signals through two functionally distinct protein complexes.

**Where:** Lysosome-associated mTORC1 and other mTORC2-associated membranes/complexes.

## Mechanism

1. Amino-acid-sensitive Rag machinery helps position mTORC1 where Rheb-GTP can activate it.
2. Growth-factor/energy pathways influence TSC-Rheb regulation.
3. mTORC1 regulates S6K and 4E-BP-associated translation and restrains selected autophagy-initiation steps.
4. mTORC2 has different components and supports AKT/SGK/PKC-associated processes.

## Control and constraints

Do not assign all mTORC1 nutrient/autophagy effects to mTORC2. mTORC1 is an integrator of permissive conditions, not a universal on/off switch for all protein synthesis.
