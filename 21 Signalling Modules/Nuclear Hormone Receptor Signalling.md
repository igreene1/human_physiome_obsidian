# Nuclear Hormone Receptor Signalling

**Function:** Links selected lipid-soluble ligands to transcriptional regulation.

**Where:** Cytosolic/nuclear receptor systems and chromatin.

## Mechanism

1. Ligand enters via diffusion or relevant transport and binds an intracellular receptor.
2. Receptor conformation changes interactions with chaperones, partners and coregulators.
3. DNA-bound complexes alter recruitment of transcriptional machinery and chromatin regulators.
4. Metabolism, ligand clearance and receptor/coregulator state determine duration.

## Control and constraints

Steroid and thyroid/retinoid receptor families differ in localisation and partner use. Some receptors are DNA-bound before ligand arrives; not every receptor moves from cytosol to nucleus after binding.
