# NF-kB Signalling

**Function:** Regulates inflammatory, survival and differentiation genes through controlled nuclear access.

**Where:** Canonical IKK/I-kappaB and noncanonical processing pathways.

## Mechanism

1. Many innate/cytokine inputs recruit signalling complexes that activate the canonical IKK module.
2. I-kappaB phosphorylation promotes ubiquitin-dependent degradation, releasing selected NF-kB dimers.
3. Nuclear dimers regulate context-dependent genes.
4. New I-kappaB and other feedback regulators restrain activity; a distinct NIK/IKKalpha-linked route processes p100 in noncanonical signalling.

## Control and constraints

NF-kB is a family of subunits/dimers, not one uniformly inflammatory protein. Ubiquitin scaffolds and proteolysis have different roles at different stages.
