# TGF Beta SMAD Signalling

**Function:** Coordinates differentiation, matrix production and immune/developmental responses.

**Where:** Type-II/type-I serine-threonine kinase receptors and SMAD complexes.

## Mechanism

1. Ligand engages receptor complexes and permits type-I receptor activation.
2. Receptors phosphorylate pathway-specific receptor SMADs.
3. R-SMADs form complexes with SMAD4 and regulate transcription with context-specific partners.
4. Inhibitory SMADs, trafficking and degradation provide feedback.

## Control and constraints

TGF-beta/activin commonly use SMAD2/3, whereas BMP-family routes commonly use SMAD1/5/9. Identical signalling components can yield different outcomes by cell context; non-SMAD branches also exist.
