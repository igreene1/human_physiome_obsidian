# AMPK Signalling

**Function:** Adjusts energy-consuming and energy-producing processes during cellular energy stress.

**Where:** AMPK complexes in multiple cellular locations.

## Mechanism

1. AMP/ADP binding influences protection/activation of the regulatory complex when energy charge falls.
2. Upstream kinases such as LKB1 or calcium-sensitive CaMKK2 phosphorylate the catalytic activation site in appropriate contexts.
3. AMPK restrains ATP-consuming synthesis, including ACC-associated lipid synthesis, and promotes selected catabolic adaptations.
4. Autophagy/mitochondrial programmes adjust longer-term capacity.

## Control and constraints

AMPK senses nucleotide state and upstream signals; it does not simply measure blood glucose. Isoform, location and tissue context determine targets.
