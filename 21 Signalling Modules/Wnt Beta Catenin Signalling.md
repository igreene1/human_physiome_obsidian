# Wnt Beta Catenin Signalling

**Function:** Changes transcription by regulating beta-catenin stability.

**Where:** Frizzled/LRP receptors, cytosolic destruction complex and nucleus.

## Mechanism

1. Without canonical ligand input, APC/AXIN/kinase machinery promotes beta-catenin phosphorylation and degradation.
2. Wnt receptor/co-receptor engagement reorganises this control through Dishevelled-associated signalling.
3. Stabilised beta-catenin accumulates and associates with TCF/LEF factors.
4. Target expression changes proliferation, differentiation or tissue patterning.

## Control and constraints

Beta-catenin also participates in cell adhesion. Wnt pathways include noncanonical branches that do not use beta-catenin as the central output.
