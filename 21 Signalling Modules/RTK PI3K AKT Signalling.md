# RTK PI3K AKT Signalling

**Function:** Coordinates metabolic, survival and growth responses downstream of selected receptors.

**Where:** Receptor/adaptor complexes, membrane phosphoinositides and intracellular targets.

## Mechanism

1. Activated receptor tyrosine kinases recruit adaptors such as IRS proteins in insulin signalling.
2. Class-I PI3K converts PIP2 to PIP3, recruiting PH-domain proteins.
3. PDK1 and mTORC2-associated phosphorylation help activate AKT.
4. AKT regulates targets including TBC1D4, GSK3, FOXO and TSC-associated control, according to cell context.

## Control and constraints

PTEN removes the PIP3 signal; phosphatases and feedback limit AKT. AKT is not the sole insulin branch, and liver does not depend on GLUT4 for glucose entry.
