# cAMP PKA Signalling

**Function:** Uses cAMP dynamics to regulate enzymes, ion channels, transcription and trafficking.

**Where:** Adenylyl cyclase-containing membranes and compartmentalised cytosolic complexes.

## Mechanism

1. Gs or other inputs regulate adenylyl cyclase conversion of ATP to cAMP.
2. cAMP binds PKA regulatory subunits, permitting catalytic activity in locally organised complexes.
3. PKA phosphorylates context-specific targets; cAMP also activates EPAC and some cyclic-nucleotide-sensitive channels.
4. Phosphodiesterases hydrolyse cAMP and phosphatases reverse substrate modifications.

## Control and constraints

cAMP is not uniformly mixed throughout the cell. AKAP scaffolds and local PDEs create distinct domains, explaining why the same messenger can control different outputs.
