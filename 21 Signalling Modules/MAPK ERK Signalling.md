# MAPK ERK Signalling

**Function:** Converts growth-factor and other inputs into regulated kinase and transcriptional responses.

**Where:** Membrane-associated Ras and cytosolic/nuclear kinase modules.

## Mechanism

1. Adaptor/GEF recruitment promotes Ras-GTP formation.
2. Ras-associated signals recruit RAF kinases.
3. RAF activates MEK, which activates ERK.
4. ERK phosphorylates cytosolic and nuclear targets that influence growth, differentiation or feedback.

## Control and constraints

Signal duration and localisation matter as much as presence. Phosphatases and feedback loops limit propagation; parallel MAPK modules such as JNK/p38 are distinct from the canonical ERK cascade.
