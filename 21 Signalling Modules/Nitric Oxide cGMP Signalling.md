# Nitric Oxide cGMP Signalling

**Function:** Transduces NO into short-range relaxation and other cell-specific responses.

**Where:** NO-generating cells and soluble guanylyl-cyclase-expressing targets.

## Mechanism

1. NOS generates NO from arginine in the relevant cell.
2. NO diffuses and binds the haem group of soluble guanylyl cyclase.
3. GTP conversion to cGMP activates effectors such as PKG.
4. Phosphodiesterases and NO removal terminate the signal.

## Control and constraints

Natriuretic peptides also generate cGMP through membrane guanylyl cyclases, a distinct input route. NO action depends on redox/haem state and local scavenging.
