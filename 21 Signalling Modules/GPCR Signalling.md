# GPCR Signalling

**Function:** Couples extracellular ligand binding to heterotrimeric G-protein and other pathways.

**Where:** Plasma membrane and signalling endosomes.

## Mechanism

1. Ligand-bound receptor promotes GDP-GTP exchange on a G-alpha subunit.
2. G-alpha-GTP and G-beta-gamma regulate effectors according to family.
3. Gs commonly stimulates adenylyl cyclase, Gi/o can inhibit it, and Gq/11 activates PLCbeta; G12/13 can recruit Rho-associated pathways.
4. Intrinsic GTPase activity, RGS proteins, receptor phosphorylation and trafficking terminate or reshape activity.

## Control and constraints

GPCR class does not uniquely determine physiological effect. G-beta-gamma has direct actions, including channel regulation; beta-arrestin-related signalling and compartmentation add further branches.
