# Creatine Kinase Phosphagen Transfer

**Part of:** [[Muscle Energy Systems]]

## Transformation

Phosphocreatine + ADP + H+ ⇌ creatine + ATP

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | CKM, CKB and mitochondrial creatine kinase isoforms |
| Compartment | Cytosol and mitochondrial intermembrane-space-associated sites |
| Physiological direction | Rapidly buffers ATP demand and transfers high-energy phosphate equivalents |

## What controls this step

Forward ATP regeneration dominates at exercise onset; recovery requires oxidative ATP to replenish phosphocreatine. Capacity is finite.
