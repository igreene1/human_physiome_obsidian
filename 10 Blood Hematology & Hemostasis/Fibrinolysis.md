# Fibrinolysis

**Function:** Removes fibrin after or during clot remodelling.

**Where:** Fibrin surface, plasma and endothelium.

## Mechanism

1. tPA and related activators convert plasminogen to plasmin, efficiently on fibrin surfaces.
2. Plasmin cleaves fibrin into soluble degradation products.
3. PAI-1 limits plasminogen activators and alpha2-antiplasmin neutralises free plasmin.
4. Clot structure and cross-linking influence susceptibility to lysis.

## Control and constraints

Fibrin turnover and clot formation can occur simultaneously. D-dimer derives from degradation of cross-linked fibrin and is not specific to a single cause of clotting or inflammation.
