# Platelet Activation

**Function:** Builds a rapidly formed platelet plug at vascular injury.

**Where:** Platelets, exposed vessel matrix and damaged endothelium.

## Mechanism

1. von Willebrand factor and collagen-associated receptors tether and activate platelets.
2. Intracellular calcium, lipid signalling and cytoskeletal changes promote shape change and granule release.
3. ADP, thromboxane and thrombin amplify recruitment.
4. Inside-out integrin signalling activates alphaIIbbeta3 for fibrinogen-mediated platelet bridging.

## Control and constraints

Platelets also expose phospholipid surfaces supporting coagulation. Intact endothelium normally opposes activation through NO, prostacyclin and ectonucleotidases. Adhesion, activation and aggregation are distinguishable steps.
