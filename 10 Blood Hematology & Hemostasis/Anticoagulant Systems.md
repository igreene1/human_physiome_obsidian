# Anticoagulant Systems

**Function:** Restricts clotting to the appropriate site and duration.

**Where:** Endothelium, plasma inhibitors and protein-C pathway.

## Mechanism

1. Antithrombin neutralises thrombin and selected activated coagulation factors.
2. TFPI limits tissue-factor/FVIIa/FXa initiation complexes.
3. Thrombin bound to thrombomodulin supports protein C activation.
4. Activated protein C with protein S inactivates Va/VIIIa, reducing amplification.

## Control and constraints

Thrombin can drive either procoagulant or regulatory actions depending on binding context. Blood flow and intact endothelial surfaces also limit spread; anticoagulant control is not the same as fibrin removal.
