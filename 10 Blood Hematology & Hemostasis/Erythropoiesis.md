# Erythropoiesis

**Function:** Produces red cells with sufficient haemoglobin and deformability for oxygen transport.

**Where:** Marrow erythroblastic islands and circulating reticulocytes.

## Mechanism

1. Renal hypoxia signalling increases EPO support of erythroid progenitors.
2. Proliferating precursors acquire haemoglobin using coordinated iron, heme and globin synthesis.
3. Nuclear extrusion and organelle removal generate reticulocytes.
4. Reticulocytes mature into anucleate red cells; improved oxygen delivery feeds back on EPO.

## Control and constraints

Iron, folate, B12 and other resources support different steps. Mature red cells cannot replace proteins through transcription/translation. A reticulocyte response is a delayed measure of marrow output, not instantaneous production.
