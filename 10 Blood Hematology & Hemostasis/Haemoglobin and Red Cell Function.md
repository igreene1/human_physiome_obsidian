# Haemoglobin and Red Cell Function

**Function:** Transports respiratory gases and supports buffering while traversing narrow vessels.

**Where:** Erythrocyte cytosol and membrane skeleton.

## Mechanism

1. Heme Fe2+ reversibly binds oxygen within globin subunits.
2. Cooperative transitions between affinity states support lung loading and tissue unloading.
3. Glycolysis supplies ATP, PPP supplies NADPH and 2,3-BPG adjusts oxygen affinity.
4. Membrane/skeletal proteins support deformability and ion/water balance.

## Control and constraints

Methemoglobin contains Fe3+ and requires reduction. Haemoglobin concentration, saturation and PaO2 describe different aspects of oxygen carriage. Cell shape and redox integrity affect survival independently of haemoglobin quantity.
