# Iron Homeostasis

**Function:** Distributes iron for haemoproteins while limiting redox toxicity.

**Where:** Duodenal enterocytes, macrophages, liver, plasma transferrin and marrow.

## Mechanism

1. Dietary iron enters enterocytes through heme/non-heme routes; ferroportin exports usable iron.
2. Transferrin carries ferric iron to transferrin-receptor-expressing cells.
3. Macrophages recycle iron from senescent erythrocytes, supplying much daily erythropoietic demand.
4. Hepcidin binds ferroportin and reduces export when iron/inflammatory signals favour retention.

## Control and constraints

The body has no major regulated iron-excretion pathway, so absorption and recycling control balance. Ferritin is also an acute-phase reactant. Erythroferrone links erythropoietic demand to hepcidin restraint.
