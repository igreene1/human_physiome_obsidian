# Coagulation Cascade

**Function:** Stabilises a haemostatic plug with a cross-linked fibrin network.

**Where:** Tissue-factor-bearing surfaces and activated platelet membranes.

## Mechanism

1. Tissue factor with FVIIa initiates activation of downstream proteases, including factor X.
2. Small thrombin amounts activate platelets and cofactors, supporting amplification.
3. Tenase/prothrombinase complexes on phospholipid surfaces generate more thrombin.
4. Thrombin converts fibrinogen to fibrin and activates factor XIII to cross-link it.

## Control and constraints

The living process is cell-surface coordinated; separate intrinsic/extrinsic cascades are teaching and laboratory frameworks. Calcium and vitamin-K-dependent gamma-carboxylation support membrane binding of selected factors.
