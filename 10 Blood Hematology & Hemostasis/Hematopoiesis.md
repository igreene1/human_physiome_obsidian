# Hematopoiesis

**Function:** Maintains blood-cell populations through stem-cell self-renewal and lineage differentiation.

**Where:** Bone marrow niches in adults; sites change during development.

## Mechanism

1. Hematopoietic stem cells integrate niche and systemic signals.
2. Progenitors progressively acquire lineage-biased transcriptional programmes.
3. Growth factors support proliferation, survival and maturation of specific populations.
4. Mature cells enter circulation while aged or damaged cells are cleared.

## Control and constraints

The hierarchy is useful but real differentiation trajectories are not a single rigid tree. EPO, thrombopoietin and colony-stimulating factors act differently. Cell counts reflect production, distribution and loss together.
