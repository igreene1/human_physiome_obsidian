# Malate Oxidation - MDH2

**Part of:** [[TCA Cycle]]

## Transformation

L-Malate + NAD+ ⇌ oxaloacetate + NADH + H+

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | MDH2 |
| Compartment | Mitochondrial matrix |
| Physiological direction | Regenerates oxaloacetate and forms the third canonical TCA NADH |

## What controls this step

The standard free-energy change disfavors oxidation, but citrate synthase consumption of oxaloacetate and the NAD+/NADH ratio support forward flux. MDH1 is the cytosolic isoenzyme.
