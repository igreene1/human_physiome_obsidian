# Transketolase Carbon Transfer - TKT

**Part of:** [[Pentose Phosphate Pathway]]

## Transformation

Xylulose 5-phosphate + ribose 5-phosphate ⇌ glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate + sedoheptulose 7-phosphate; a second transfer uses erythrose 4-phosphate

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | TKT; thiamine pyrophosphate and Mg2+ |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Transfers a two-carbon unit between sugar phosphates |

## What controls this step

Requires vitamin B1-derived TPP. Reversible rearrangements link the pentose phosphate pathway with fructose 6-phosphate and glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate.
