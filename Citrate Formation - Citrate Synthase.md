# Citrate Formation - Citrate Synthase

**Part of:** [[TCA Cycle]]

## Transformation

Oxaloacetate + acetyl-CoA + H2O → citrate + CoA-SH

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | CS |
| Compartment | Mitochondrial matrix |
| Physiological direction | Condenses a four-carbon acceptor with a two-carbon acetyl group |

## What controls this step

Availability of oxaloacetate and acetyl-CoA controls entry. The cycle regenerates oxaloacetate; net withdrawal for biosynthesis requires anaplerosis.
