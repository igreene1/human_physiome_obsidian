# Isocitrate Oxidative Decarboxylation - IDH3

**Part of:** [[TCA Cycle]]

## Transformation

Isocitrate + NAD+ → 2-oxoglutarate + CO2 + NADH + H+

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | IDH3A, IDH3B and IDH3G; Mg2+ or Mn2+ |
| Compartment | Mitochondrial matrix |
| Physiological direction | First canonical TCA oxidative decarboxylation and NADH-forming step |

## What controls this step

ADP and Ca2+ can stimulate; high energy and NADH restrain flux. NADP-dependent IDH2 is a distinct mitochondrial reaction and is not interchangeable in cofactor accounting.
