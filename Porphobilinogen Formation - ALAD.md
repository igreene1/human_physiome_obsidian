# Porphobilinogen Formation - ALAD

**Part of:** [[Heme Synthesis and Degradation]]

## Transformation

2 5-Aminolevulinate → porphobilinogen + 2 H2O

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | ALAD; zinc-dependent enzyme |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Condenses two aminolevulinate molecules into one pyrrole |

## What controls this step

Lead inhibits this enzyme. Four porphobilinogens are required to build the tetrapyrrole skeleton of one heme molecule.
