# Citrulline Formation - OTC

**Part of:** [[Amino Acid Metabolism and Urea Cycle]]

## Transformation

Ornithine + carbamoyl phosphate → citrulline + Pi

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | OTC |
| Compartment | Mitochondrial matrix |
| Physiological direction | Combines the first nitrogen-bearing group with ornithine |

## What controls this step

Ornithine entry and citrulline export require mitochondrial transport, especially SLC25A15. Ureagenesis spans two compartments.
