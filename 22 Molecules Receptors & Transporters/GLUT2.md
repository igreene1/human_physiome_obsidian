# GLUT2

**Gene:** SLC2A2. **Class:** Facilitative hexose transporter.

GLUT2 supports gradient-dependent glucose transport, notably in hepatocytes and basolateral membranes of selected intestinal/renal epithelial cells. It does not hydrolyse ATP or obligatorily cotransport sodium; net direction follows substrate gradients and cellular metabolism.

GLUT2 is prominent in rodent beta-cell glucose-sensing diagrams. Human beta cells often depend substantially on GLUT1/GLUT3, so GLUT2 should not be treated as the defining human beta-cell entry route.
