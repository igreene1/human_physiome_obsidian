# Beta2 Adrenergic Receptor

**Class:** Gs-coupled adrenergic GPCR

## What it does

Catecholamine activation usually raises cAMP; in airway and many vascular smooth muscles the net effect is relaxation, while other tissues show context-specific metabolic responses.
