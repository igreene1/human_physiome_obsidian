# Alpha1 Adrenergic Receptor

**Class:** Gq-coupled adrenergic GPCR

## What it does

Catecholamine activation engages PLC/IP3/DAG/Ca2+ signalling and commonly promotes smooth-muscle contraction, including vasoconstriction.
