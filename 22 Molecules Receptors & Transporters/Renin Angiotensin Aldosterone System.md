# Renin Angiotensin Aldosterone System

**Role:** Coordinates renal perfusion sensing, vascular tone and sodium retention to support arterial pressure and extracellular volume.

## Mechanism chain

↓ renal perfusion / ↓ macula-densa NaCl / sympathetic β1 input → juxtaglomerular renin release → angiotensinogen → angiotensin I → ACE → angiotensin II → [[AT1 Receptor]] effects → vasoconstriction + aldosterone secretion + renal sodium-retaining actions → ↑ effective circulating volume / pressure.

## Downstream aldosterone arm

aldosterone → [[Mineralocorticoid Receptor]] in [[Collecting Duct Principal Cell]] → ↑ [[ENaC]] and [[Na-K ATPase]] activity/expression → ↑ Na+ reabsorption; K+ secretion is favoured.
