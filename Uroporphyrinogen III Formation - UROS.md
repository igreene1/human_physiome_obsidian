**Part of:** [[Heme Synthesis and Degradation]]

## Transformation

Hydroxymethylbilane → uroporphyrinogen III

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | UROS |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Cyclises the tetrapyrrole with the asymmetric arrangement required for heme |

## What controls this step

Without normal enzymatic routing, non-productive isomer formation can occur. Porphyrinogens are reduced precursors, distinct from their oxidised porphyrin forms.
