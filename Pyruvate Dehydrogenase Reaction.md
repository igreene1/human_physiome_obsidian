# Pyruvate Dehydrogenase Reaction

**Part of:** [[Pyruvate and Lactate Metabolism]]

## Transformation

Pyruvate + CoA-SH + NAD+ → acetyl-CoA + CO2 + NADH + H+

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | PDHA1/PDHB, DLAT and DLD; TPP, lipoate, FAD, NAD+ and CoA |
| Compartment | Mitochondrial matrix |
| Physiological direction | Irreversible oxidative decarboxylation linking carbohydrate to acetyl-CoA metabolism |

## What controls this step

PDK phosphorylation inhibits; PDP dephosphorylation activates. High NADH/acetyl-CoA favour inhibition; Ca2+ activates PDP in working muscle. Acetyl-CoA cannot yield net glucose through this reaction in humans.
