# Argininosuccinate Cleavage - ASL

**Part of:** [[Amino Acid Metabolism and Urea Cycle]]

## Transformation

Argininosuccinate ⇌ arginine + fumarate

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | ASL |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Separates the carbon skeleton of aspartate as fumarate while retaining its nitrogen |

## What controls this step

Fumarate can become malate and oxaloacetate, reconnecting to aspartate metabolism. Arginine also supplies nitric oxide synthesis in other contexts.
