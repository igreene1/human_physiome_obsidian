# Pain and Nociception

**Function:** Detects potentially damaging events and constructs a context-sensitive protective experience.

**Where:** Peripheral nociceptors, dorsal horn, ascending pathways and distributed brain networks.

## Mechanism

1. Thermal, mechanical or chemical stimuli recruit transducer channels at nociceptor endings.
2. A-delta and C-fibre activity releases glutamate and peptides in dorsal horn circuits.
3. Spinal processing and ascending signals interact with affective and cognitive networks.
4. Descending pathways and local inhibitory circuits can suppress or facilitate transmission.

## Control and constraints

Nociception and consciously experienced pain are related but not identical. Inflammation can lower thresholds; repeated activity can alter central gain. Pain intensity is not a direct measure of tissue damage.
