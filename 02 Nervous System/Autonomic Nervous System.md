# Autonomic Nervous System

**Function:** Controls visceral activity through sympathetic, parasympathetic and enteric pathways.

**Where:** Brainstem/hypothalamic centres, preganglionic neurons, ganglia and organ effectors.

## Mechanism

1. Visceral afferents and higher centres alter autonomic preganglionic firing.
2. Both major divisions release acetylcholine onto nicotinic receptors in autonomic ganglia.
3. Postganglionic transmitters recruit tissue-specific receptors; most sympathetic terminals release noradrenaline, whereas parasympathetic terminals release acetylcholine.
4. Local reflexes, circulating hormones and organ state modify the response.

## Control and constraints

Sympathetic sweat-gland fibres are cholinergic. The adrenal medulla receives preganglionic input. Autonomic divisions are not always simple opposites; some tissues receive mostly one division.
