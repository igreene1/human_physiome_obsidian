# Somatosensation

**Function:** Encodes touch, position, vibration, temperature and other body-surface/deep-tissue signals.

**Where:** Peripheral receptors, spinal cord, thalamus and cortex.

## Mechanism

1. Mechanical or thermal energy changes receptor-channel activity.
2. Receptor potentials alter firing in afferent fibres; receptor density and receptive fields influence spatial resolution.
3. Ascending pathways preserve and transform information about location and modality.
4. Thalamocortical and association circuits combine sensation with attention and body models.

## Control and constraints

Dorsal-column pathways and anterolateral pathways carry overlapping but different modalities. Adaptation rate, fibre type and population coding matter; a sensory receptor is not a complete percept.
