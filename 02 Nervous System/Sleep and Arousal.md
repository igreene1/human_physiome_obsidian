# Sleep and Arousal

**Function:** Coordinates alternating sleep and waking states with restorative and behavioural functions.

**Where:** Hypothalamic, brainstem, thalamocortical and basal forebrain networks.

## Mechanism

1. Circadian timing interacts with accumulating homeostatic sleep pressure.
2. Sleep-promoting preoptic neurons inhibit several arousal systems using GABA and galanin.
3. Orexin helps stabilise wake-promoting networks; reciprocal inhibition supports state transitions.
4. NREM and REM depend on partly distinct oscillatory and brainstem circuits.

## Control and constraints

Melatonin signals biological night rather than acting as the sole cause of sleep. REM includes cortical activation with largely suppressed skeletal motor output. Dreams and sleep architecture cannot be reduced to one transmitter.
