# Vision

**Function:** Transforms light into retinal signals and visual representations.

**Where:** Retina, visual pathways and occipital/association cortex.

## Mechanism

1. Photons activate visual pigment and the transducin-PDE6 cascade in rods or cones.
2. Falling cGMP closes cation channels, hyperpolarising the photoreceptor and reducing glutamate release.
3. ON and OFF bipolar pathways interpret the transmitter change differently; ganglion cells generate action potentials.
4. Optic pathways and cortical circuits process contrast, motion, form and colour.

## Control and constraints

Photoreceptors depolarise in darkness. Retinal processing already extracts features before cortex. Adaptation spans calcium-dependent feedback, pigment recovery and network changes.
