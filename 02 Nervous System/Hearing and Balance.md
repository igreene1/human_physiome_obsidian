# Hearing and Balance

**Function:** Converts acoustic and head-motion energy into neural signals.

**Where:** Cochlea and vestibular sensory epithelia.

## Mechanism

1. Sound-driven fluid motion or head acceleration bends hair bundles.
2. Tension in tip-link-associated machinery opens mechanotransduction channels; endolymph supplies a strong K+ driving force.
3. Hair-cell depolarisation opens Ca2+ channels and changes glutamate release onto afferents.
4. Auditory pathways analyse timing/frequency; vestibular circuits stabilise gaze and posture.

## Control and constraints

Inner cochlear hair cells carry most auditory output; outer hair cells amplify mechanical responses through prestin. Semicircular canals encode angular motion; otolith organs detect linear acceleration and gravity-related tilt.
