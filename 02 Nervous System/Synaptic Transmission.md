# Synaptic Transmission

**Function:** Converts presynaptic activity into a postsynaptic response.

**Where:** Chemical synapses; electrical synapses use gap junctions.

## Mechanism

1. An arriving action potential opens presynaptic voltage-gated Ca2+ channels.
2. Local Ca2+ binds release sensors and triggers SNARE-mediated vesicle fusion.
3. Transmitter binds ionotropic or metabotropic receptors on the receiving membrane.
4. Reuptake, degradation and diffusion terminate the signal; vesicle membrane is recycled.

## Control and constraints

Excitation or inhibition depends on receptor, ion reversal potential and timing, not solely transmitter name. Summation combines inputs across space and time. Release probability and receptor abundance can change with plasticity.
