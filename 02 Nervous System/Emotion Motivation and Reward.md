# Emotion Motivation and Reward

**Function:** Integrates internal needs, learned value, social context and action selection.

**Where:** Corticolimbic, hypothalamic and basal-ganglia networks.

## Mechanism

1. Sensory and interoceptive information is evaluated in context.
2. Amygdala, insula, prefrontal and hypothalamic networks influence salience and autonomic responses.
3. Dopamine signals in selected circuits can support reinforcement learning and action vigour.
4. Behaviour changes outcomes, which update future expectations and physiological state.

## Control and constraints

Emotion is a distributed computation. Dopamine is not simply pleasure; reward prediction errors, motivation and learning vary by circuit and cell population. Subjective states cannot be inferred from a single hormone measurement.
