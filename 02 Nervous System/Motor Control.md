# Motor Control

**Function:** Selects, coordinates and corrects movement using predictions and sensory feedback.

**Where:** Cortex, basal ganglia, cerebellum, brainstem, spinal cord and motor units.

## Mechanism

1. Cortical and subcortical networks represent goals and candidate actions.
2. Descending commands recruit spinal interneurons and alpha/gamma motor neurons.
3. Motor-unit recruitment and firing rate determine muscle force.
4. Proprioceptive and other sensory feedback updates spinal responses and cerebellar error correction.

## Control and constraints

Basal ganglia influence selection/vigour; the cerebellum contributes prediction, timing and adaptation. These systems interact rather than functioning as isolated serial boxes.
