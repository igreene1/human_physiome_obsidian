# Action Potentials

**Function:** Propagates regenerative electrical signals along excitable membranes.

**Where:** Axon initial segment, axons and other excitable cells.

## Mechanism

1. Sufficient depolarisation opens voltage-gated inward-current channels.
2. In a typical neuron, rapid Na+ entry causes the upstroke; sodium-channel inactivation then limits it.
3. Delayed K+ conductance repolarises the membrane and may cause after-hyperpolarisation.
4. Local current depolarises adjacent membrane; myelination concentrates regeneration at nodes of Ranvier.

## Control and constraints

Absolute refractoriness depends largely on sodium-channel inactivation; relative refractoriness also reflects residual K+ conductance. Cardiac pacemaker and smooth-muscle action potentials use different channel combinations. Pumps maintain gradients rather than executing each rapid repolarisation.
