# Neurotransmitter Systems

**Function:** Uses transmitter-specific synthesis, release, receptors and clearance to organise neural communication.

**Where:** Defined neuronal populations and their projection fields.

## Mechanism

1. Precursors are converted into transmitters by enzymes such as tyrosine hydroxylase or glutamate decarboxylase.
2. Vesicular transporters load transmitters using a proton gradient.
3. Activity-dependent release recruits receptor subtypes in specific circuits.
4. Transporters and metabolic enzymes clear the extracellular signal and recycle components.

## Control and constraints

Dopamine, serotonin and noradrenaline have multiple circuit-dependent roles; no transmitter maps one-to-one to one emotion. Peptides are often synthesised in the soma and transported in dense-core vesicles, unlike many small-molecule transmitters.
