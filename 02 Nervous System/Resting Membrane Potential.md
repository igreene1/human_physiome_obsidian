# Resting Membrane Potential

**Function:** Creates the electrical baseline from which excitable cells respond.

**Where:** Cell membrane, especially neurons and muscle.

## Mechanism

1. The Na-K ATPase maintains high intracellular K+ and high extracellular Na+.
2. Selective resting permeability, often dominated by K+, lets charge redistribute near the membrane.
3. Electrical and chemical forces oppose one another; each ion has its own equilibrium potential.
4. The resting voltage reflects the relative conductances and gradients of several ions.

## Control and constraints

Most bulk solution remains electrically neutral. The Nernst equation describes one ion; multi-ion resting potentials need a conductance or permeability model. Extracellular K+ changes can strongly alter excitability.
