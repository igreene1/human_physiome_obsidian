# OMP Decarboxylation - UMPS

**Part of:** [[Pyrimidine and Thymidylate Synthesis]]

## Transformation

Orotidine monophosphate → UMP + CO2

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | OMP-decarboxylase domain of UMPS |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Produces UMP as the common early pyrimidine nucleotide |

## What controls this step

Kinases and additional enzymes generate UDP/UTP/CTP and deoxy forms. UMP formation is not yet DNA nucleotide synthesis.
