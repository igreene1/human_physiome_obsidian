# Citrate Isomerisation - Aconitase

**Part of:** [[TCA Cycle]]

## Transformation

Citrate ⇌ cis-aconitate ⇌ isocitrate

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | ACO2; iron-sulfur cluster |
| Compartment | Mitochondrial matrix |
| Physiological direction | Repositions a hydroxyl group to enable oxidative decarboxylation |

## What controls this step

Reversible and sensitive to substrate ratios. Oxidative stress can damage the iron-sulfur centre; cytosolic ACO1 has separate iron-regulatory functions.
