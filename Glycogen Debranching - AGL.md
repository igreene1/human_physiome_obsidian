# Glycogen Debranching - AGL

**Part of:** [[Glycogen Metabolism]]

## Transformation

Short chain transfer followed by alpha-1,6-bond hydrolysis → free glucose at the branch point

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | AGL with transferase and glucosidase activities |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Resolves branch structures that stop glycogen phosphorylase |

## What controls this step

Most glycogen residues emerge as glucose 1-phosphate, whereas branch-point hydrolysis releases free glucose. Lysosomal glycogen breakdown uses a different enzyme, acid alpha-glucosidase.
