# Ketone Activation and Oxidation - OXCT1

**Part of:** [[Ketone Body Metabolism]]

## Transformation

Acetoacetate + succinyl-CoA → acetoacetyl-CoA + succinate; then thiolase → 2 acetyl-CoA

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | OXCT1 (SCOT), then ACAT1 |
| Compartment | Mitochondria of extrahepatic tissues |
| Physiological direction | Converts ketone bodies into acetyl-CoA for the TCA cycle |

## What controls this step

Liver lacks appreciable functional SCOT-based ketolysis, so it exports the ketones it produces. Mature erythrocytes cannot oxidise ketones because they lack mitochondria.
