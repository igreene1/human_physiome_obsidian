# Glycogen Phosphorolysis - Glycogen Phosphorylase

**Part of:** [[Glycogen Metabolism]]

## Transformation

Glycogen(n) + Pi → glycogen(n−1) + glucose 1-phosphate

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | PYGL, PYGM or PYGB; pyridoxal phosphate cofactor |
| Compartment | Cytosol at glycogen particles |
| Physiological direction | Removes alpha-1,4-linked glucose without direct ATP expenditure |

## What controls this step

Phosphorylase kinase activates through phosphorylation. Ca2+ links muscle contraction to mobilisation; AMP activates muscle isoforms. Glucagon acts mainly in liver, not directly on skeletal muscle glycogen.
