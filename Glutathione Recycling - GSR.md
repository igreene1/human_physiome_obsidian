# Glutathione Recycling - GSR

**Part of:** [[Reactive Oxygen Species and Antioxidant Defence]]

## Transformation

GSSG + NADPH + H+ → 2 GSH + NADP+

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | GSR; FAD cofactor |
| Compartment | Cytosol and mitochondrial pools |
| Physiological direction | Restores reduced glutathione after peroxide reduction or other oxidation |

## What controls this step

Depends on NADPH-generating pathways in the relevant compartment. Recycling is not a substitute for new synthesis when glutathione is lost through export or conjugation.
