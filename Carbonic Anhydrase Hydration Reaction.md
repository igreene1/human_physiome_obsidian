# Carbonic Anhydrase Hydration Reaction

**Part of:** [[Carbon Dioxide Transport]]

## Transformation

CO2 + H2O ⇌ HCO3− + H+

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | Carbonic anhydrase family; zinc cofactor |
| Compartment | Erythrocytes, renal epithelium and other tissue-specific compartments |
| Physiological direction | Accelerates a reversible acid-base reaction without changing its equilibrium |

## What controls this step

CO2 partial pressure, buffering and bicarbonate transport determine direction. Renal luminal and cytosolic isoenzymes participate in bicarbonate reclamation.
