# Carnitine Shuttle for Long-Chain Acyl Groups

**Part of:** [[Lipolysis and Beta Oxidation]]

## Transformation

Acyl-CoA → acylcarnitine → matrix acyl-CoA, with carnitine recycling

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | CPT1A/B, SLC25A20 and CPT2 |
| Compartment | Outer and inner mitochondrial membranes |
| Physiological direction | Transfers long-chain acyl groups across the inner membrane; acyl-CoA itself does not freely cross |

## What controls this step

Malonyl-CoA inhibits CPT1, linking fatty-acid synthesis to restraint of oxidation. This shuttle is not required in the same way for short/medium-chain fatty acids.
