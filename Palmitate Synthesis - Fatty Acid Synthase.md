# Palmitate Synthesis - Fatty Acid Synthase

**Part of:** [[Fatty Acid Synthesis and Lipogenesis]]

## Transformation

Acetyl primer + 7 malonyl units + 14 NADPH → palmitate, with decarboxylation and water formation

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | FASN; acyl carrier phosphopantetheine domain |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Repeated condensation, reduction, dehydration and reduction produce a 16-carbon chain |

## What controls this step

Requires cytosolic acetyl-CoA, ATP-dependent malonyl formation and NADPH. Insulin/SREBP1 promote lipogenic capacity; elongases/desaturases subsequently diversify products.
