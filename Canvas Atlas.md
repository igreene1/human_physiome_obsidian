# Canvas Atlas

The new maps connect selected mechanisms with labelled arrows. Open cards for full explanations. The original maps remain available below.

## New and rebuilt maps

- [[Human Body — Master Map.canvas]] — Start with a regulated variable or a body system, then move to cellular machinery and reaction detail.
- [[V4 Glucose Insulin and Cellular Uptake.canvas]] — Glucose sensing, insulin action and tissue-specific fuel handling.
- [[V4 Glycolysis Reaction Map.canvas]] — Ten principal glycolytic reactions, with redox and branch connections. The aldolase products converge through triose handling.
- [[V4 TCA and Respiratory Coupling.canvas]] — The canonical cycle with redox outputs and replenishment. Arrows show carbon progression rather than every cofactor reaction.
- [[V4 Oxygen to ATP and Work.canvas]] — Connects delivery of oxygen and fuel to ATP supply and cellular work.
- [[V4 Water ADH and Renal Concentration.canvas]] — ADH changes water permeability; medullary gradients provide the driving force.
- [[V4 Nephron Segment Transport.canvas]] — Major segment-specific transport functions, with selected endocrine controls.
- [[V4 Gene Expression and Protein Quality.canvas]] — From transcription to functional protein, with processing and quality-control branches.
- [[V4 DNA Repair Decisions.canvas]] — Different lesions recruit different repair routes; cell-cycle context also matters.
- [[V4 Innate to Adaptive Immunity.canvas]] — Recognition, antigen processing and adaptive effector selection.
- [[V4 Injury Hemostasis and Repair.canvas]] — Overlapping defence and repair processes after tissue injury.
- [[V4 Ovarian Cycle and Steroid Cooperation.canvas]] — Gonadal cell cooperation, ovulation and uterine response.
- [[V4 Steroid Reaction Branches.canvas]] — Selected steroidogenic reactions. Some connecting arrows include intermediate steps described in the notes.
- [[V4 Cell Maintenance and Regulated Death.canvas]] — Energy, folding and damage signals influence maintenance, recycling and death programmes.
- [[V4 Multiscale Routes.canvas]] — Three examples show how whole-body questions connect to anatomy, cells, signalling and reactions.
