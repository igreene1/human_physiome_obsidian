# Complex IV Oxygen Reduction

**Part of:** [[Electron Transport and Oxidative Phosphorylation]]

## Transformation

4 reduced cytochrome c + O2 + matrix protons → 4 oxidised cytochrome c + 2 H2O, plus proton pumping

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | Cytochrome c oxidase; MT-CO1, MT-CO2, MT-CO3 and nuclear subunits; haem/copper centres |
| Compartment | Inner mitochondrial membrane |
| Physiological direction | Uses oxygen as terminal electron acceptor; pumps about 2 H+ per pair of electrons in addition to chemical proton consumption |

## What controls this step

Limited by electron supply, oxygen at very low concentrations, and proton backpressure. Cyanide and carbon monoxide can inhibit; these are perturbations, not normal inputs.
