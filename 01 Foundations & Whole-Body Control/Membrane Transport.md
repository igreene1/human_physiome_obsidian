# Membrane Transport

**Function:** Moves ions and molecules while preserving cellular gradients and compartment identity.

**Where:** Plasma membranes and organelle membranes.

## Mechanism

1. Small permeant molecules diffuse down chemical gradients; ion channels conduct ions down electrochemical gradients.
2. Carriers bind substrates and change conformation; facilitated diffusion is saturable.
3. Primary pumps use energy directly, exemplified by the Na-K ATPase.
4. Secondary transport couples uphill movement to downhill ion movement; epithelial polarity allows vectorial transport across a tissue.

## Control and constraints

Transport direction depends on all coupled gradients and stoichiometry. A channel being open does not guarantee inward movement. Paracellular movement through junctions must be distinguished from transcellular routes.
