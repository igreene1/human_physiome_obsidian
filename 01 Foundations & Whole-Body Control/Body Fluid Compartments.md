# Body Fluid Compartments

**Function:** Distributes water and dissolved solutes between intracellular, interstitial and vascular spaces.

**Where:** Cell membranes and capillary-lymphatic interfaces.

## Mechanism

1. Water crosses cell membranes according to effective osmotic gradients; transporters maintain unequal solute composition.
2. Capillary hydrostatic and oncotic forces influence movement between plasma and interstitium.
3. Lymphatics return filtered fluid and protein to venous circulation.
4. Kidneys, thirst and vasopressin regulate net water balance; renal sodium handling strongly influences extracellular volume.

## Control and constraints

Osmolality counts particles per mass of water; tonicity concerns effective osmoles that change cell volume. Total-body sodium content and plasma sodium concentration are different quantities. Urea can contribute to osmolality without equivalent sustained tonicity across many membranes.
