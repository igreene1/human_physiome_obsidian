# Intercellular Communication

**Function:** Coordinates cells through chemical, electrical and mechanical signals.

**Where:** Synapses, blood, interstitial fluid, cell contacts and extracellular matrix.

## Mechanism

1. A cell releases a signal, displays a membrane ligand, or transmits current through a gap junction.
2. A receiving cell detects the signal through a compatible receptor or junction.
3. Intracellular signalling changes channels, metabolism, secretion, gene expression or mechanics.
4. Signal clearance, receptor desensitisation and feedback limit duration.

## Control and constraints

Endocrine, paracrine, autocrine, synaptic and contact-dependent routes overlap. The same ligand can produce different effects because tissues express different receptors, scaffolds and downstream enzymes.
