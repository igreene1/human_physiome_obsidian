# Biological Rhythms and Circadian Timing

**Function:** Aligns physiology with daily environmental cycles while maintaining endogenous rhythmicity.

**Where:** Suprachiasmatic nucleus and clocks in peripheral tissues.

## Mechanism

1. Retinal melanopsin pathways convey light timing to the suprachiasmatic nucleus.
2. CLOCK/BMAL1 activate clock-gene transcription; PER/CRY proteins accumulate and later repress this activity.
3. Protein degradation releases repression, permitting a new cycle.
4. Neural, hormonal, feeding and temperature signals coordinate peripheral clocks and daily outputs.

## Control and constraints

The molecular clock is a transcriptional feedback network with delays, not a simple melatonin switch. Light has timing-dependent phase effects. Sleep pressure accumulates through mechanisms partly distinct from the circadian clock.
