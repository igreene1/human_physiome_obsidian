# Stress and Allostatic Responses

**Function:** Adjusts resource allocation to physical or perceived demands.

**Where:** Corticolimbic networks, hypothalamus, autonomic pathways and endocrine organs.

## Mechanism

1. Threat, inflammation or metabolic demand is assessed by distributed brain and body sensors.
2. Sympathetic outflow rapidly changes cardiac output, blood distribution and fuel mobilisation.
3. CRH and ACTH recruit a slower glucocorticoid response.
4. Feedback, recovery behaviour and removal of the stressor restore a different or prior operating state.

## Control and constraints

Acute responses can be useful; persistent activation can impose costs. Cortisol secretion is pulsatile and circadian. A symptom such as tachycardia can arise from several mechanisms, so this map does not diagnose its cause.
