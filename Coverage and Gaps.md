## What is now connected

Major nervous, endocrine, cardiovascular, respiratory, renal, digestive, metabolic, immune, blood, musculoskeletal, skin, reproductive, developmental and cellular functions have populated mechanism notes. Linked branches include glucose/insulin, oxygen/ATP, water/ADH, renal acid handling, hemostasis/repair, DNA/RNA/protein processing and selected reproductive/sensory mechanisms.

## Remaining gaps

| Area | What still needs deeper work |
|---|---|
| Biochemical completeness | Many carbohydrate, amino-acid, lipid, glycan, cofactor and nucleotide branches still lack every reaction/intermediate; no complete reaction database is imported. |
| Protein and gene detail | Full isoforms, splice variants, modification sites, complexes, binding constants and gene-regulatory targets are incomplete. |
| Anatomy and cell atlas | Regional histology, microvascular units, immune subtypes, connective tissue variants and cell-state transitions remain selective. |
| Development and life stages | Organ-specific embryology, fetal/neonatal transitions, puberty, pregnancy and aging need more tissue-specific depth. |
| Quantitative physiology | Concentration ranges, kinetics, flux equations, parameters and computational models are not systematically populated. |
| Neural mechanisms | Circuit detail, cognition and many plasticity mechanisms remain summaries; unresolved science is not converted into certainty. |
| Host-microbe chemistry | Only selected interactions are mapped; microbial reactions are not mislabelled as human-cell reactions. |
| Disease and drugs | Normal physiology is primary; systematic perturbation maps and treatment information are not provided. |
| Evidence audit | Retained v3 summaries and new synthesis need finer claim-level sourcing, external expert review and systematic human-versus-model checks. |
| Atomic/structural scale | Protein structures, individual catalytic intermediates and atomistic simulations are not an exhaustive layer here. |

Use [[Expansion Roadmap]] to deepen one branch at a time and [[Evidence and Scope]] to interpret the depth labels. Structural validity does not establish biological completeness.
