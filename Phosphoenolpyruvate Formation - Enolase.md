# Phosphoenolpyruvate Formation - Enolase

**Part of:** [[Glycolysis]]

## Transformation

2-Phosphoglycerate ⇌ phosphoenolpyruvate + H2O

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | ENO1, ENO2 or ENO3; Mg2+ |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Dehydration creates a high phosphoryl-transfer-potential intermediate |

## What controls this step

Flux follows substrate supply and downstream pyruvate kinase activity; metal availability affects catalysis.
