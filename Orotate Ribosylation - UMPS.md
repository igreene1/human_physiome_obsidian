# Orotate Ribosylation - UMPS

**Part of:** [[Pyrimidine and Thymidylate Synthesis]]

## Transformation

Orotate + PRPP → orotidine monophosphate + PPi

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | Orotate-phosphoribosyltransferase domain of UMPS |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Attaches the completed ring to activated ribose phosphate |

## What controls this step

PPi hydrolysis supports forward drive. UMPS contains two consecutive activities, but the chemical transformations remain distinct.
