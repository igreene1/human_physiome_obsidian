# Beta-Hydroxybutyrate Interconversion - BDH1

**Part of:** [[Ketone Body Metabolism]]

## Transformation

Acetoacetate + NADH + H+ ⇌ D-beta-hydroxybutyrate + NAD+

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | BDH1 |
| Compartment | Inner mitochondrial membrane, matrix-facing |
| Physiological direction | Interconverts circulating ketone forms; beta-hydroxybutyrate is chemically a hydroxy acid |

## What controls this step

The mitochondrial NADH/NAD+ ratio influences the beta-hydroxybutyrate/acetoacetate ratio. Extrahepatic tissues oxidise beta-hydroxybutyrate back to acetoacetate during utilisation.
