# GABA

**Role:** Principal inhibitory neurotransmitter in the adult brain.

## Production, release and clearance

Synthesised from glutamate by glutamate decarboxylase; vesicular release is followed by neuronal/glial uptake and metabolism.

## Receptor logic

GABA-A receptors are ligand-gated chloride channels; GABA-B receptors are Gi/o-coupled GPCRs.
