# Nitric Oxide

**Role:** Short-lived gaseous signalling molecule that diffuses locally rather than being stored in conventional vesicles.

## Production, release and clearance

Nitric oxide synthases convert arginine to NO; endothelial eNOS activation is a major vascular source.

## Receptor logic

NO activates soluble guanylyl cyclase → cGMP → PKG, causing smooth-muscle relaxation among other effects.
