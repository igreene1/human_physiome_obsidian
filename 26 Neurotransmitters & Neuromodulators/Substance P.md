# Substance P

**Role:** Tachykinin neuropeptide involved in nociception, neurogenic inflammation and autonomic/GI signalling.

## Production, release and clearance

Released from subsets of sensory and central neurons, often alongside glutamate or CGRP.

## Receptor logic

Acts mainly at neurokinin-1 receptors to produce slower modulatory effects.
