# Acetylcholine

**Role:** Classical neurotransmitter used at all autonomic preganglionic synapses, most parasympathetic postganglionic synapses, sympathetic sweat-gland synapses and the neuromuscular junction.

## Production, release and clearance

Choline acetyltransferase synthesises ACh from choline + acetyl-CoA; vesicular release follows Ca²⁺ entry; acetylcholinesterase rapidly hydrolyses it.

## Receptor logic

Nicotinic receptors are ligand-gated cation channels; muscarinic receptors are GPCRs.
