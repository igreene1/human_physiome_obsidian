# Glutamate

**Role:** Principal fast excitatory neurotransmitter in the CNS.

## Production, release and clearance

Packed into synaptic vesicles by VGLUT transporters; released Ca²⁺-dependently; cleared largely by excitatory amino-acid transporters in neurons and astrocytes and recycled through the glutamate–glutamine cycle.

## Receptor logic

Activates AMPA/kainate and NMDA ionotropic receptors plus metabotropic glutamate receptors.
