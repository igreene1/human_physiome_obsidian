# Noradrenaline

**Role:** Primary transmitter released by most sympathetic postganglionic neurons and a major central neuromodulator from the locus coeruleus.

## Production, release and clearance

Synthesised from tyrosine → L-DOPA → dopamine → noradrenaline; stored in vesicles, released Ca²⁺-dependently, cleared mainly by neuronal reuptake via NET plus metabolism by MAO/COMT.

## Receptor logic

Acts on α1, α2 and β adrenergic receptors with tissue-dependent effects.
