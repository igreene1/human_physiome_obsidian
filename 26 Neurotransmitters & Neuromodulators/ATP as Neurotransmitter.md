# ATP as Neurotransmitter

**Role:** ATP can act as a co-transmitter in autonomic and sensory systems in addition to its intracellular energy role.

## Production, release and clearance

Released from vesicles or cells and rapidly converted extracellularly to ADP, AMP and adenosine.

## Receptor logic

Acts on P2X ion channels and P2Y GPCRs; downstream metabolism generates adenosine signalling.
