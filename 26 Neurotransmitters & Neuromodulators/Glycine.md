# Glycine

**Role:** Major inhibitory transmitter in spinal cord and brainstem; also an obligatory co-agonist at NMDA receptors.

## Production, release and clearance

Released from inhibitory interneurons and cleared by glycine transporters.

## Receptor logic

Postsynaptic glycine receptors are ligand-gated chloride channels.
