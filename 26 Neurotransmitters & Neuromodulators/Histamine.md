# Histamine

**Role:** Central neuromodulator produced mainly by tuberomammillary hypothalamic neurons, strongly associated with wakefulness and arousal.

## Production, release and clearance

Histaminergic neurons fire most during wakefulness and reduce activity during sleep.

## Receptor logic

H1 receptors promote cortical arousal; H3 receptors act importantly as inhibitory autoreceptors/heteroreceptors.
