# Orexin

**Role:** Hypothalamic neuropeptides (orexin-A and orexin-B/hypocretins) that stabilise wakefulness and link arousal to motivation and energy state.

## Production, release and clearance

Produced by neurons in the lateral hypothalamic area with widespread projections.

## Receptor logic

Activates OX1 and OX2 GPCRs in arousal, autonomic and reward networks.
