# Endogenous Opioids

**Role:** Peptides including endorphins, enkephalins and dynorphins that modulate pain, stress, reward and autonomic/endocrine responses.

## Production, release and clearance

Derived from precursor proteins such as POMC, proenkephalin and prodynorphin.

## Receptor logic

Act at μ, δ and κ opioid GPCRs, generally reducing neuronal excitability and transmitter release.
