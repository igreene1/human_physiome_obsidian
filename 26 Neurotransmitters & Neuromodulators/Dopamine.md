# Dopamine

**Role:** Catecholamine neurotransmitter with major roles in movement, reward, motivation, cognition and endocrine regulation.

## Production, release and clearance

Synthesised from tyrosine via L-DOPA; released from distinct pathways including nigrostriatal, mesolimbic/mesocortical and tuberoinfundibular neurons.

## Receptor logic

D1-like receptors generally couple to Gs/olf; D2-like receptors generally couple to Gi/o. Hypothalamic dopamine tonically inhibits prolactin secretion.
