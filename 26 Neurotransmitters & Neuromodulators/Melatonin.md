# Melatonin

**Role:** Pineal hormone that conveys information about biological night rather than simply acting as a general sedative.

## Production, release and clearance

Synthesised from serotonin in the pineal gland; secretion is promoted at night by SCN-controlled sympathetic input.

## Receptor logic

Acts mainly at MT1/MT2 receptors to influence circadian timing and sleep propensity.
