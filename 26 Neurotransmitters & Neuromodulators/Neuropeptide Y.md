# Neuropeptide Y

**Role:** Widely distributed peptide neuromodulator involved in appetite, stress, autonomic tone and vascular regulation.

## Production, release and clearance

Produced in hypothalamic NPY/AgRP neurons and many sympathetic neurons.

## Receptor logic

Acts through Y-family GPCRs; in the hypothalamus it strongly promotes feeding.
