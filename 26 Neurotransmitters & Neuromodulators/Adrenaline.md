# Adrenaline

**Role:** Circulating catecholamine secreted mainly by adrenal chromaffin cells during sympathetic activation.

## Production, release and clearance

Adrenal medullary PNMT converts noradrenaline to adrenaline; secretion is driven by preganglionic sympathetic acetylcholine acting on nicotinic receptors.

## Receptor logic

Acts on α and β adrenergic receptors; increases cardiac performance, redistributes blood flow, supports bronchodilation and mobilises metabolic fuel.
