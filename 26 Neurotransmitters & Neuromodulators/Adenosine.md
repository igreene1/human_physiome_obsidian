# Adenosine

**Role:** Purine neuromodulator whose extracellular concentration tends to rise with cellular activity and prolonged wakefulness.

## Production, release and clearance

Generated from ATP/AMP metabolism and released/transported across membranes; levels reflect energy use and extracellular nucleotide breakdown.

## Receptor logic

A1 signalling is generally inhibitory; A2A signalling can oppose A1 effects in selected circuits.
