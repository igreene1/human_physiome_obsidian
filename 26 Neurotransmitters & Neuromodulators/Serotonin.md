# Serotonin

**Role:** Monoamine transmitter involved in mood, sleep, pain, nausea, vascular/GI function and many other processes.

## Production, release and clearance

Synthesised from tryptophan → 5-hydroxytryptophan → serotonin; central neurons are concentrated in raphe nuclei, while most body serotonin is in the gut/platelets.

## Receptor logic

Acts through many GPCR families plus the ionotropic 5-HT3 receptor.
