# CGRP

**Role:** Calcitonin gene-related peptide is a potent sensory neuropeptide and vasodilator.

## Production, release and clearance

Released from subsets of primary sensory neurons, frequently with substance P.

## Receptor logic

Acts on CGRP receptor complexes to dilate vessels and participate in nociceptive signalling.
