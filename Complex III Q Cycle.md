# Complex III Q Cycle

**Part of:** [[Electron Transport and Oxidative Phosphorylation]]

## Transformation

Ubiquinol electrons → cytochrome c, coupled to Q-cycle proton translocation

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | Cytochrome bc1 complex; MT-CYB, UQCRFS1, CYC1 and other subunits |
| Compartment | Inner mitochondrial membrane |
| Physiological direction | Transfers two electrons to two cytochrome c molecules and contributes about 4 H+ to the intermembrane space per electron pair |

## What controls this step

Depends on ubiquinone/cytochrome c redox states. Semiquinone intermediates can transfer electrons to oxygen prematurely.
