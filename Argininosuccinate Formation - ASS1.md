# Argininosuccinate Formation - ASS1

**Part of:** [[Amino Acid Metabolism and Urea Cycle]]

## Transformation

Citrulline + aspartate + ATP → argininosuccinate + AMP + PPi

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | ASS1 |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | Adds the second urea nitrogen from aspartate; costs two high-energy phosphate equivalents |

## What controls this step

Substrate supply and PPi hydrolysis favour flux. Aspartate links nitrogen disposal to transamination and the TCA-related carbon network.
