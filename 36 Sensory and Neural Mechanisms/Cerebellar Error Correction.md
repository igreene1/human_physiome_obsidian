# Cerebellar Error Correction

**Function:** Supports prediction, timing and adaptation of sensorimotor control.

**Where:** Cerebellar cortex, deep nuclei and connected brainstem/cortical loops.

## Mechanism

1. Mossy-fibre inputs convey context and movement-related information through granule cells.
2. Climbing fibres provide powerful teaching-related signals to Purkinje cells.
3. Purkinje-cell output shapes deep-nuclear activity.
4. Plasticity at several sites updates future responses and predictions.

## Control and constraints

Climbing-fibre activity is not always a simple scalar error signal. Cerebellar roles extend beyond movement; this note maps the core sensorimotor interpretation without equating it to all cerebellar computation.
