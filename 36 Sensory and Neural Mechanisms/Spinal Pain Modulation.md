# Spinal Pain Modulation

**Function:** Adjusts nociceptive transmission before and during ascending processing.

**Where:** Dorsal-horn excitatory/inhibitory interneurons and descending terminals.

## Mechanism

1. Nociceptor glutamate/peptides recruit postsynaptic receptors.
2. GABA/glycine interneurons and presynaptic inhibition restrain transmission.
3. Descending noradrenergic/serotonergic/opioid-related circuits can inhibit or facilitate depending on receptor and circuit.
4. Activity-dependent receptor and network changes alter gain over time.

## Control and constraints

Descending control is not uniformly inhibitory. NMDA-related plasticity is one contributor to central sensitisation, but the full human experience of pain is distributed beyond spinal circuits.
