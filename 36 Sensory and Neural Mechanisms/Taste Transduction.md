# Taste Transduction

**Function:** Detects nutrient-related and potentially harmful oral chemical qualities.

**Where:** Taste-bud receptor cells and cranial afferents.

## Mechanism

1. Sweet, bitter and umami receptors use GPCR-associated PLCbeta2/IP3/Ca2+ signalling in relevant cell types.
2. TRPM5 and associated conductances support depolarisation; ATP release recruits afferent purinergic receptors.
3. Sour sensing prominently involves OTOP1 proton conductance in a different cell type.
4. Salt sensing uses multiple mechanisms, with human details more complex than a universal ENaC-only model.

## Control and constraints

Taste-bud cells are renewed epithelial sensory cells. Taste combines with smell, temperature and texture to form flavour; those are distinct input streams.
