# Blood-Brain Barrier Transport

**Function:** Regulates exchange between blood and brain extracellular fluid.

**Where:** Brain endothelium with pericyte/astrocyte-associated support.

## Mechanism

1. Specialised endothelial junctions restrict many paracellular routes.
2. Selective transporters supply glucose, amino acids and other needed substrates.
3. Efflux transporters limit entry/retention of selected compounds.
4. Transcytosis and immune trafficking are tightly regulated and can change with injury.

## Control and constraints

The barrier is primarily endothelial, not a wall formed solely by astrocytes. Lipid solubility alone does not determine entry because transport, binding and metabolism also matter.
