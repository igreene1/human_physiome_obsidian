# Neurovascular Coupling

**Function:** Adjusts local blood supply with neural activity and tissue state.

**Where:** Neurons, astrocytes, vascular smooth muscle, pericytes and endothelium.

## Mechanism

1. Neural activity changes transmitter release, ions and metabolic demand.
2. Signals involving NO, potassium and multiple other mediators affect nearby vessels.
3. Arteriolar/capillary network responses alter local perfusion.
4. Oxygen/glucose delivery and waste transport adapt with delays relative to neural events.

## Control and constraints

Mechanisms vary by region, vessel level and state. BOLD imaging is an indirect haemodynamic signal; it is not a direct neuron-count or firing-rate measurement.
