# Synaptic Vesicle Cycle

**Function:** Reuses vesicles and maintains transmitter release capacity.

**Where:** Presynaptic terminals.

## Mechanism

1. V-ATPase builds a proton electrochemical gradient for vesicular transmitter transporters.
2. Vesicles dock and prime near release sites through Rab/Munc/SNARE-associated machinery.
3. Local Ca2+ influx triggers fusion of release-ready vesicles.
4. Endocytosis, sorting, reacidification and refilling replenish pools.

## Control and constraints

Different synapses use multiple recycling modes depending on activity. Vesicle number, release probability and calcium coupling jointly shape synaptic strength.
