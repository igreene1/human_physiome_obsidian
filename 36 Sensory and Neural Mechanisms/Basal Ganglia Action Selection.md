# Basal Ganglia Action Selection

**Function:** Influences action selection, vigour and learning through recurrent loops.

**Where:** Striatum, pallidum, substantia nigra, subthalamic nucleus and thalamocortical circuits.

## Mechanism

1. Cortical/thalamic inputs recruit striatal populations.
2. Direct, indirect and hyperdirect pathways reshape output-nucleus inhibition.
3. Thalamic/brainstem targets alter the probability and execution of actions.
4. Dopamine and activity-dependent plasticity update action-related value and control.

## Control and constraints

The classic direct-go/indirect-no-go model is an introductory simplification; pathways can coactivate during movement. Cell types, subregions and temporal patterns matter.
