# Myelination and Saltatory Conduction

**Function:** Speeds and supports axonal signalling through segmented electrical insulation.

**Where:** Oligodendrocytes in CNS and Schwann cells in PNS.

## Mechanism

1. Glial membranes wrap axons to form compact myelin segments.
2. Nodes/paranodes organise channel distribution and electrical boundaries.
3. Reduced effective capacitance and increased membrane resistance permit current to spread farther.
4. Action potentials regenerate at nodes rather than across every point of internodal membrane.

## Control and constraints

Myelin also participates in metabolic support. One oligodendrocyte can support multiple internodes, whereas a myelinating Schwann cell forms one segment. Demyelination affects timing and reliability, not only speed.
