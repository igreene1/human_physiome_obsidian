# Phototransduction

**Function:** Converts photons into changes in rod/cone membrane potential.

**Where:** Photoreceptor outer-segment discs and plasma membrane.

## Mechanism

1. Light isomerises the retinal chromophore and activates opsin.
2. Transducin activates PDE6, increasing cGMP hydrolysis.
3. Lower cGMP closes cyclic-nucleotide-gated channels, reducing dark inward current.
4. The cell hyperpolarises and decreases glutamate release; recovery restores cGMP and pigment readiness.

## Control and constraints

Light hyperpolarises photoreceptors, unlike depolarising many other sensory receptors. Calcium-dependent feedback, opsin phosphorylation and arrestin support adaptation and shutoff.
