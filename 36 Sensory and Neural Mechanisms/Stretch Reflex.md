# Stretch Reflex

**Function:** Resists sudden muscle lengthening and supports posture.

**Where:** Muscle spindle afferents, spinal circuits and motor neurons.

## Mechanism

1. Stretch deforms spindle sensory endings and increases selected afferent firing.
2. Ia afferents excite homonymous alpha motor neurons through a short spinal route.
3. Reciprocal inhibitory interneurons reduce antagonist drive in the canonical circuit.
4. Gamma motor neurons adjust spindle sensitivity during changing muscle state.

## Control and constraints

A spindle senses length-related dynamics, whereas Golgi tendon organs predominantly report tension-related information. Descending control adjusts reflex gain; it is not a fixed isolated wire.
