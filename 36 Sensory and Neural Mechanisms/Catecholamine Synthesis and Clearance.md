# Catecholamine Synthesis and Clearance

**Function:** Produces and terminates dopamine, noradrenaline and adrenaline signals.

**Where:** Catecholaminergic neurons and adrenal chromaffin cells.

## Mechanism

1. Tyrosine hydroxylase forms L-DOPA using BH4 and oxygen; aromatic L-amino-acid decarboxylase forms dopamine using PLP.
2. VMAT loads vesicles, where dopamine beta-hydroxylase can form noradrenaline in appropriate cells.
3. PNMT converts noradrenaline to adrenaline in cells expressing that enzyme.
4. Transporters retrieve released transmitters while MAO/COMT-associated routes metabolise them.

## Control and constraints

Not every catecholaminergic cell expresses the whole enzyme sequence. Tyrosine hydroxylase is a major regulated step; transporter activity strongly shapes synaptic duration.
