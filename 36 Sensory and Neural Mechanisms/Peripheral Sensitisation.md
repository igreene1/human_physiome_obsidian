# Peripheral Sensitisation

**Function:** Lowers nociceptor activation thresholds during local inflammation or injury.

**Where:** Peripheral sensory endings and nearby inflammatory/tissue cells.

## Mechanism

1. Prostaglandins, bradykinin, cytokines and growth factors bind receptors on or near nociceptors.
2. Kinase pathways alter channels such as TRP and voltage-gated sodium channels.
3. Lower thresholds or greater firing amplify responses to stimuli.
4. Longer-lasting transcriptional changes can extend altered sensitivity.

## Control and constraints

This can support protection while tissue heals but can persist in some conditions. It is distinct from central sensitisation and does not imply every pain experience has ongoing peripheral tissue damage.
