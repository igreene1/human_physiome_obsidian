# Vestibular Transduction

**Function:** Encodes head motion and orientation for gaze and posture.

**Where:** Semicircular canal cristae and utricular/saccular maculae.

## Mechanism

1. Angular acceleration deflects canal cupulae through endolymph mechanics.
2. Linear acceleration/gravity shift otoconial membranes in otolith organs.
3. Hair-bundle deflection changes mechanotransduction and transmitter release.
4. Push-pull afferent patterns feed vestibulo-ocular and postural circuits.

## Control and constraints

Otolith organs encode gravito-inertial acceleration, so the brain must infer tilt versus translation using additional signals. Canal and otolith information complement vision and proprioception.
