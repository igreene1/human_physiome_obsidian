# Mechanotransduction and PIEZO Channels

**Function:** Converts physical deformation into ionic current.

**Where:** Mechanosensory endings and multiple non-neural cell membranes.

## Mechanism

1. Force alters membrane/cytoskeletal conditions around mechanically sensitive channels.
2. PIEZO channels change open probability, permitting cation entry.
3. Receptor potentials or intracellular calcium signals recruit cell-specific responses.
4. Adaptation and mechanical relaxation reshape the signal.

## Control and constraints

PIEZO2 is central to many touch/proprioceptive functions; PIEZO1 has prominent vascular and other roles. Channel gating alone does not specify the final percept or physiological response.
