# Glutamate Glutamine Cycle

**Function:** Recovers transmitter glutamate and coordinates neuron-astrocyte nitrogen/carbon exchange.

**Where:** Synaptic cleft, astrocytes and neurons.

## Mechanism

1. EAAT transporters remove extracellular glutamate, using ion gradients ultimately supported by ATP.
2. Astrocytic glutamine synthetase converts glutamate plus ammonia to glutamine.
3. Transporters exchange glutamine between astrocytes and neurons.
4. Neuronal glutaminase regenerates glutamate for metabolism or vesicular loading; GABAergic cells can convert it further.

## Control and constraints

Glutamate also participates in general metabolism, so this cycle is not isolated from TCA flux. Astrocytes help limit excitotoxic exposure, but regional and activity-dependent details vary.
