# NMDA-Dependent Synaptic Plasticity

**Function:** Links selected patterns of coincident activity to lasting synaptic changes.

**Where:** Glutamatergic synapses, with strong regional/cell-type variation.

## Mechanism

1. Glutamate binding plus sufficient depolarisation relieves NMDA-receptor magnesium block when a co-agonist is available.
2. Calcium entry recruits kinases or phosphatases according to signal pattern.
3. AMPA-receptor trafficking and local structural changes alter synaptic efficacy.
4. Longer-lasting forms recruit gene expression and protein synthesis.

## Control and constraints

The calcium-to-LTP/LTD relationship is a useful motif, not a universal rule for every synapse. NMDA receptors are not required for all forms of learning or plasticity.
