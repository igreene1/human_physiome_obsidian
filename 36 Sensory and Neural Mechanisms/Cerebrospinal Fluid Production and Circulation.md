# Cerebrospinal Fluid Production and Circulation

**Function:** Maintains a fluid environment around the CNS and exchanges solutes with surrounding tissues.

**Where:** Choroid plexus, ventricles, subarachnoid spaces and outflow pathways.

## Mechanism

1. Choroid-plexus epithelium uses coordinated ion transport and water movement to secrete CSF.
2. Ventricular/subarachnoid pathways distribute fluid under pressure and pulsatile influences.
3. Exchange occurs with interstitial compartments.
4. Arachnoid, perineural and meningeal lymphatic-associated routes contribute to drainage.

## Control and constraints

Relative contributions of drainage/exchange routes vary and remain actively studied. The choroid-plexus blood-CSF barrier is distinct from the endothelial blood-brain barrier.
