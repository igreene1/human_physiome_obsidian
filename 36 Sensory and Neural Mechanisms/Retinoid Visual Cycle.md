# Retinoid Visual Cycle

**Function:** Regenerates chromophore after photopigment activation.

**Where:** Photoreceptors and retinal pigment epithelium, with additional cone-associated routes.

## Mechanism

1. Activated pigment releases all-trans retinal, which is reduced and transported in the retinoid network.
2. RPE uptake/esterification and RPE65-associated isomerisation produce an 11-cis precursor.
3. Oxidation and transport return 11-cis retinal to photoreceptors.
4. Chromophore recombines with opsin to restore light-sensitive pigment.

## Control and constraints

Vitamin A chemistry is necessary but must be tightly handled because reactive retinoids can be harmful. Cone chromophore regeneration includes additional support routes beyond the standard rod-RPE diagram.
