# Olfactory Transduction

**Function:** Converts volatile chemical binding into olfactory nerve activity.

**Where:** Olfactory receptor-neuron cilia and olfactory bulb circuits.

## Mechanism

1. Odorant receptors activate Golf-associated signalling.
2. Adenylyl cyclase raises cAMP and opens cyclic-nucleotide-gated channels.
3. Calcium-activated chloride current can amplify depolarisation because of the specialised intracellular chloride gradient.
4. Axonal firing carries combinatorial receptor information to glomerular networks.

## Control and constraints

One receptor can respond to multiple ligands and one odorant can activate multiple receptor types. Smell perception depends on population patterns, adaptation and context.
