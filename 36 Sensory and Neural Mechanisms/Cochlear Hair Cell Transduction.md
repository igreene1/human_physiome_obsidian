# Cochlear Hair Cell Transduction

**Function:** Converts basilar-membrane motion into auditory afferent activity.

**Where:** Organ of Corti, endolymph and auditory nerve endings.

## Mechanism

1. Frequency-dependent cochlear mechanics deflect hair bundles.
2. Tip-link-associated mechanotransduction machinery opens channels.
3. The endolymphatic electrochemical gradient drives predominantly K+ entry and depolarisation.
4. Basolateral calcium entry triggers ribbon-synapse glutamate release; K+ leaves toward lower-K+ surroundings.

## Control and constraints

Outer hair-cell prestin supports amplification; inner hair cells supply most auditory nerve output. Hair cells signal with graded receptor potentials rather than typical axonal action potentials.
