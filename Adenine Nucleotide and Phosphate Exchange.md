# Adenine Nucleotide and Phosphate Exchange

**Part of:** [[Electron Transport and Oxidative Phosphorylation]]

## Transformation

Matrix ATP exchanged for cytosolic ADP; phosphate imported with proton coupling

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | SLC25A4/A5 and related adenine nucleotide carriers; SLC25A3 phosphate carrier |
| Compartment | Inner mitochondrial membrane |
| Physiological direction | Exports ATP for cellular work while replenishing substrates for ATP synthesis |

## What controls this step

The ATP4−/ADP3− exchange depends on membrane potential. ATP yield per substrate is not determined by ATP synthase alone.
