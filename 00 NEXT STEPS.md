# 00 NEXT STEPS

The material from the older expansion packs is already integrated here. No separate pack installation is needed.

1. Open [[00 START HERE]].
2. Choose a body system in [[Human Body — Master Map.canvas]].
3. Move through a process to its cell/signalling/reaction children.
4. Use [[Molecular Reaction Index]] for enzyme-level navigation and [[Cell Type Index]] to see cellular executors.
5. Use [[Canvas Atlas]] for the new visual maps, or [[Cross-Scale Routes]] for guided routes through the whole hierarchy.

The shortest starting routes are glucose/insulin, oxygen/ATP, water/ADH and tissue injury/repair. [[Coverage and Gaps]] identifies the remaining depth limitations.
