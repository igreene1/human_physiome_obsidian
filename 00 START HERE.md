## How to read it

Start with a question such as “How does the body conserve water?” Open [[Water and Osmolality Homeostasis]], follow the ADH/V2/cAMP/AQP2 route, and then inspect [[Countercurrent Multiplication and Exchange]] to understand what powers the water movement. For metabolism, open [[Glycolysis]] and its individual reaction notes.

Every detailed process has a location, mechanism, regulation/constraints and related links. Reaction notes identify machinery and compartment; cell notes show which processes a cell performs. Arrows on maps have labels because containment, transport, catalysis and feedback are different relationships.

## What this edition contains

The v3 body-system outlines have been replaced with fuller mechanisms; all previous topics are retained. The expansion adds molecular cell biology, immune mechanisms, organ transport, sensory/reproductive mechanisms, cell types and reaction-level metabolism. It also repairs damaged filenames and escaped-newline formatting from the uploaded archive.

[[Coverage and Gaps]] shows current counts and remaining depth limits. [[Evidence and Scope]] explains what is and is not source-verified. [[VALIDATION V4]] reports structural checks. This is a substantial, expandable educational map; it is not literally every human process at every molecular scale.

Extract the ZIP fully, then use Obsidian's **Open folder as vault** option and select the `Human_Physiome_Obsidian_Vault_v4` folder. Open this note. Canvas and ordinary internal links work without community plugins; external references need an internet connection.
