# Superoxide Dismutation

**Part of:** [[Reactive Oxygen Species and Antioxidant Defence]]

## Transformation

2 Superoxide + 2 H+ → H2O2 + O2

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | SOD1, SOD2 or SOD3; metal cofactors |
| Compartment | Cytosol/intermembrane space, matrix, or extracellular space according to isoenzyme |
| Physiological direction | Converts superoxide into hydrogen peroxide |

## What controls this step

Hydrogen peroxide still requires removal by catalase, peroxiredoxins or glutathione peroxidases. Antioxidant enzymes form a network rather than a single detoxification step.
