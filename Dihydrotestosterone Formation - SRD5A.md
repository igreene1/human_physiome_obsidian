# Dihydrotestosterone Formation - SRD5A

**Part of:** [[Testosterone]]

## Transformation

Testosterone + NADPH → dihydrotestosterone + NADP+

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | SRD5A1 or SRD5A2 |
| Compartment | Membrane-associated enzyme systems in target tissues |
| Physiological direction | Amplifies selected local androgen actions by reducing a double bond |

## What controls this step

DHT has different receptor potency and cannot be directly aromatised to estrogen. Tissue enzyme expression means serum testosterone alone does not specify local androgen signalling.
