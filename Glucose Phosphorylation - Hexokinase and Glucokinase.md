# Glucose Phosphorylation - Hexokinase and Glucokinase

**Part of:** [[Glycolysis]]

## Transformation

Glucose + ATP → glucose 6-phosphate + ADP

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | HK1, HK2, HK3 or GCK; Mg2+-ATP |
| Compartment | Cytosol; some hexokinase associates with the mitochondrial outer membrane |
| Physiological direction | Consumes ATP and traps glucose as a phosphorylated sugar; strongly forward under ordinary cellular conditions |

## What controls this step

HK1-3 are generally inhibited by glucose 6-phosphate. GCK has lower glucose affinity and is not inhibited by this product; hepatic GCK localisation is regulated by GCKR. This is distinct from glucose transport.
