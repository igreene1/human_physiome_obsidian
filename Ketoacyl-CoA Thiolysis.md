# Ketoacyl-CoA Thiolysis

**Part of:** [[Lipolysis and Beta Oxidation]]

## Transformation

3-Ketoacyl-CoA + CoA-SH → acetyl-CoA + acyl-CoA shortened by two carbons

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | ACAA2 or HADHB thiolase domain |
| Compartment | Mitochondrial matrix / trifunctional protein |
| Physiological direction | Cleaves the beta-keto substrate; the shortened acyl-CoA re-enters the spiral |

## What controls this step

The last cleavage of an even chain yields two acetyl-CoA molecules. Odd chains finish with propionyl-CoA, which needs biotin/B12-dependent processing toward succinyl-CoA.
