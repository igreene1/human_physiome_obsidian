# Pregnenolone Formation - CYP11A1

**Part of:** [[Steroid Hormone Synthesis]]

## Transformation

Cholesterol → pregnenolone + cleaved side-chain product through sequential oxygenations

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | CYP11A1 with ferredoxin/reductase electron-transfer partners |
| Compartment | Inner mitochondrial membrane in steroidogenic cells |
| Physiological direction | Removes part of cholesterol's side chain to start steroid-hormone biosynthesis |

## What controls this step

Cholesterol delivery through StAR-associated mechanisms is a major acute control in many steroidogenic tissues. Multiple NADPH-derived reducing equivalents and oxygen are required.
