# Glutamate Cysteine Ligation

**Part of:** [[Reactive Oxygen Species and Antioxidant Defence]]

## Transformation

Glutamate + cysteine + ATP → gamma-glutamylcysteine + ADP + Pi

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | GCLC with regulatory GCLM |
| Compartment | Cytosol |
| Physiological direction | First major regulated step in glutathione synthesis |

## What controls this step

Cysteine availability and glutathione feedback affect synthesis. The unusual gamma-glutamyl bond differs from ordinary protein peptide linkage.
