# Iron Insertion - Ferrochelatase

**Part of:** [[Heme Synthesis and Degradation]]

## Transformation

Protoporphyrin IX + Fe2+ → heme + 2 H+

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Catalyst / machinery | FECH; iron-sulfur-containing enzyme |
| Compartment | Matrix-facing inner mitochondrial membrane |
| Physiological direction | Final insertion of ferrous iron into the porphyrin ring |

## What controls this step

Iron delivery, FECH function, and porphyrin supply must be coordinated. Heme then supports haemoglobin, cytochromes and other haemoproteins.
